# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

**Measures:** S. 181

```
                   AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 28. Mr. ENZI submitted an amendment intended to be 
     proposed by him to the bill S. 181, to amend title VII of the 
     Civil Rights Act of 1964 and the Age Discrimination in 
     Employment Act of 1967, and to modify the operation of the 
     Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 and the 
     Rehabilitation Act of 1973, to clarify that a discriminatory 
     compensation decision or other practice that is unlawful 
     under such Acts occurs each time compensation is paid 
     pursuant to the discriminatory compensation decision or other 
     practice, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
     the table.

     proposed by him to the bill S. 181, supra; which was ordered
```
