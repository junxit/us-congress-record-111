# COMMUNICATION FROM CONSTITUENT SERVICES DIRECTOR, THE HONORABLE CHRISTOPHER P. CARNEY, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
    COMMUNICATION FROM CONSTITUENT SERVICES DIRECTOR, THE HONORABLE 
               CHRISTOPHER P. CARNEY, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Joe Fabricatore, Constituent Services Director, the 
Honorable Christopher P. Carney, Member of Congress:

                                Congress of the United States,

                                  Washington, DC, January 9, 2009.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to nofify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I have been served with a subpoena for 
     testimony issued by the Court of Common Pleas for Wayne 
     County, Pennsylvania.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     will make the determinations required by Rule VIII.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Joe Fabricatore,
     Constituent Services Director.

                          ____________________
```
