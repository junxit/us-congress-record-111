# RECOGNIZING SARAH JOSEPHINE EVEN

```
                    RECOGNIZING SARAH JOSEPHINE EVEN

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Sarah Josephine 
Even, an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work 
she has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the 
past several weeks.
  Sarah is a graduate of T.F. Riggs High School in Pierre, SD. 
Currently she is attending South Dakota State University, where she is 
majoring in journalism and mass communication. She is a hard worker who 
has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Sarah 
for all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in 
the years to come.●
```
