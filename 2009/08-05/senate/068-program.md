# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. WARNER. Under the previous order, at 3 p.m. tomorrow, the Senate 
will proceed to vote on confirmation of the nomination of Judge Sonia 
Sotomayor to be Associate Justice of the Supreme Court of the United 
States. Upon disposition of the nomination, the Senate will return to 
the consideration of the supplemental appropriations bill for the 
Consumer Assistance to Recycle and Save Program.
  Under the agreement, up to seven amendments are in order prior to a 
vote on the passage of the bill. When we return from the August recess, 
at 5:30 p.m. on Tuesday, September 8, the Senate will proceed to a 
cloture vote on the Dorgan substitute amendment to the Travel Promotion 
Act.
```
