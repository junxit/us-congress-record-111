# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, AUGUST 6, 2009

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, AUGUST 6, 2009

  Mr. WARNER. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. tomorrow, Thursday, 
August 6; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and that there be a period of morning business until 10 a.m. with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; that 
following morning business the Senate proceed to executive session and 
resume consideration of the nomination of Sonia Sotomayor as provided 
for under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
