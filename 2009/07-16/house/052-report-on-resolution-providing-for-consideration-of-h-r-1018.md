# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1018, RESTORE OUR AMERICAN MUSTANGS ACT

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1018, RESTORE 
                       OUR AMERICAN MUSTANGS ACT

  Mr. McGovern, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 111-212) on the resolution (H. Res. 653) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1018) to amend the Wild Free-
Roaming Horses and Burros Act to improve the management and long-term 
health of wild free-roaming horses and burros, and for other purposes, 
which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
