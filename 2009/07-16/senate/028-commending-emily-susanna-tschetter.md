# COMMENDING EMILY SUSANNA TSCHETTER

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                   COMMENDING EMILY SUSANNA TSCHETTER

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Emily Susanna 
Tschetter, an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard 
work she has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over 
the past several weeks.
  Emily is a graduate of Brookings High School in Brookings, SD. 
Currently she is attending South Dakota State University, where she is 
majoring in biology. She is a hard worker who has been dedicated to 
getting the most out of her internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Emily 
for all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
