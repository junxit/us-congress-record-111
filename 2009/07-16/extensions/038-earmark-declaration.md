# EARMARK DECLARATION

**Speaking:** Heller, Dean (H001041) [R-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3170

```
                          EARMARK DECLARATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DEAN HELLER

                               of nevada

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 16, 2009

  Mr. HELLER. Madam Speaker, Pursuant to the Republican Leadership 
standards on earmarks, I am submitting the following information 
regarding an earmark I received as part of H.R. 3170--Financial 
Services and General Government Appropriations Act, 2010:
  Requesting Member: Congressman Dean Heller
  Bill Number: H.R. 3170--Financial Services and General Government 
Appropriations Act, 2010
  Account: Small Business Administration--Salaries and Expenses
  Legal Name of Requesting Entity: Western Nevada Development District
  Address of Requesting Entity: 704 West Nye Lande, Suite 201, Carson 
City, NV 89701
  Description of Request: $250,000. These funds would contribute to the 
Western Nevada Development District's small business job creation 
efforts, which extend over Carson City, Douglas, Lyon, Mineral, 
Pershing, and Storey Counties in Western Nevada. In the current 
economic climate, small businesses have been especially affected, and 
the efforts of the Western Nevada Development District in promoting job 
creation will help spur local economic development.

                          ____________________
```
