# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2847

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
                 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2847

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 4 p.m., 
Monday, October 5, the Senate proceed to the consideration of Calendar 
No. 87, H.R. 2847, the Commerce, Justice Appropriations Act; and that 
once the bill is reported, there be debate only, with no amendments in 
order except the committee-reported substitute.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
