# IN RECOGNITION OF THE CITY OF DALLAS AND 2009 NATIONAL NIGHT OUT

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
    IN RECOGNITION OF THE CITY OF DALLAS AND 2009 NATIONAL NIGHT OUT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2009

  Mr. SESSIONS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the City of 
Dallas and the Dallas Police Department for its efforts to combat crime 
and its 2009 National Night Out on October 6, 2009.
  Since its inception twenty-six years ago, the National Night Out 
program has successfully reached out to numerous communities nationwide 
in its mission to raise crime and drug prevention awareness. The City 
of Dallas has utilized innovative methods to encourage citywide 
participation such as organizing neighborhood block parties, safety 
fairs, visits from police, contests, and rallies. These gatherings 
unite citizens, law enforcement agencies, businesses, civic 
organizations, and local officials. It shows their resolve to fight 
back against criminals and their commitment to keeping our 
neighborhoods safe. National Night Out has helped the Dallas Police 
strengthen neighborhood spirit and build partnerships within the local 
community.
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in commending the City 
of Dallas and the Dallas Police Department for their hard work and 
dedication. Their efforts have made our community a safer and better 
place.

                          ____________________
```
