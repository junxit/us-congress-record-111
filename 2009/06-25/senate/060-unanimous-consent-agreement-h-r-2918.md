# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2918

```
                 UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2918

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 5:30 p.m. on 
Monday, July 6, the Senate proceed to vote in relation to the McCain 
amendment No. 1366; that prior to the vote, there be 10 minutes of 
debate equally divided and controlled between Senators Nelson of 
Nebraska and McCain or their designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, for the information of the Senate, following 
the disposition of the McCain amendment, the Senate is expected to then 
vote on final passage of the Legislative Branch appropriations bill, so 
it is the McCain amendment and then final passage of the Legislative 
Branch appropriations bill.

                          ____________________
```
