# CLOSE THE SILO/LILO LOOPHOLE ACT

**Speaking:** Baucus, Max (B000243) [D-MT]

```
                    CLOSE THE SILO/LILO LOOPHOLE ACT

  Mr. BAUCUS. Mr. President, I have been extremely concerned about the 
problems lease-in/lease-out and sale-in/lease-out transactions cause 
our tax system for years. I have made clear before that gaming the 
system at the taxpayers' expense is simply unacceptable. In 2004, 
Senator Grassley and I successfully shut down the loophole that allowed 
losses from these deductions, but the current economic crisis has 
created new problems. I applaud the work of Senator Menendez to address 
these issues, and I support his efforts to resolve this problem.

                          ____________________
```
