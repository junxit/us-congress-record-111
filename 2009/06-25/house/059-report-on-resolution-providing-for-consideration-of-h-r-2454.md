# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2454, AMERICAN CLEAN ENERGY AND SECURITY ACT OF 2009

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2454, AMERICAN 
                 CLEAN ENERGY AND SECURITY ACT OF 2009

  Mr. Polis, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 111-185) on the resolution (H. Res. 587) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 2454) to create clean energy jobs, 
achieve energy independence, reduce global warming pollution and 
transition to a clean energy economy, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
