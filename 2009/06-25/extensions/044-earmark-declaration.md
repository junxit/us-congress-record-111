# EARMARK DECLARATION

```
                          EARMARK DECLARATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DEAN HELLER

                               of nevada

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2009

  Mr. HELLER. Madam Speaker, pursuant to the Republican Leadership 
standards on earmarks, I am submitting the following information 
regarding earmarks I received as part of H.R. 2996, the Department of 
the Interior, Environment, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 
2010:
  Requesting Member: Congressman Dean Heller
  Bill Number: H.R. 2996
  Account: Environmental Protection Agency--STAG Water and Wastewater 
Infrastructure Project
  Legal Name of Requesting Entity: Lyon County Utilities
  Address of Requesting Entity: 275 Main Street, Yerington, NV 89447
  Description of Request: $500,000. This project will expand the 
current wastewater treatment facility at Mound House to serve residents 
currently on septic. This will help remediate the groundwater 
contamination currently taking place downstream of the wastewater 
treatment plant's leach field. Through this project groundwater 
contamination can be reversed with improved wastewater treatment and by 
eliminating the existing septic system.
```
