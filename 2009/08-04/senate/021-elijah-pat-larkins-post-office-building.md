# ELIJAH PAT LARKINS POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                ELIJAH PAT LARKINS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1271) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2351 West Atlantic Boulevard in Pompano 
Beach, Florida, as the ``Elijah Pat Larkins Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
