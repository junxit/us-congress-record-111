# HERBERT A LITTLETON POSTAL STATION

**Speaking:** Sanders, Bernard (S000033) [I-VT], Kohl, Herb (K000305) [D-WI], McCaskill, Claire (M001170) [D-MO]

**Measures:** H.R. 2162

```
                   HERBERT A LITTLETON POSTAL STATION

  The bill (H.R. 2162) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 123 11th Avenue South in Nampa, Idaho, as the 
``Herbert A Littleton Postal Station,'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
  Mr. SANDERS. I yield to the chairman, Senator Kohl.
  Mr. KOHL. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mrs. McCASKILL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
order for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
