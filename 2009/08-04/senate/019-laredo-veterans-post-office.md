# LAREDO VETERANS POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 2325

```
                      LAREDO VETERANS POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 2325) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1300 Matamoros Street in Laredo, Texas, as 
the ``Laredo Veterans Post Office,'' was considered, ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
