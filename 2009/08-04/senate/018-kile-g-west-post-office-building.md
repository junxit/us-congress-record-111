# KILE G. WEST POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2422

```
                   KILE G. WEST POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2422) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2300 Scenic Drive in Georgetown, Texas, as 
the ``Kile G. West Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
