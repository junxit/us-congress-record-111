# GERALDINE FERRARO POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 774

```
                 GERALDINE FERRARO POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 774) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 46-02 21st Street in Long Island City, New 
York, as the ``Geraldine Ferraro Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
