# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 5, 2009

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 5, 2009

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Wednesday, August 5; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate proceed to a period of morning 
business until 10 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up 
to 10 minutes each; further, I ask that following morning business, the 
Senate proceed to executive session and resume consideration of the 
nomination of Sonia Sotomayor, as provided under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
