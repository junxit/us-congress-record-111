# FREDERIC REMINGTON POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                FREDERIC REMINGTON POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2090) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 431 State Street in Ogdensburg, New York, as 
the ``Frederic Remington Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
