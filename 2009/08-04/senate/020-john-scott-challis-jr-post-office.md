# JOHN SCOTT CHALLIS, JR. POST OFFICE

```
                  JOHN SCOTT CHALLIS, JR. POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 987) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 601 8th Street in Freedom, Pennsylvania, as 
the ``John Scott Challis, Jr. Post Office,'' was considered, ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
