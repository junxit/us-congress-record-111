# CAROLINE O'DAY POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                  CAROLINE O'DAY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1397) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 41 Purdy Avenue in Rye, New York, as the 
``Caroline O'Day Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
