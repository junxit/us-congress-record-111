# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, DECEMBER 14, 2009

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, DECEMBER 14, 2009

  Mr. UDALL of Colorado. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m., 
Monday, December 14; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate proceed to a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
Republicans controlling the first 30 minutes, and the majority 
controlling the next 30 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
