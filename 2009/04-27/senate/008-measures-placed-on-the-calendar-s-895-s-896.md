# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 895, S. 896

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 895, S. 896

```
            MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 895, S. 896

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, there are two bills at the desk due for a 
second reading, I am told.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the titles of the bills 
the second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 895) to prevent mortgage foreclosures and 
     enhance mortgage credit availability.
       A bill (S. 896) to prevent mortgage foreclosures and 
     enhance mortgage credit availability.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I object to further proceedings with respect 
to these bills, en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bills will be placed 
on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
