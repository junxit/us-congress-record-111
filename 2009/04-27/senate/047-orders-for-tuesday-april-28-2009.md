# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 28, 2009

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 28, 2009

  Mr. BROWN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. tomorrow, 
Tuesday, April 28; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate proceed to executive session under the previous 
order; further, I ask consent that the Senate recess from 12:30 until 
2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly caucus luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
