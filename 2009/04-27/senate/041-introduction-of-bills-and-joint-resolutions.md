# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 899, S. 900, S. 901, S. 902

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Ms. SNOWE (for herself and Ms. Collins):
       S. 899. A bill to establish an assistance program for the 
     construction of digital TV translators to fill coverage gaps 
     that are created from the transition from analog to digital 
     signals; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
           By Mr. WYDEN:
       S. 900. A bill to require the establishment of a credit 
     card safety star rating system for the benefit of consumers, 
     and for other purposes; to the Committee on Banking, Housing, 
     and Urban Affairs.
           By Mr. MERKLEY (for himself and Mr. Wyden):
       S. 901. A bill to establish the Oregon Task Force on 
     Sustainable Revenue for Counties, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
           By Mr. KERRY (for himself, Mr. Reed, and Mr. Inouye):
       S. 902. A bill to provide grants to establish veteran's 
     treatment courts; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
