# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. BROWN. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to 
executive session to consider Calendar Nos. 472 and 473; that the 
nominations be confirmed en bloc, the motions to reconsider be laid on 
the table en bloc; that no further motions be in order; and that any 
statements relating to

[[Page S10439]]

the nominations be printed in the Record as if read; provided further 
that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action and 
the Senate return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nominations considered and confirmed en bloc are as follows:


                         department of justice

       David Lyle Cargill, Jr., of New Hampshire, to be United 
     States Marshal for the District of New Hampshire for the term 
     of four years.
       Timothy J. Heaphy, of Virginia, to be United States 
     Attorney for the Western District of Virginia for the term of 
     four years.
```
