# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 9, 2009

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 9, 2009

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 1 p.m. Monday, 
February 9; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 1, the Economic 
Recovery and Reinvestment Act, as under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
