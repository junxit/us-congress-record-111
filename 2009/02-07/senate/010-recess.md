# RECESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from the State of 
Nevada, I ask unanimous consent the Senate stand in recess until 8:30 
p.m.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 4:24 p.m., recessed until 
8:30 p.m. and reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Tester).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader is recognized.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
