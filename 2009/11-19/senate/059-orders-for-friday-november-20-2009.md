# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 20, 2009

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 20, 2009

  Mr. KAUFMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:45 a.m. 
tomorrow, Friday, November 20; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate resume debate on the motion to proceed 
to H.R. 3590, as provided for under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
