# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Udall, Tom (U000039) [D-NM]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period of morning business for 1 hour, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the time 
equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the majority controlling the first half and the 
Republicans controlling the final half.
  The Senator from New Mexico is recognized.
  Mr. UDALL of New Mexico. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent, 
during the time we control for the next half hour, that we be able to 
engage in a colloquy with other Senators.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
