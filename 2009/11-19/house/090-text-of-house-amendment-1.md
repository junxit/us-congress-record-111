# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** Coffman, Mike (C001077) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 3961

```
H.R. 3961

                  Offered By: Mr. Coffman of Colorado

       Amendment No. 1: Page 13, after line 3, insert the 
     following:

     SEC. 3. PAYFOR THROUGH USE OF UNUSED STIMULUS FUNDS.

       Any unobligated balances, as of the date of the enactment 
     of this Act, of funds made available under division A of the 
     American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 (Public Law 
     111-5) are rescinded.
```
