# IN HONOR OF REV. ROBERT CORNELL, FORMER MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
       IN HONOR OF REV. ROBERT CORNELL, FORMER MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  (Mr. KAGEN asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. KAGEN. Madam Speaker, I would respectfully ask that all House 
Members rise and observe a moment of silence on the passing of our 
former colleague, Congressman Rev. Robert Cornell, who passed away on 
Sunday, May 10. Father Cornell represented the Eighth District of 
Wisconsin in this House from 1975 to 1979. He was a life-long advocate 
for the betterment of

[[Page 12215]]

mankind, a deep-thinking educator, and a keeper of his faith.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Members will rise for a moment of silence.

                          ____________________
```
