# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1728

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:33 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Zapata, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1728. An act to amend the Truth in Lending Act to 
     reform consumer mortgage practices and provide accountability 
     for such practices, to provide certain minimum standards for 
     consumer mortgage loans, and for other purposes.
```
