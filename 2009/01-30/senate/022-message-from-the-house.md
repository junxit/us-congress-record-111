# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 2:35 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the following concurrent resolution, in which it requests the 
concurrence of the Senate:

  H. Con. Res. 27. Concurrent resolution authorizing the use of the 
rotunda of the Capitol for a ceremony in honor of the bicentennial of 
the birth of President Abraham Lincoln.
```
