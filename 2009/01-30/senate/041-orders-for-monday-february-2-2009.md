# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 2009

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 1

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 2009

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 2 p.m., Monday, 
February 2; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate proceed to the consideration of H.R. 1, the 
American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009; further, that at 3:15 
p.m., the Senate proceed to executive session, as under the previous 
order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
