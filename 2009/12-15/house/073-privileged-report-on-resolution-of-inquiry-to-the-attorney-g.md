# PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE ATTORNEY GENERAL

**Measures:** H.Res. 920

```
   PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE ATTORNEY GENERAL

  Mr. CONYERS, from the Committee on the Judiciary, submitted an 
adverse privileged report (Rept. No. 111-378) on the resolution (H. 
Res. 920) directing the Attorney General to transmit to the House of 
Representatives all information in the Attorney General's possession 
regarding certain matters pertaining to detainees held at Naval 
Station, Guantanamo Bay, Cuba who are transferred into the United 
States, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
