# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Pascrell, Bill (P000096) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.Res. 779, H.Res. 942

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BILL PASCRELL, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 15, 2009

  Mr. PASCRELL. Madam Speaker, I want to state for the record that 
yesterday I missed the two rollcall votes of the day. Unfortunately, I 
missed these votes because I was detained in my district.
  Had I been present I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall vote No. 
969 On Motion to Suspend the Rules and Agree, as Amended--H. Res. 779--
Recognizing and supporting the goals and ideals of National Runaway 
Prevention Month.
  Lastly, had I been present I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall 
vote No. 970 On Motion to Suspend the Rules and Agree--H. Res. 942--
Commending the Real Salt Lake soccer club for winning the 2009 Major 
League Soccer Cup.
```
