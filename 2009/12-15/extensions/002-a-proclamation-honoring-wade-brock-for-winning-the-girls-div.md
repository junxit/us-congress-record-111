# A PROCLAMATION HONORING WADE BROCK FOR WINNING THE GIRLS' DIVISION IV STATE SOFTBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

```
 A PROCLAMATION HONORING WADE BROCK FOR WINNING THE GIRLS' DIVISION IV 
                      STATE SOFTBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 15, 2009

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Wade Brock showed hard work and dedication to the sport of 
softball; and
  Whereas, Wade Brock was a supportive coach; and
  Whereas, Wade Brock always displayed sportsmanship on and off of the 
field; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Wade Brock on winning 
the Girls' Division IV State Softball Championship. We recognize the 
tremendous hard work and sportsmanship he has demonstrated during the 
2008-2009 softball season.

                          ____________________
```
