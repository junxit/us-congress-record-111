# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 23, 2009

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 23, 2009

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. tomorrow, 
Thursday, July 23; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of Calendar No. 89, S. 1390, 
which is the Department of Defense authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
