  Legal Name of Requesting Entity: North Country Vietnam Veterans

  Address of Requesting Entity: 27 Town Line Road, P.O. Box 1161,

the renovation and expansion of the existing North Country Veterans 
Service Center, a non-profit organization that provides services and 
support to veterans and their families.
