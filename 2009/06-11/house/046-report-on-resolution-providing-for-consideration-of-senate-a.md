```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENT TO 
      H.R. 1256, FAMILY SMOKING PREVENTION AND TOBACCO CONTROL ACT

  Mr. POLIS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 111-145) on the resolution (H.

Res. 532) providing for consideration of the Senate amendment to the
```
