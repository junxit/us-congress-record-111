# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:44 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House 
disagrees to the amendment of the Senate to the bill (H. R. 2346) 
making supplemental appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 
30, 2009, and for other purposes, and agrees to the conference asked by 
the Senate on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses thereon, and 
appoints the following Members as managers of the conference on the 
part of the House: Mr. Obey, Mr. Murtha, Ms. DeLauro, Mrs. Lowey, Mr. 
Edwards, Mr. Lewis of California, Mr. Young of Florida, and Ms. 
Granger.

                          ____________________
```
