# EXPRESSION OF APPRECIATION

```
                       EXPRESSION OF APPRECIATION

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I walked in the Chamber and saw you 
presiding. And I said to Lula Davis, who helps us so much here, what a 
terrific addition you have been to the Senate. That is really true. The 
people of Alaska are so fortunate to have you in the Senate.
  You are very constructive. You protect the State of Alaska like no 
one I have ever seen look out for the interests of a State.
  And I think everyone in the Senate recognizes what a fine person you 
are, and as the days go on, you are going to get even better. So on a 
personal note, I appreciate all of your good work.
```
