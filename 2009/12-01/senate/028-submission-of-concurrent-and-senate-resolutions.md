# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Res. 365

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mrs. BOXER:
       S. Res. 365. A resolution recognizing the 50th anniversary 
     of the signing of the Antarctic Treaty; considered and agreed 
     to.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S12070, December 1, 2009, in the third column, under the 
heading SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS, the 
following appears: The following concurrent resolutions and Senate 
resolutions were read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated: 
By Mr. DURBIN: S. Res. 365. A resolution recognizing the 50th 
anniversary of the signing of the Antarctic Treaty; considered and 
agreed to.
  
  The online version has been corrected to read: The following 
concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were read, and 
referred (or acted upon), as indicated: By Mrs. BOXER: S. Res. 
365. A resolution recognizing the 50th anniversary of the signing 
of the Antarctic Treaty; considered and agreed to.


 ========================= END NOTE =========================
```
