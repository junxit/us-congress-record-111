# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 6(a) OF RULE XIII WITH RESPECT TO CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 158

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 6(a) OF RULE XIII 
          WITH RESPECT TO CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. POLIS of Colorado, from the Committee on Rules (during the 
Special Order of Mr. Jackson of Illinois), submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 111-15) on the resolution (H. Res. 158) waiving a 
requirement of clause 6(a) of rule XIII with respect to consideration 
of certain resolutions reported from the Committee on Rules, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
