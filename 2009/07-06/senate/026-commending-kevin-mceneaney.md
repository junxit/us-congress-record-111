# COMMENDING KEVIN McENEANEY

**Speaking:** Shaheen, Jeanne (S001181) [D-NH]

```
                       COMMENDING KEVIN McENEANEY

● Mrs. SHAHEEN. Mr. President, I would like to congratulate and 
honor Mr. Kevin McEneaney of Dover, NH, for his faithful service to the 
New Hampshire Board of Land Surveyors. Mr. McEneaney served on the 
board from August 18, 1999, until July 11, 2009, and he led the board 
as chairman during the final 2 years of his term.
  The New Hampshire Board of Land Surveyors' mission is to set the 
standards for licensing and regulating land surveyors. The board sets 
the technical and ethical standards for the profession and is committed 
to upholding the highest level of public safety.
  For a decade, Mr. McEneaney demonstrated the utmost integrity and 
professionalism in his work on the board. His colleagues have 
recognized his diligence and his generosity of spirit. His 
contributions to the Board of Land Surveyors and to the people of New 
Hampshire are admirable. I commend Mr. McEneaney for his exemplary 
service to our State.
  On a personal note, I have known Mr. McEneaney since he was a student 
in my class at Dover High School almost 40 years ago. I have seen 
firsthand his integrity, his work ethic, and his commitment to his 
community. I know he will succeed in whatever he does, and I wish him 
well in his future endeavors.●

                          ____________________
```
