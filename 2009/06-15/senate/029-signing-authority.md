# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the majority 
leader be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions 
from Monday, June 15 to Wednesday, June 18.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
