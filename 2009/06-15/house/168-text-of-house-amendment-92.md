# Text of House Amendment 92

**Speaking:** Lamborn, Doug (L000564) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                        Offered By: Mr. Lamborn

       Amendment No. 92: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec.     . None of the funds appropriated by this Act may 
     be used by the Bureau of Prisons to incarcerate individuals 
     currently held in the Naval Station, Guantanamo Bay, Cuba
```
