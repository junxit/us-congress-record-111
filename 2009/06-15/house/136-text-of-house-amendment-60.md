# Text of House Amendment 60

**Speaking:** Broun, Paul C. (B001262) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                    Offered By: Mr. Broun of Georgia

       Amendment No. 60: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may, 
     for purposes of carrying out the 2010 decennial census, be 
     used to apply the statistical method known as ``sampling''.
```
