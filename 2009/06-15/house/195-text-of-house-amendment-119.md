# Text of House Amendment 119

**Speaking:** Lewis, Jerry (L000274) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                  Offered By: Mr. Lewis of California

       Amendment No. 119: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       ``Sec.   . None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to implement Executive Order 13492 issued January 22, 
     2009, titled ``Review and Disposition of Individuals Detained 
     at the Guantanamo Bay Naval Base and Closure of Detention 
     Facilities''.''
```
