# Text of House Amendment 94

**Speaking:** Pence, Mike (P000587) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                         Offered By: Mr. Pence

       Amendment No. 94: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

[[Page H6812]]

       Sec. 535.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to enforce any of the amendments made by subtitle A 
     of title II of the Bipartisan Campaign Reform Act of 2002 
     (Public Law 107-155).
```
