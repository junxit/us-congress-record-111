# Text of House Amendment 72

**Speaking:** Burton, Dan (B001149) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                    Offered by Mr. Burton of Indiana

       Amendment No. 72: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to adjudicate or assist in a lawsuit seeking to 
     prevent the enforcement of the Defense of Marriage Act 
     (Public Law 104-199).
```
