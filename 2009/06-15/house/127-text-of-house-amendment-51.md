# Text of House Amendment 51

**Speaking:** Heller, Dean (H001041) [R-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                         Offered By: Mr. Heller

       Amendment No.  51: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                TITLE VI--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 601.  None of the funds made available in this Act for 
     the State Criminal Alien Assistance Program under the heading 
     ``State and Local Law Enforcement Assistance'' may be used 
     for any State or local government that has any law, policy, 
     or procedure in effect that circumvents the enforcement of 
     any Federal immigration law.
```
