# Messrs. LAMBORN and ROYCE changed their vote from "yea" to "nay." So (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.

**Speaking:** Braley, Bruce L. (B001259) [D-IA], Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 729
