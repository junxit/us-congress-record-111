# Text of House Amendment 54

**Speaking:** McCaul, Michael T. (M001157) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                         Offered By: Mr. McCaul

       Amendment No. 54: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for a project or program named for an individual then 
     serving as a Member, Delegate, Resident Commissioner, or 
     Senator of the United States Congress.
```
