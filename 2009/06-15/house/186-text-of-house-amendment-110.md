# Text of House Amendment 110

**Speaking:** Cardoza, Dennis A. (C001050) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                        Offered By: Mr. Cardoza

       Amendment No. 110: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                TITLE VI--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. __.  For ``Federal Prison System--Salaries and 
     Expenses'' for hiring additional corrections officers, and 
     the amount otherwise provided by this Act for ``National 
     Science Foundation--Research and Related Activities'' for the 
     procurement of polar icebreaking services is hereby reduced 
     by, $50,000,000.
```
