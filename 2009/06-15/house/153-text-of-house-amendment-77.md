# Text of House Amendment 77

**Speaking:** Hensarling, Jeb (H001036) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                       Offered By: Mr. Hensarling

       Amendment No. 77: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec.--. None of the funds made available by this Act may be 
     used by the Marine Mammal Center, Sausalito, CA, for 
     Emergency Response and Health Investigations for Endangered 
     and Threatened Pinniped Populations in the Pacific.
```
