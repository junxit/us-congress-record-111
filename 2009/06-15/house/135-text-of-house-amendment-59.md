# Text of House Amendment 59

**Speaking:** Broun, Paul C. (B001262) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                    Offered By: Mr. Broun of Georgia

       Amendment No. 59: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following new section:
       Sec. 535.  None of the funds made available by this Act 
     shall be used to establish or implement a National Climate 
     Service.
```
