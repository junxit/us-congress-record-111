# Text of House Amendment 108

**Speaking:** Campbell, John (C001064) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                        Offered By: Mr. Campbell

       Amendment No. 108: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds provided in this Act under the 
     heading ``International Trade Administration--Operations and 
     Administration'' shall be available for the Textile Research 
     Programs project of the Textile/Clothing Textile Corporation, 
     Raleigh, North Carolina, and the amount otherwise provided 
     under such heading (and the portion of such amount specified 
     for Congressionally-designated items) are hereby reduced by 
     $965,000.
```
