# Text of House Amendment 93

**Speaking:** Linder, John (L000321) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                         Offered By: Mr. Linder

       Amendment No. 93: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. 534.  None of the funds available in this Act may be 
     used by the Department of Justice for carrying out or 
     enforcing compliance with section 5 of the Voting Rights Act 
     of 1965 (42 U.S.C. 1973c).
```
