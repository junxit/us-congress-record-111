# Text of House Amendment 53

**Speaking:** Gohmert, Louie (G000552) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                        Offered By: Mr. Gohmert

       Amendment No. 53: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following (and make such technical and 
     conforming changes as may be appropriate):
       Sec. 534.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to pay salaries or expenses related to the 
     Presidential Task Force on the Auto Industry.
```
