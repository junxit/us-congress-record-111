# Text of House Amendment 62

**Speaking:** Quigley, Mike (Q000023) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                        Offered By: Mr. Quigley

       Amendment No. 62: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for either the construction or rental of 
     infrastructure that does not comply with both the General 
     Services Administration Sustainable Design Program for 
     Federal buildings and the Public Buildings Service Project 
     Plan.
```
