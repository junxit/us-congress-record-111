# Text of House Amendment 75

**Speaking:** Hensarling, Jeb (H001036) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                       Offered By: Mr. Hensarling

       Amendment No. 75: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following new section:
       Sec. 535. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to enforce section 526 of the Energy Independence and 
     Security Act of 2007.
```
