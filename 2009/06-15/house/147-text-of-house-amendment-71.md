# Text of House Amendment 71

**Speaking:** Burton, Dan (B001149) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                   Offered By: Mr. Burton of Indiana

       Amendment No. 71: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to relocate the Office of the Census or employees 
     from the Department of Commerce to the jurisdiction of the 
     Executive Office of the President.
```
