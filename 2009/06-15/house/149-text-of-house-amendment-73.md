# Text of House Amendment 73

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike J. (R000572) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                   Offered By: Mr. Rogers of Michigan

       Amendment No. 73: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following:
       Sec. 534.  None of the funds available in this Act may be 
     used to provide rights under Miranda v. Arizona, 384 U.S. 436 
     (1966) by the Department of Justice, including all component 
     agencies, to detainees in the custody of the armed forces of 
     the United States in Afghanistan.
```
