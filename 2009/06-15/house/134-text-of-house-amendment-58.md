# Text of House Amendment 58

**Speaking:** Broun, Paul C. (B001262) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                    Offered By: Mr. Broun of Georgia

       Amendment No. 58: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) the following:
       Sec. 534.  None of the funds available in this Act may be 
     used to carry out or enforce section 5 of the Voting Rights 
     Act of 1965 (42 U.S.C. 1973c).
```
