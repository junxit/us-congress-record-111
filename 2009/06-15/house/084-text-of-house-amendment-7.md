# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Garrett, Scott (G000548) [R-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                 Offered By: Mr. Garrett of New Jersey

       Amendment No. 7: At the end of title V, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       ``Sec. _. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to make full or partial completion of the American 
     Community Survey mandatory or to enforce civil penalties, 
     including fines, for failure to complete the Survey in its 
     entirety.''
```
