# Text of House Amendment 83

**Speaking:** Flake, Jeff (F000444) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                         Offered By: Mr. Flake

       Amendment No. 83: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds provided in this Act under the 
     heading ``National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration--
     Operations, Research, and Facilities'' shall be available for 
     the Natural Stream Design and Restoration project of the West 
     Virginia Department of Natural Resources, Charleston, West 
     Virginia, and the amount otherwise provided under such 
     heading (and the portion of such amount specified for 
     Congressionally-designated items) are hereby reduced by 
     $750,000.
```
