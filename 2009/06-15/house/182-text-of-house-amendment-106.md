# Text of House Amendment 106

**Speaking:** Campbell, John (C001064) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                        Offered By: Mr. Campbell

       Amendment No. 106: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds provided in this Act under the 
     heading ``National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration--
     Operations, Research, and Facilities'' shall be available for 
     the Steller Sea Lion Comanagement, Biosampling and Outreach/
     Education project of The Alaska Sea Otter and Steller Sea 
     Lion Commission, Old Harbor, Alaska, and the amount otherwise 
     provided under such heading (and the portion of such amount 
     specified for Congressionally-designated items) are hereby 
     reduced by $500,000.
```
