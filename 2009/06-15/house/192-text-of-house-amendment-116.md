# Text of House Amendment 116

**Speaking:** McHenry, Patrick T. (M001156) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                        Offered By: Mr. McHenry

       Amendment No. 116: At the end of the bill, insert the 
     following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act to 
     the Department of Commerce or the Bureau of the Census may be 
     used to carry out statistical sampling in violation of 
     section 195 of title 13, United States Code.
```
