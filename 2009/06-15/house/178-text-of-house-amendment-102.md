# Text of House Amendment 102

**Speaking:** Cuellar, Henry (C001063) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                        Offered By: Mr. Cuellar

       Amendment No. 102: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following new section:
       Sec. 535.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to purchase light bulbs unless the light bulbs have 
     the ``Energy Star'' or ``Federal Energy Management Program'' 
     designation.
```
