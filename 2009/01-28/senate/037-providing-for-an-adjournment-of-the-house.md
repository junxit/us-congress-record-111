# PROVIDING FOR AN ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE

```
               PROVIDING FOR AN ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. TESTER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 26, the House 
adjournment resolution, which is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:


[[Page 1792]]

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 26) providing for an 
     adjournment of the House.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. TESTER. I ask unanimous consent the concurrent resolution be 
agreed to and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 26) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
