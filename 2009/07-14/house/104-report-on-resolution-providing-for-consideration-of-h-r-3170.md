# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3170, FINANCIAL SERVICES AND GENERAL GOVERNMENT APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2010

**Measures:** H.Res. 644, H.R. 3170

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3170, 
   FINANCIAL SERVICES AND GENERAL GOVERNMENT APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2010

  Mr. Arcuri (during the Special Order of Mr. Akin), from the Committee 
on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 111-208) on the
```
