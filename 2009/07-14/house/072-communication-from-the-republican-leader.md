# COMMUNICATION FROM THE REPUBLICAN LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE REPUBLICAN LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable John A. Boehner, Republican Leader:

                                                    U.S. Congress,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, July 14, 2009.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, U.S. Capitol,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Pelosi: Pursuant to Section 4 of the Ronald 
     Reagan Centennial Commission Act, (Public Law 111-25), I am 
     pleased to appoint Mr. Elton Gallegly of California as a 
     member of the Commission.
       Mr. Gallegly has expressed interest in serving in this 
     capacity and I am pleased to fulfill his request.
           Sincerely,
                                                  John A. Boehner,
     Republican Leader.
```
