# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 15, 2009

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 15, 2009

  Mr. KAUFMAN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday, July 15; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of Calendar No. 89, S. 
1390, the Department of Defense authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. Shaheen). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
