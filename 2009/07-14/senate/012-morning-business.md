# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period of morning business for 1 hour, with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally 
divided between the two leaders or their designees, with the majority 
controlling the first half and the Republicans controlling the final 
half.
  The Senator from New Hampshire.

                          ____________________
```
