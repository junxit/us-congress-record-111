  Address of requesting entity: 1101 Channelside Drive, Tampa, Florida

  Description: The $5,600,000 will be used for dredging the federal 
navigation channels in Tampa Harbor. The Tampa Harbor is a federally-
authorized project for which, by statute, the Army Corps of Engineers 
is responsible for maintaining. Maintenance of these channels is 
essential to ensuring that commerce can move efficiently and safely 
through Tampa Harbor.
