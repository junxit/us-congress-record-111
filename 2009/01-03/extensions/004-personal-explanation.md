                            HON. BART GORDON

                       Saturday, January 3, 2009

  Mr. GORDON of Tennessee. Madam Speaker, on rollcall 689 and 690, I 
would have voted Aye, but family responsibilities kept me from the 
Chamber. I believe enactment of this legislation would have helped the 
people of Tennessee. Due to a miscommunication on which vote was

was in the Chamber and attempted to vote. I regret the confusion and 
want the Record to reflect my support for both measures.
