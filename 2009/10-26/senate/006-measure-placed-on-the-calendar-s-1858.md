# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1858

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1858

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I am told that S. 1858 is at the desk and is 
due for a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the bill by 
title for the second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1858) to require Senate candidates to file 
     designations, statements, and reports in electronic form.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I object to any further proceedings with 
regard to this bill.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be placed on the calendar.
```
