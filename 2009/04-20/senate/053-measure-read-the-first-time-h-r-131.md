# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 131

**Speaking:** Stabenow, Debbie (S000770) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 131

```

  Ms. STABENOW. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 131 has been 
received from the House and is at the desk.
```
