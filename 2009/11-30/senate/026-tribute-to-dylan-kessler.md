# TRIBUTE TO DYLAN KESSLER

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO DYLAN KESSLER

● Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize Dylan Thomas 
Kessler, an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard 
work he has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over 
the past several months.
  Dylan is a graduate of Roncalli High School in Aberdeen, SD. 
Currently he is attending the Hillsdale College, where he is majoring 
in English. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the 
most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Dylan 
for all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
