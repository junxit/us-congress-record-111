# TRIBUTE TO THOMAS KURT JAROS

```
                      TRIBUTE TO THOMAS KURT JAROS

● Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize Thomas Kurt 
Jaros, an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work 
he has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the 
past several months.
  Kurt is a graduate of Downers Grove South High School in Downers 
Grove, IL. Currently he is attending the Biola University, where he is 
majoring in philosophy and political science. He is a hard worker who 
has been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Kurt for 
all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●
```
