# TRIBUTE TO DENNIS D'AQUILA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO DENNIS D'AQUILA

● Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize Dennis D'Aquila, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Dennis is a graduate of Wantagh High School in Wantagh, NY. Currently 
he is attending the Catholic University of America, where he is 
majoring in politics. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to 
getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Dennis 
for all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
