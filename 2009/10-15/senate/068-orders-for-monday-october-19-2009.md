# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 19, 2009

**Speaking:** Casey, Robert P., Jr. (C001070) [D-PA]

**Measures:** S. 1776

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 19, 2009

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m. on Monday, 
October 19; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate proceed to a period of morning business until 4:30 
p.m, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each; that following morning business, the Senate resume consideration 
of the motion to proceed to S. 1776, the Medicare Physicians Fairness 
Act of 2009, under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
