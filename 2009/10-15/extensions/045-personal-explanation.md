# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Conyers, John, Jr. (C000714) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.Res. 768, H.Res. 786, H.Res. 816, H.R. 1327, H.R. 3371

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOHN CONYERS, JR.

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2009

  Mr. CONYERS. Madam Speaker, on October 14, 2009, I was unable to cast 
votes, due to personal reasons. I was not present for rollcall votes 
775 through 779. Had I been present, I would have casted a ``yea'' vote 
for final passage of H. Res. 768, Expressing support for the 
designation of the month of October as ``National Work and Family 
Month''; H.R. 1327, Iran Sanctions Enabling Act of 2009; H. Res. 816, 
Mourning the loss of life caused by the earthquakes and tsunamis that

[[Page E2551]]

occurred on September 29, 2009 in American Samoa and Samoa; H.R. 3371, 
Airline Safety and Pilot Training Improvement Act of 2009 and H. Res. 
786, Commemorating the canonization of Father Damien de Veuster, SS.CC. 
to sainthood.

                          ____________________
```
