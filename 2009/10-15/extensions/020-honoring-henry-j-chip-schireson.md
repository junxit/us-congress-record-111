# HONORING HENRY J. "CHIP" SCHIRESON

**Speaking:** Gerlach, Jim (G000549) [R-PA]

```
                  HONORING HENRY J. ``CHIP'' SCHIRESON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIM GERLACH

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2009

  Mr. GERLACH. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Honorable 
Henry J. ``Chip'' Schireson who is completing his 25th year of 
dedicated public service as a Magisterial District Judge in Lower 
Merion, Montgomery County, Pennsylvania.
  While efficiently administering a court that averages 1,000 filings 
each month, Judge Schireson has made a tremendous commitment to 
positively impacting the youth in the community. His efforts include 
establishing an innovative and award-winning public service program at 
Bryn Mawr Hospital, which allows juvenile offenders to serve others to 
avoid a criminal record. That is just one example of how Judge 
Schireson utilizes creative sanctions to change the attitudes of 
offenders and, in some cases, help turn around lives.
  Madam Speaker, I ask that my colleagues join me today in recognizing 
the Honorable Henry J. ``Chip'' Schireson as he celebrates this 
memorable milestone and honor his extraordinary dedication to making 
Lower Merion a great place to live, work and raise a family.

                          ____________________
```
