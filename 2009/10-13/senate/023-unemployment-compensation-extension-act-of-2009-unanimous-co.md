# UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2009--UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST

**Speaking:** Stabenow, Debbie (S000770) [D-MI], Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

**Measures:** H.R. 3548

```
  UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2009--UNANIMOUS CONSENT 
                                REQUEST

  Ms. STABENOW. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 3548, which was 
received from the House; further, that a Reid substitute amendment,

third time and passed; the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The Senator from Utah is recognized.
```
