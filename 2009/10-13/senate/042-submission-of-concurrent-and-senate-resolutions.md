            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

     Agency for International Development (USAID); to the 
     Committee on Foreign Relations.

             Grassley, Mrs. Hutchison, Mr. Menendez, Mr. Crapo, 
             and Mr. Bennett):
       S. Res. 313. A resolution supporting the goals and ideals 
     of Red Ribbon Week, 2009; considered and agreed to.
