# A PROCLAMATION HONORING JOHNNY IACOBUCCI FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION III STATE BASEBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING JOHNNY IACOBUCCI FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION 
                    III STATE BASEBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 30, 2009

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Johnny Iacobucci showed hard work and dedication to the 
sport of baseball; and
  Whereas, Johnny Iacobucci was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Johnny Iacobucci always displayed sportsmanship on and off 
of the field; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Johnny Iacobucci on 
winning the Boys' Division III State Baseball Championship. We 
recognize the tremendous hard work and sportsmanship he has 
demonstrated during the 2008-2009 baseball season.

                          ____________________
```
