**Measures:** H.Res. 788, H.R. 3183

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF CONFERENCE REPORT 
    ON H.R. 3183, ENERGY AND WATER DEVELOPMENT AND RELATED AGENCIES 
                        APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2010
```
