# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 19, 2009

**Speaking:** Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND]

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 19, 2009

  Mr. DORGAN. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes

[[Page S6827]]

its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. tomorrow, Friday, June 
19; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and there be a period of morning business, with Senators permitted 
to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
