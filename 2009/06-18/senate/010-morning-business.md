# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for morning business for 1 hour, with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the time divided and 
controlled between the two leaders or their designees, with the 
majority controlling the first half and the Republicans controlling the 
final half.
  The Senator from New Jersey is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
