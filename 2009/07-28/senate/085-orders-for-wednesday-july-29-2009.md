# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 29, 2009

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3183

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 29, 2009

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. tomorrow, July 29; that 
following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then 
resume consideration of Calendar No. 116, H.R. 3183, the Energy and 
Water Appropriations Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
