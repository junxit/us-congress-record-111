**Measures:** H.Res. 685, H.R. 3326

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3326, 
             DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2010
```
