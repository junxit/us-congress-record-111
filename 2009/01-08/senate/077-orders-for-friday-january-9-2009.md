# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 9, 2009

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                   ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 9, 2009

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. Friday, 
January 9; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and there then be a period of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
