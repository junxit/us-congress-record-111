# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, there will be a 
period of morning business with Senators allowed to speak for up to 10 
minutes each. All Senators are invited to gather in the Senate Chamber 
at 12:45 p.m. to proceed to the Hall of the House for the counting of 
electoral ballots. The joint session will commence at 1 p.m. The Senate 
will recess from 3:30 until 4:45 to allow for a special Democratic 
caucus meeting. If none have been to the counting of the electoral 
ballots, it is quite historic and interesting, and people should 
consider going to that.

                          ____________________
```
