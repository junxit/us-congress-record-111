# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Conrad, Kent (C000705) [D-ND]

                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


           committee on agriculture, nutrition, and forestry

  Mr. CONRAD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee

session of the Senate on Wednesday, April 1, 2009, at 2 p.m. in room 
216 of the Hart Senate office building.

Senate on Wednesday, April 1, 2009, at 9:30 a.m.

 subcommittee on emerging threats and capabilities and subcommittee on
