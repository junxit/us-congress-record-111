**Speaking:** Menendez, Robert (M000639) [D-NJ], Bennet, Michael F. (B001267) [D-CO]

```

  Mr. MENENDEZ. Mr. President, there will be no rollcall votes during 
Friday's session of the Senate. I ask unanimous consent that following

  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BENNET. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
```
