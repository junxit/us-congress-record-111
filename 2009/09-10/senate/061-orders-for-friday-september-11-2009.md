# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 11, 2009

```
                 ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 11, 2009

  Mr. MENENDEZ. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Friday, September 11; that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the

[[Page 21425]]

morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day; that there then be a moment of 
silence in commemoration of the eighth anniversary of the September 11 
attacks; further, that following the moment of silence, the Senate 
proceed to a period of morning business until 10:30 a.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; and, finally, I 
ask that following morning business, the Senate resume consideration of 
Calendar No. 153, H.R. 3288, the Transportation, HUD, and related 
agencies appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
