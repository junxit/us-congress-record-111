# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 30, 2009

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 30, 2009

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I now ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 11 a.m. Monday, 
March 30; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate begin consideration of the budget resolution 
reported by the Budget Committee.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
