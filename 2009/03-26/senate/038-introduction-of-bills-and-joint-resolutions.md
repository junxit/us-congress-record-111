       S. 713. A bill to require the Administrator of the Federal 
     Emergency Management Agency to quickly and fairly address the 
     abundance of surplus manufactured housing units stored by the 
     Federal Government around the country at taxpayer expense; to 
     the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S3897, March 26, 2009, in the second column, the 
following appears: S. 713. A bill to require the Administrator of 
the Federal Emergency Management Agency to quickly and fairly 
address the abundance of surplus manufactures housing units stored 
by the Federal Government around the country at taxpayer expense.
  
  The online version was corrected to read: S. 713. A bill to 
require the Administrator of the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency to quickly and fairly address the abundance of surplus 
manufactured housing units stored by the Federal Government around 
the country at taxpayer expense.
