# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


             Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs

  Mr. KAUFMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the

Oversight To Increase Transparency and Reduce Risks.''

           Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions

  Mr. KAUFMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions be authorized to 
meet during the session of the Senate on Monday, June 22, 2009 at 3 
p.m. in room 325 of the Russell Senate Office Building.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
