                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. LARSON of Connecticut. Madam Speaker, on March 4, 2009. I missed 
two votes, that on H. Res. 195 (rollcall vote No. 95) and H. Res. 45

for both H. Res. 195, Recognizing and honoring the employees of the 
Department of Homeland Security on its sixth anniversary for their 
continuous efforts to keep the Nation safe, and H. Res. 45, Raising 
awareness and promoting education on the criminal justice system by 
establishing March as ``National Criminal Justice Month.''
