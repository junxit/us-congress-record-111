# Text of Senate Amendment 651

**Speaking:** DeMint, Jim (D000595) [R-SC]

```
  SA 651. Mr. DeMINT submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 1105, making omnibus appropriations for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2009, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. PROHIBITION OF USE OF APPROPRIATED FUNDS TO STUDY, 
                   RECOMMEND, OR IMPLEMENT A NEW METHOD OF 
                   TAXATION BASED ON VEHICLE MILES TRAVELED.

       Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, none of 
     the funds appropriated or otherwise made available by this 
     Act may be used to study, recommend, or implement a new 
     method of taxation based on vehicle miles traveled.
                                 ______
```
