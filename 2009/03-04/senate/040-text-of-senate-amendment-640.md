# Text of Senate Amendment 640

**Speaking:** DeMint, Jim (D000595) [R-SC]

```
  SA 640. Mr. DeMINT submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 1105, making omnibus appropriations for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2009, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. __. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, 
     none of the funds made available in Title II of Division C 
     under the heading ``Juvenile Justice Programs'' under the 
     heading ``Office of Justice Programs'' may be used for the At 
     the Park After Dark Gang Prevention Program in California 
     through a congressionally directed spending initiative and 
     the amount made available under that heading is reduced by 
     $50,000.

                                 ______
```
