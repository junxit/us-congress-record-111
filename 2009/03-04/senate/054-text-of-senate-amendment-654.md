# Text of Senate Amendment 654

**Speaking:** DeMint, Jim (D000595) [R-SC]

```
  SA 654. Mr. DeMINT submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 1105, making omnibus appropriations for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2009, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. USE OF APPROPRIATED FUNDS FOR 2010 CENSUS.

       Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, funds 
     appropriated or otherwise made available under this Act for 
     the 2010 Census shall be used in a nonpartisan fashion 
     preserving the integrity and independence of the census 
     process, and no such funds shall be used by the Executive 
     Office of the President or other political officials to 
     interfere with the conduct of the 2010 Census or to 
     manipulate the census process for partisan gain.
                                 ______
```
