# Text of Senate Amendment 653

**Speaking:** DeMint, Jim (D000595) [R-SC]

```
  SA 653. Mr. DeMINT submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 1105, making omnibus appropriations for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2009, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in title VI of division D, insert 
     the following:
       Sec. __.  No funds made available under this Act shall be 
     used to ensure compliance with the requirements of subchapter 
     IV of chapter 31 of title 40, United States Code, or any 
     other provision of law requiring prevailing wages to be paid.
                                 ______
```
