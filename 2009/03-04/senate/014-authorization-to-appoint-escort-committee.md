# AUTHORIZATION TO APPOINT ESCORT COMMITTEE

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
               AUTHORIZATION TO APPOINT ESCORT COMMITTEE

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, before responding to the Senator from 
Iowa, I ask unanimous consent that the President of the Senate be 
authorized to appoint a committee on the part of the Senate to join 
with a like committee on the part of the House of Representatives to 
escort the Honorable Gordon Brown, Prime Minister of the United 
Kingdom, into the House Chamber for the joint meeting.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
