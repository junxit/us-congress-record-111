# Text of Senate Amendment 645

**Speaking:** DeMint, Jim (D000595) [R-SC]

```
  SA 645. Mr. DeMINT submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 1105, making omnibus appropriations for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2009, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 427, lines 10 and 11, strike ``$6,590,000, to 
     remain available until expended'' and insert ``$5,090,000, to 
     remain available until expended:  Provided, That no amounts 
     made available under this heading shall be made available for 
     the California National Historic Trail Interpretive Center''.
                                 ______
```
