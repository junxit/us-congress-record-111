# Text of Senate Amendment 660

**Speaking:** Voinovich, George V. (V000126) [R-OH]

```
  SA 660. Mr. VOINOVICH submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill H.R. 1105, making omnibus appropriations for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 2009, and for other purposes; which 
was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 444, line 21, insert ``, of which $2,000,000 shall 
     be available for Cuyahoga Valley National Park, of which 
     $1,500,000 shall be available for emergencies and hardships, 
     of which $1,500,000 shall be available for inholdings,'' 
     before ``and of which''.
                                 ______
```
