# Text of Senate Amendment 643

**Speaking:** DeMint, Jim (D000595) [R-SC]

```
  SA 643. Mr. DeMINT submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 1105, making omnibus appropriations for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2009, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of title VII in Division A, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       Sec. 7__.  Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, 
     none of the funds made available under the heading ``salaries 
     and expenses'' under the heading ``Agricultural Research 
     Service'' in title I may be used for a honey bee laboratory 
     in Texas through a congressionally directed spending 
     initiative and the amount made available under that heading 
     is reduced by $1,762,000.
                                 ______
```
