# Text of Senate Amendment 652

**Speaking:** DeMint, Jim (D000595) [R-SC]

```
  SA 652. Mr. DeMINT submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 1105, making omnibus appropriations for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2009, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:


[[Page S2777]]


       On page 823, beginning on line 12, strike ``may be used to 
     pay'' and all that follows through ``practice abortions'' on 
     line 14 and insert ``may be made available for any private, 
     nongovernmental, or multilateral organization that performs 
     or actively promotes abortion as a method of birth control''.

                                 ______
```
