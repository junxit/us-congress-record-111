# JOHN J. SHIVNEN POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                  JOHN J. SHIVNEN POST OFFICE BUILDING

Postal Service located at 140 Merriman Road in Garden City, Michigan, 
as the ``John J. Shivnen Post Office Building,'' was ordered to a third
```
