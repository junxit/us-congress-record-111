# IRAQ AND AFGHANISTAN VETERANS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 3386

```
           IRAQ AND AFGHANISTAN VETERANS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 3386) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1165 2nd Avenue in Des Moines, Iowa, as the 
``Iraq and Afghanistan Veterans Memorial Post Office,'' was ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
