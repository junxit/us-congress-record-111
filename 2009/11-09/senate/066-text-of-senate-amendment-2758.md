# Text of Senate Amendment 2758

**Speaking:** Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK]

```
  SA 2758. Mr. INHOFE (for himself, Mr. Brownback, Mr. Cornyn, Mr. 
Hatch, Mr. Kyl, Mr. Thune, Mr. Vitter, Mr. DeMint, Mr. Enzi, and Mr. 
Roberts) submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 
2730 proposed by Mr. Johnson (for himself and Mrs. Hutchison) to the 
bill H.R. 3082, making appropriations for military construction, the 
Department of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2010, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 60, after line 24, add the following:
       Sec. 608. (a) None of the funds appropriated or otherwise 
     made available by this Act or any prior Act may be used to 
     construct or modify a facility or facilities in the United 
     States or its territories to permanently or temporarily hold 
     any individual who was detained as of October 1, 2009, at 
     Naval Station, Guantanamo Bay, Cuba.
       (b) In this section, the term ``United States'' means the 
     several States and the District of Columbia.
                                 ______
```
