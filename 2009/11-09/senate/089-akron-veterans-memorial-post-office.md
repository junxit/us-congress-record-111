# AKRON VETERANS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 2004

```
                  AKRON VETERANS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 2004) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 4282 Beach Street in Akron, Michigan, as the 
``Akron Veterans Memorial Post Office,'' was ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
