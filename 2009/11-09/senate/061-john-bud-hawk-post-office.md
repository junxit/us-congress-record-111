# JOHN ``BUD'' HAWK POST OFFICE

```
                     JOHN ``BUD'' HAWK POST OFFICE

Postal Service located at 10355 Northeast Valley Road in Rollingbay, 
Washington, as the ``John `Bud' Hawk Post Office,'' was ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
