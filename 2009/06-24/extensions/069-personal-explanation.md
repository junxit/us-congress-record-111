# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** McHenry, Patrick T. (M001156) [R-NC]

**Measures:** S. 407, H.R. 1016, H.R. 1172, H.R. 1211, H.R. 1777

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. PATRICK T. McHENRY

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 24, 2009

  Mr. McHENRY. Madam Speaker, had I been present to vote on S. 407 
``Veterans' Compensation Cost-of-Living Adjustment Act of 2009'' my 
vote would have been cast in support of this bill. In addition, had I 
been present I would have cast my vote in support of the following 
bills, H.R. 1016 ``Veterans Health Care Budget Reform and Transparency 
Act of 2009'', H.R. 1211 ``Women Veterans Health Care Improvement 
Act'', H.R. 1172 ``To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to 
include on the Internet website of the Department of Veterans Affairs a 
list of organizations that provide scholarships to veterans and their 
survivors'' and H.R. 1777 ``Making technical corrections to the Higher 
Education Act of 1965, as amended''.
```
