# MEASURES REFERRED

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1172. An act to direct the Secretary of Veterans 
     Affairs to include on the Internet website of the Department 
     of Veterans Affairs a list of organizations that provide 
     scholarships to veterans and their survivors; to the 
     Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
       H.R. 1211. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     expand and improve health care services available to women 
     veterans, especially those serving in Operation Enduring 
     Freedom and Operation Iraqi Freedom, from the Department of 
     Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Veterans' Affairs.
```
