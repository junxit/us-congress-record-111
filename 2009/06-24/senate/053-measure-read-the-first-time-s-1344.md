# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1344

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1344

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand that S. 1344, introduced 
earlier today by a Senator, is at the desk and due for its first 
reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the title of the bill.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1344) to temporarily protect the solvency of the 
     Highway Trust Fund.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask now for its second reading, but I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
