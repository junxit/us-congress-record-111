# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 1549

```
Committee Meetings
TRANSPORTATION, HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT AND RELATED AGENCIES 
APPROPRIATIONS FY 2010
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Transportation, Housing 
and Urban Development, and Related Agencies approved for full Committee 
action the Transportation, Housing and Urban Development and Related 
Agencies appropriations for fiscal year 2010.
PREVENTING UNFAIR TRADING BY GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS
Committee on Financial Services: Held a hearing entitled ``Preventing 
Unfair Trading by Government Officials.'' Testimony was heard from 
Representatives Slaughter and Baird; H. David Kotz, Inspector General, 
SEC; and public witnesses.
PRESERVATION OF ANTIBIOTICS FOR MEDICAL TREATMENT ACT OF 2009
Committee on Rules: Held a hearing on H.R. 1549, Preservation of 
Antibiotics for Medical Treatment Act of 2009. Testimony was heard from 
Representatives Schakowsky and Boswell; Joshua M. Sharfstein, M.D., 
Principal Deputy Commissioner, Food and Drug Administration, Department 
of Health and Human Services; and public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
