# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JULY 14, 2009

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1390

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JULY 14, 2009

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. tomorrow, July 
14; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and then there 
be a period for morning business of 1 hour, with the time equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders or their designees, with 
the majority controlling the first half and the Republicans controlling 
the final half, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each; further, that following morning business, the Senate resume 
consideration of Calendar No. 89, S. 1390, the Department of Defense 
authorization bill; then I ask the Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. until 
2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly caucus luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
