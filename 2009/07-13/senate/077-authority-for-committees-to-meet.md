# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


          Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions be authorized to meet during 
the session of the Senate on Monday, July 13, 2009, at 2 p.m. in room 
325 of the Russell Senate Office Building.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                       Committee on the Judiciary

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on the Judiciary be authorized to meet during the session of the 
Senate, on July 13, 2009, at 10 a.m., in room SH-216 of the Hart Senate 
Office Building, to conduct a hearing on the nomination of Sonia 
Sotomayor to be an Associate Justice of the Supreme Court of the United 
States.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
