# Text of Senate Amendment 21

**Speaking:** Vitter, David (V000127) [R-LA]

```
  SA 21. Mr. VITTER submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 22, to designate certain land components of the 
National Wilderness Preservation System, to authorize certain programs 
and activities in the Department of the Interior and the Department of 
Agriculture, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. EFFECTIVE DATE.

       This Act shall not go into effect until--
       (1) the President certifies that the Act would not increase 
     the Federal deficit; and
       (2) the Secretary of Commerce and the Secretary of Energy 
     certify that the Act would not limit access to energy 
     resources.
```
