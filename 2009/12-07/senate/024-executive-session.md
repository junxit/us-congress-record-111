# EXECUTIVE SESSION

```
                           EXECUTIVE SESSION

                                 ______
                                 

                         NOMINATIONS DISCHARGED

  Mr. BROWN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate 
proceed to executive session and the Foreign Relations Committee be 
discharged en bloc from PN1001, PN1002, PN1003, PN1005, PN1016; and 
then the Senate proceed en bloc to the consideration of the 
nominations; that the nominations be confirmed en bloc; the motions to 
reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table en bloc; that no 
further motions be in order, and any statements relating to the 
nominations be printed in the Record; the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action and the Senate then resume legislative 
session.
```
