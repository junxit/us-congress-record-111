# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the motion to proceed to H.R. 3590. Under a 
previous agreement, the debate will continue with alternating hours 
from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., with the majority controlling the first hour. 
The time from 6 to 6:30 in the evening will be under the control of the 
majority; 6:30 until 7:15 p.m. will be under the control of the 
Republican side; from 7:15 to 7:30 the majority will control that time; 
the time from 7:30 to 8 will be for the two leaders, with Senator 
McConnell controlling the first 15 minutes. At 8 p.m. tonight, the 
Senate will proceed to a rollcall vote on the motion to invoke cloture 
on the motion to proceed to the health care legislation.
```
