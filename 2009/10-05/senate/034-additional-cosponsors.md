                                 S. 213

revise the authorized route of the North Country National Scenic Trail

National Forest, and for other purposes.

  At the request of Mr. Durbin, the name of the Senator from Rhode 
Island (Mr. Whitehouse) was added as a cosponsor of S. 565, a bill to
