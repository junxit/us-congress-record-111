# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 6, 2009

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 6, 2009

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. Tuesday, 
October 6; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate then proceed to executive session to consider the 
nomination of Thomas Perez, to be Assistant Attorney General, as 
provided for under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
