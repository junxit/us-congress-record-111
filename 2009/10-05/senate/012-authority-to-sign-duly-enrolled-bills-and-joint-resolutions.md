**Speaking:** Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND]

```
      AUTHORITY TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the majority
```
