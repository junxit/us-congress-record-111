# 1ST LIEUTENANT LOUIS ALLEN POST OFFICE

```
                 1ST LIEUTENANT LOUIS ALLEN POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 2877) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 76 Brookside Avenue in Chester, New York, as 
the ``1st Lieutenant Louis Allen Post Office'', was ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
