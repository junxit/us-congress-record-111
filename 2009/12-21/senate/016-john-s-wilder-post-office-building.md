# JOHN S. WILDER POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                  JOHN S. WILDER POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1817) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 116 North West Street in Somerville, 
Tennessee, as the ``John S. Wilder Post Office Building'', was ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
