# W. HAZEN HILLYARD POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                 W. HAZEN HILLYARD POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3767) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 170 North Main Street in Smithfield, Utah, as 
the ``W. Hazen Hillyard Post Office Building'', was ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
