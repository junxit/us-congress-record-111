# PATRICIA D. McGINTY-JUHL POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3539

```
             PATRICIA D. McGINTY-JUHL POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3539) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 427 Harrison Avenue in Harrison, New Jersey, 
as the ``Patricia D. McGinty-Juhl Post Office Building'', was ordered
```
