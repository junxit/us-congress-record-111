# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, DECEMBER 21, 2009

**Speaking:** Kaufman, Edward E. (K000373) [D-DE]

**Measures:** H.R. 3590

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, DECEMBER 21, 2009

  Mr. KAUFMAN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now stand in recess until 12 noon today, that immediately upon 
reconvening at noon and after any leader time, the Senate then resume 
consideration of H.R. 3590, with the time until 12:30 p.m. equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders or their designees; that 
from 12:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m., there be 1-hour alternating blocks of 
time, with the majority controlling the first block; that all 
postcloture time continue to run during any recess, adjournment, or 
period of morning business until 6:30 p.m. Monday.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, the request is agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
