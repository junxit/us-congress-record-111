# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2647, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2010

**Measures:** H.Res. 572, H.R. 2647

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2647, NATIONAL 
             DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2010

  Ms. PINGREE of Maine, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 111-182) on the resolution (H. Res. 572) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2647) to authorize 
appropriations for fiscal year 2010 for military activities of the 
Department of Defense, to prescribe military personnel strengths for 
fiscal year 2010, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
