# COMMENDING KATHERINE DOUGLAS

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      COMMENDING KATHERINE DOUGLAS

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I rise to recognize Katherine 
Douglas, an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard 
work she has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over 
the past several weeks.
  Katherine is a graduate of T.F. Riggs High School in Pierre, SD. 
Currently she is attending the University of South Dakota, where she is 
majoring in political science. She is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of her internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to 
Katherine

[[Page S6936]]

for all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in 
the years to come.
```
