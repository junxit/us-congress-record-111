# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BEGICH. Mr. President, under a previous order, tomorrow at 
approximately 10 a.m. the Senate will proceed

[[Page 15898]]

to impeachment proceedings and will conduct a live quorum call. 
Senators are encouraged to be in the Chamber and seated at their desks 
at 10 a.m. When a quorum is ascertained, the Senate will receive the 
House managers, who will deliver the articles of impeachment, and the 
Senators will be sworn in as a body in order to proceed with the 
impeachment of Samuel B. Kent, a Judge of the U.S. District Court for 
the Southern District of Texas. The Senate will then consider two 
resolutions by consent.
  At 11 a.m., the Senate will proceed to the cloture vote on the Koh 
nomination.

                          ____________________
```
