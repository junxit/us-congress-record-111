# EARMARK DECLARATION

**Speaking:** Miller, Candice S. (M001150) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2892

```
                          EARMARK DECLARATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CANDICE S. MILLER

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 23, 2009

  Mrs. MILLER. Madam Speaker, pursuant to the Republican Leadership 
standards on earmarks, I am submitting the following information 
regarding earmarks I received as part of H.R. 2892 the Department of 
Homeland Security Appropriations Act of 2010
  Requesting Member: Congressman Candice S. Miller
  Bill Number: H.R. 2892
  Account: State and Local Programs/Emergency Operations Center
  Legal Name of Requesting Entity: Macomb County Emergency Management 
and Communications Center
  Address of Requesting Entity: 10 N. Main St. 1st Floor, Mt. Clemens, 
MI 48043
  Description of Request: This request, in the amount of $250,000.00, 
would be used to purchase and install communications and technology 
equipment for the Macomb County Emergency Communications Center. The 
EOC is paramount to assisting and supporting the response and recovery 
efforts of the local community. With this funding, the EOC will be able 
to provide both primary and secondary communication and technology 
modes that will allow them to be interoperable within our EOC at a 
local, state and federal level.

                          ____________________
```
