# RECOGNIZING PATTI GILMORE OF HUTTO, TX

```
                 RECOGNIZING PATTI GILMORE OF HUTTO, TX

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 23, 2009

  Mr. CARTER. Madam Speaker, I would like to recognize Patti Gilmore 
with the City of Hutto, Texas for her countless hours of volunteerism 
to the Team Hutto, Adopt-a-Unit Program.
  The cities of Hutto and Taylor, Texas jointly adopted the 1-4 ARB 
Unit out of Fort Hood, Texas last year providing the deployed troops 
and their families with supplies, encouragement and a sense of family 
from their neighboring cities in Texas District 31. Patti has been 
instrumental in obtaining donations, organizing events and providing 
support to the deployed troops and their families. Her acts are a sign 
of true patriotism to our great nation and to the men and women who 
serve our country.
  It is an honor to recognize Patti, and she continues to be a true 
inspiration through her acts of support and dedication.

                          ____________________
```
