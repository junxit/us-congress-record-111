# MORNING IN AMERICA BRINGS NEW COURSE OF ACTION

```
             MORNING IN AMERICA BRINGS NEW COURSE OF ACTION

  (Mr. JOHNSON of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. JOHNSON of Georgia. Good morning, everybody; good morning, 
America. How are you? I am showing my age a little bit.
  Ladies and gentlemen, it is always great to be an American, and it is 
always a great time in America because we have hope and vision for the 
future, and I am happy to report to you that Congress and the Obama 
administration have departed on a new course of action--no more voodoo 
economics, no more trickle-down economics. These are failed policies, 
and it is time for something new.
  Whenever something new is on the table, there are always those who, 
instead of appreciating being Americans, they complain and don't add 
anything positive to the discussion.
  But despite the obstacles that we confront, we will continue down 
this road. And, indeed, America will continue to experience morning in 
America.

                          ____________________
```
