# PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING PROCEEDINGS TODAY

**Speaking:** Andrews, Robert E. (A000210) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.Res. 250, H.R. 1388

```
  PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING PROCEEDINGS 
                                 TODAY

  Mr. ANDREWS. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, during 
consideration of H.R. 1388 pursuant to House Resolution 250, the Chair 
may reduce to 2 minutes the minimum time for electronic voting under 
clause 6 of rule XVIII and clauses 8 and 9 of rule XX.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New Jersey?
  There was no objection.
```
