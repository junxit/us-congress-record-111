# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

**Speaking:** Bingaman, Jeff (B000468) [D-NM]

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


               COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND NATURAL RESOURCES

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a hearing has been 
scheduled before the Subcommittee on Energy of the Senate Committee on 
Energy and Natural Resources. The hearing will be held on Wednesday, 
March 25, 2009, at 9:30 a.m., in room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate 
Office Building.
  The purpose of the legislative hearing is to receive testimony on 
draft legislation to improve energy market transparency and regulation.
  Because of the limited time available for the hearing, witnesses may 
testify by invitation only. However, those wishing to submit written 
testimony for the hearing record may do so by sending it to the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, U.S. Senate, Washington, DC 
20510-6150, or by e-mail to [email protected]
.gov.
  For further information, please contact Tara Billingsley at (202) 
224-4756 or Rosemarie Calabro at (202) 224-5039.
```
