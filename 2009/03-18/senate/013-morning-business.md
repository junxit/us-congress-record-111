# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 
1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each and with the time equally divided, the Republicans controlling the 
first half hour and the majority controlling the second half hour.
  The Senator from Oklahoma is recognized.
  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, it is my understanding I have the first 15 
minutes, and I would ask the Chair to advise me when I have 1 minute 
left.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Chair will do so.

                          ____________________
```
