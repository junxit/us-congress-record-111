```

  Ms. STABENOW. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to a 
period of morning business with Senators allowed to speak for up to 10 
minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, that would be wonderful. I have spoken to 
the Republican leader. We can come back and start voting at 1:45. I 
would ask that be the order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. The problem now is, the Republican leader and I did not 
know about a problem. So we will come back about 2.
  I yield to my distinguished colleague.
```
