# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1728, MORTGAGE REFORM AND ANTI-PREDATORY LENDING ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 406, H.R. 1728

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1728, 
             MORTGAGE REFORM AND ANTI-PREDATORY LENDING ACT

  Mr. ARCURI, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 111-98) on the resolution (H. Res. 406) providing for 
further consideration of the bill (H.R. 1728) to amend the Truth in 
Lending Act to reform consumer mortgage practices and provide 
accountability for such practices, to provide certain minimum standards 
for consumer mortgage loans, and for other purposes, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
