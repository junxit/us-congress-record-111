# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 1728

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

       Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits were submitted as follows:

       The amendment to be offered by Representative Frank of 
     Massachusetts, or a designee, to H.R. 1728, the Mortgage 
     Reform and Anti-Predatory Lending Act, does not contain any 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of 
     Rule XXI.
```
