# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 21, 2009

**Speaking:** Reed, Jack (R000122) [D-RI]

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 21, 2009

  Mr. REED. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 12 noon 
tomorrow, Wednesday, January 21; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate proceed to executive session as under 
the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Nelson of Nebraska). Without objection, it 
is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
