# SENATOR TED KENNEDY

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                          SENATOR TED KENNEDY

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, we have just left the memorable inauguration 
of Barack Obama. But as President Obama said a few minutes ago, our 
minds are not on the events of today but for the fact that Senator 
Kennedy took ill during the lunch. Senator Kennedy is now in a 
Washington hospital.

[[Page S663]]

He and I sat together at the inauguration. We had such a wonderful time 
visiting about where we were and where we are.
  So I would ask all, within the sound of my voice, to pause for a 
brief moment with our thoughts and, for those who feel it appropriate, 
our prayers for Senator Kennedy and his lovely wife Vicki.
  (Moment of silence.)

                          ____________________
```
