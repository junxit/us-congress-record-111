# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 8, 2009, AT 2 P.M.

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 172

```
        ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 8, 2009, AT 2 P.M.

  THE ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order and 
pursuant to the provisions of H. Con. Res. 172, the Senate stands 
adjourned until 2 p.m., Tuesday, September 8, 2009.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 1 and 37 seconds p.m., adjourned until 
Tuesday, September 8, 2009, at 2 p.m.
```
