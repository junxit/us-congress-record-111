# ORDERS FOR SUNDAY, DECEMBER 13, 2009

```
                  ORDERS FOR SUNDAY, DECEMBER 13, 2009

  Mr. BEGICH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 1:30 p.m., 
Sunday, December 13; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of the conference report 
accompanying H.R. 3288, the consolidated appropriations bill, as 
provided for under the previous order; and that following any leader 
remarks, the time until 2 p.m. be equally divided and controlled 
between the two leaders or their designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
