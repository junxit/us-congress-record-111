# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER 9, 2009

```
          CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER 9, 2009

  Mr. CONNOLLY of Virginia. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the House adjourns today on a motion offered pursuant to this 
order, it adjourn to meet at 6 p.m. on Monday, November 9, 2009, unless 
it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting its 
concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 210, in which case the House 
shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Virginia?
  There was no objection.
```
