# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 9, S.J.Res. 12

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has agreed to a concurrent resolution and a joint 
resolution of the following titles in which the concurrence of the 
House is requested:

       S. Con. Res. 9. Concurrent resolution supporting the goals 
     and ideals of Multiple Sclerosis Awareness Week.
```
