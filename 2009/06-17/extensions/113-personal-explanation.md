# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** McHugh, John M. (M000472) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1256

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN M. McHUGH

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 17, 2009

  Mr. McHUGH. Madam Speaker, I was inadvertently recorded as having 
voted in the negative on H.R. 1256, House rollcall vote 335, on June 
12, 2009. I would like the record to show that I fully intended to vote 
``yea.'' I strongly support this measure and, indeed, voted for the 
legislation when the measure first came before the House for a vote on 
April 2, 2009.
```
