# PERMISSION TO REALLOCATE TIME FOR GENERAL DEBATE DURING CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4173

```
 PERMISSION TO REALLOCATE TIME FOR GENERAL DEBATE DURING CONSIDERATION 
                              OF H.R. 4173

  Mr. WAXMAN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during 
consideration of H.R. 4173 pursuant to H. Res. 956, the Chair of the 
Committee on Financial Services be permitted to control 10 minutes of 
the time allocated to the Ranking Member of the Committee on Energy and 
Commerce.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
  Mr. BARTON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during 
consideration of H.R. 4173 pursuant to H. Res. 956, the ranking member 
of the Committee on Financial Services be permitted to control 10 
minutes of the time allocated to the ranking member of the Committee on 
Energy and Commerce.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
