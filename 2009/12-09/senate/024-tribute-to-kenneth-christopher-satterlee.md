# TRIBUTE TO KENNETH CHRISTOPHER SATTERLEE

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                TRIBUTE TO KENNETH CHRISTOPHER SATTERLEE

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Kenneth 
Christopher Satterlee, an intern in my Washington DC, office, for all 
of the hard work he has done for me, my staff, and the State of South 
Dakota over the past several months.
  Kenny is a graduate of La Jolla High School in San Diego, CA. 
Currently he is attending the American University, where he is majoring 
in history. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the 
most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Kenny 
for all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
