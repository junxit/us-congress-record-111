# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Bingaman, Jeff (B000468) [D-NM]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a business meeting has 
been scheduled before Committee on Energy and Natural Resources. The 
business meeting will be held on Wednesday, May 6, 2009 at 10 a.m., in 
room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building.
  The purpose of the business meeting is to consider legislation on 
siting of interstate electric transmission facilities, energy finance, 
and nuclear energy.

or Amanda Kelly at (202) 224-6836.
```
