# ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, DECEMBER 12, 2009

```
                 ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, DECEMBER 12, 2009

  Mr. MENENDEZ. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9 a.m., Saturday, 
December 12; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of the conference report 
accompanying H.R. 3288, the consolidated appropriations bill, as 
provided for under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
