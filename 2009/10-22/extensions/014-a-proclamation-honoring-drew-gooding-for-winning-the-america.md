# A PROCLAMATION HONORING DREW GOODING FOR WINNING THE AMERICANISM ESSAY WRITING CONTEST

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING DREW GOODING FOR WINNING THE AMERICANISM ESSAY 
                            WRITING CONTEST

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2009

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker,
  Whereas, Drew Gooding won the Elks Grand Lodge essay writing contest, 
demonstrating a mastery of thought and word; and
  Whereas, he has shown to take interest in upholding the values that 
make us a free nation; and
  Whereas, Drew Gooding is honored for his patriotism and excellence in 
writing and; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I commend and thank Drew Gooding for 
his contributions to his community and country.

                          ____________________
```
