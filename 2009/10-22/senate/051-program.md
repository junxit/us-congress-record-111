# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. As previously announced, there will be no rollcall votes 
during Monday's session of the Senate. The next vote will occur at 2:30 
p.m. on Tuesday, October 27. That vote will be on the motion to proceed 
to H.R. 3548, the Unemployment Compensation Extension Act of 2009.
  Next week will be a busy week. We hope to complete action on the 
Unemployment Insurance Extension Act, Commerce-Justice-Science 
Appropriations, and Military Construction Appropriations. We also need 
to pass a continuing resolution before the end of the week.
  Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum call 
be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
