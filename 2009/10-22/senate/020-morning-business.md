# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to a period of morning business, with Senators permitted to 
speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; that during morning business, 
Senator Brown control up to 1 hour; and that during that time, he be 
permitted to enter into colloquies.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Whitehouse). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. LEVIN. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DORGAN. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum 
call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
