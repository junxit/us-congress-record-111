# PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY

**Speaking:** Terry, Lee (T000459) [R-NE]

```
                         PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY

  Mr. TERRY. Mr. Speaker, I have a parliamentary inquiry.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The gentleman may state his parliamentary 
inquiry.
  Mr. TERRY. Are non-Members of Congress allowed to vocalize a vote?
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Only Members of the House are allowed to 
vote in the House.
  Mr. TERRY. There were more than two ``ayes'' and there are only two 
Members on the House floor.

                          ____________________
```
