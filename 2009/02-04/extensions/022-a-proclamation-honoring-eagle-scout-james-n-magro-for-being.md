# A PROCLAMATION HONORING EAGLE SCOUT JAMES N. MAGRO FOR BEING NAMED THE FIRST DISTINGUISHED EAGLE SCOUT FROM THE UPPER OHIO VALLEY ON DECEMBER 4, 2008

**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING EAGLE SCOUT JAMES N. MAGRO FOR BEING NAMED THE 
FIRST DISTINGUISHED EAGLE SCOUT FROM THE UPPER OHIO VALLEY ON DECEMBER 
                                4, 2008

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 4, 2009

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, the Distinguished Eagle Award is one of the highest and most 
respected in Scouting; and
  Whereas, previous recipients include President Gerald Ford and 
Secretary of Defense Robert Gates; and
  Whereas, Mr. Magro was recognized for his professional 
accomplishments with Consol Energy as well as his community service 
with a number of organizations; and
  Whereas, Mr. Magro surely exemplifies the Scout oath of doing one's 
best in every aspect of his daily life; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, the Boy Scouts of 
America and the residents of the 18th Congressional District, I 
congratulate Jim Magro on being awarded the Distinguished Eagle Award. 
We recognize the tremendous resource he has been for the Scouts of St. 
Clairsville and commend the example he has provided for generations of 
Scouts to come.

                          ____________________
```
