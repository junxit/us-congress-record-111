# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2009

**Speaking:** Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2009

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 a.m. 
Thursday, February 5; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and that the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 1, the 
Economic Recovery and Reinvestment Act.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
