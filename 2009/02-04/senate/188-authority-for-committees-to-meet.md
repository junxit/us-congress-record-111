# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Inouye, Daniel K. (I000025) [D-HI]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


            committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs

  Mr. INOUYE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs be authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on February 4, 2009 at 3 p.m., to conduct a 
committee hearing on modernizing the U.S. financial regulatory system.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
