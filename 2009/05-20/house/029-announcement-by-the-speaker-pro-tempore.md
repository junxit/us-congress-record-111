# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 456, H.Res. 457

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 and clause 9 of rule 
XX, proceedings will resume on questions previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order: on adopting House 
Resolution 456, by the yeas and nays; on ordering the previous question 
on House Resolution 457, by the yeas and nays; on adopting House 
Resolution 457, if ordered.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Remaining electronic votes will be conducted as 5-minute votes.
```
