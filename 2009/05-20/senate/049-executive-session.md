# EXECUTIVE SESSION

```
                           EXECUTIVE SESSION

                                 ______
                                 

                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider Calendar Nos. 31 and 108; that 
the nominations be confirmed, en bloc; the motions to reconsider be 
laid upon the table, en bloc; that any statements relating to the 
nominations be printed in the Record; that no further motions be in 
order; that upon confirmation, the President be immediately notified of 
the Senate's action, and the Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nominations were considered and confirmed en bloc as follows:


                       department of the interior

       David J. Hayes, of Virginia, to be Deputy Secretary of the 
     Interior.


                          department of energy

       Ines R. Triay, of New Mexico, to be an Assistant Secretary 
     of Energy (Environmental Management).
```
