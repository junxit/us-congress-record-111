# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 24, 2009

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 24, 2009

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Friday, July 24; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later

[[Page 19091]]

in the day; that there then be a moment of silence in honor of the 
fallen Capitol Police officers, and that following the moment of 
silence, there be a period of morning business, with Senators permitted 
to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
