# Text of House Amendment 16

**Speaking:** Gingrey, Phil (G000550) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3293

```
H.R. 3293

                   Offered By: Mr. Gingrey of Georgia

       Amendment No. 16: At the end of the bill (before the short

     be used to implement subsections (a) and (c) of section 7131
```
