# Text of House Amendment 14

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 3293

```
H.R. 3293

                         Offered By: Mr. Carter

       Amendment No. 14: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. ___.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to promulgate, amend, or repeal any regulation 
     pursuant to the Federal Family Education Loan program under 
     part B of title IV of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 
     U.S.C. 1071 et seq.).
```
