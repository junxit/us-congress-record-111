     Calendar.
       Mr. FILNER: Committee on Veterans' Affairs. H.R. 3219. A

     Veterans Affairs relating to insurance and health care, and 
     for other purposes (Rept. 111-223). Referred to the Committee 
     of the Whole House on the State of the Union.
       Mr. FILNER: Committee on Veterans' Affairs. H.R. 3155. A

     State of the Union.
