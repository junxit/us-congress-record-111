# Text of House Amendment 11

**Speaking:** Burton, Dan (B001149) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3293

```
H.R. 3293

                   Offered By: Mr. Burton of Indiana

       Amendment No. 11: At the end of the bill (before the short
```
