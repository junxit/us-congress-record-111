# Text of House Amendment 17

**Measures:** H.R. 3293

```
H.R. 3293

                         Offered By: Mr. Graves

       Amendment No. 17: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to promulgate, issue, implement, administer, or 
     enforce any regulation that requires an owner of a small 
     business concern as defined in section 3 of the Small 
     Business Act (15 U.S.C. 632) to offer a health benefits plan 
     to an employee.
```
