# Text of House Amendment 15

**Speaking:** Gingrey, Phil (G000550) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3293

```
H.R. 3293

                   Offered By: Mr. Gingrey of Georgia

       Amendment No. 15: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to establish or implement any requirement that an 
     individual receive vaccination for human papillomavirus (HPV) 
     as a condition of school admittance or matriculation.
```
