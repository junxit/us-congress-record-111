# Text of House Amendment 12

**Speaking:** Camp, Dave (C000071) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 3293

```
H.R. 3293

                          Offered By: Mr. Camp

       Amendment No. 12: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by any entity receiving funds under this Act, other 
     than the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, to alter 
     Medicare reimbursement rates under part A or B of title XVIII 
     of the Social Security Act.
```
