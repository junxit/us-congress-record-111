Mathematics (STEM) Education Center on its Orland Park campus to better 
prepare educators and education students to teach STEM disciplines to 
pre-school through college-age students. The STEM Education Center will 
feature a state-of-the-art methods lab that will be used to provide 
exciting, hands-on science, math, and technology programs designed to 
increase teachers' knowledge and skills in these areas.
