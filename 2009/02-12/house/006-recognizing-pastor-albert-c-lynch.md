# RECOGNIZING PASTOR ALBERT C. LYNCH

```
                   RECOGNIZING PASTOR ALBERT C. LYNCH

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the gentleman from 
Virginia (Mr. Cantor) is recognized for 1 minute.
  There was no objection.
  Mr. CANTOR. Madam Speaker, today I have the distinct honor of 
inviting for the opening prayer a man for whom I have tremendous 
respect, Pastor Al Lynch. Pastor Lynch, who is here with his wife Susan 
and his son Matthew, serves as the St. Andrew's United Methodist Church 
in Henrico County, Virginia. He has a passion for public service, in 
particular in the public safety arena, where he has been appointed 
chaplain of the Henrico County Sheriff's Office. His years of 
contributions to the greater Richmond region have left a profound 
imprint on our community, and we are all grateful for his service.

                          ____________________
```
