# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2009

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2009

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., 
Friday, November 6; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 3082, the Military 
Construction and Veterans Affairs appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
