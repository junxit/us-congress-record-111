# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 3, 2009

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 3, 2009

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until tomorrow morning at 10 
a.m., March 3; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 1105, the Omnibus 
appropriations bill; further, that the Senate recess from 12:30 to 2:15 
p.m. for the weekly party conference lunches.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
