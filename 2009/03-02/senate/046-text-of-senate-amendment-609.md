# Text of Senate Amendment 609

**Speaking:** Coburn, Tom (C000560) [R-OK]

```
  SA 609. Mr. COBURN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 1105, making omnibus appropriations for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2009, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available under this Act 
     may be obligated or otherwise expended for any 
     congressionally directed spending item for any client of a 
     lobbying firm under Federal investigation, including the PMA 
     Group of Arlington, Virginia.
                                 ______
```
