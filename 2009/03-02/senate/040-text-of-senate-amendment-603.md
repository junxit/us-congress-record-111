# Text of Senate Amendment 603

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

```
  SA 603. Mr. SESSIONS submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill H.R. 1105, making omnibus appropriations for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 2009, and for other purposes; which 
was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 1121, line 5, strike ``143, 144,'' and insert 
     ``144''.
       On page 1121, between lines 10 and 11, insert the 
     following:
       Sec. 102.  Section 143 of division A of the Consolidated 
     Security, Disaster Assistance, and Continuing Appropriations 
     Act, 2009 (Public Law 110-329; 122 Stat. 3580) is amended by 
     striking ``shall'' and all that follows through the end and 
     inserting ``is amended by striking `Unless' and all that 
     follows through the end.''.
                                 ______
```
