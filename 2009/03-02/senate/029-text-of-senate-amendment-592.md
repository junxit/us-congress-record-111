# Text of Senate Amendment 592

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

```
  SA 592. Mr. McCAIN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 1105, making omnibus appropriations for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2009, and for other purposes; as follows:

       Strike all after the enacting clause and insert the 
     following:

     SECTION 1. CONTINUING 2008 FUNDING LEVELS.

       Section 106(3) of Public Law 110-329 is amended by striking 
     ``March 6, 2009'' and inserting ``September 30, 2009''.
```
