# YVONNE INGRAM-EPHRAIM POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 663

```
               YVONNE INGRAM-EPHRAIM POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 663) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 12877 Broad Street in Sparta, Georgia, as the 
``Yvonne Ingram-Ephraim Post Office Building'', was ordered to a third
```
