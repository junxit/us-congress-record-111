# SIGNING AUTHORITY

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. PRYOR. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the majority 
leader be permitted to sign any duly enrolled bills and joint 
resolutions during today's session.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. PRYOR. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
order for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
