# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to a period of morning business for up to 30 
minutes, with the time equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees, with the Republicans controlling the first 
half and the majority controlling the final half.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Tennessee is 
recognized.
  Mr. ALEXANDER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to speak for up 
to 15 minutes in morning business, and would the Chair please let me 
know when I have 2 minutes left.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered, and the Chair will do so.
```
