# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BEGICH. Mr. President, tomorrow, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the Fraud Enforcement Recovery Act, and rollcall votes 
are expected to occur throughout the day in relation to the pending 
amendments. Earlier today, the majority leader announced if the Senate 
is unable to complete action on the bill tomorrow, the Senate would 
remain in session through the weekend.
  In addition, the Senate will turn to the consideration of the House 
message to request a conference with respect to the budget resolution 
when it is available. Senators should expect rollcall votes in relation 
to motions to instruct the conferees during tomorrow's session.
```
