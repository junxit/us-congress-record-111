# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 23, 2009

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 23, 2009

  Mr. BEGICH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Thursday, April 23; that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 386.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
