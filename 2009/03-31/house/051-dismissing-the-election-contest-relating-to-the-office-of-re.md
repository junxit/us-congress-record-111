# DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE FIRST CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF HAWAII

```
       DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF 
     REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE FIRST CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF HAWAII

  Mr. BRADY of Pennsylvania, from the Committee on House 
Administration, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 111-68) on the 
resolution (H. Res. 303) dismissing the election contest relating to 
the office of Representative from the First Congressional District of 
Hawaii, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
  Mr. BRADY of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I call up House Resolution 
303 and ask unanimous consent for its immediate consideration in the 
House.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
  The text of the resolution is as follows:

                              H. Res. 303

       Resolved, That the election contest relating to the office 
     of Representative from the First Congressional District of 
     Hawaii is dismissed.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
