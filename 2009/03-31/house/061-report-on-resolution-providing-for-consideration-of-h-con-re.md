# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 85, CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2010

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 85, 
        CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2010

  Mr. Perlmutter, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 111-70) on the resolution (H. Res. 305) providing for 
consideration of the concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 85) setting 
forth the congressional budget for the United States Government for 
fiscal year 2010 and including the appropriate budgetary levels for 
fiscal years 2009 and 2011 through 2014, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
