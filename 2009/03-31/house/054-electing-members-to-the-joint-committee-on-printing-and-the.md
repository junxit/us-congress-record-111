# ELECTING MEMBERS TO THE JOINT COMMITTEE ON PRINTING AND THE JOINT COMMITTEE OF CONGRESS ON THE LIBRARY

**Speaking:** Brady, Robert A. (B001227) [D-PA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 304

```
   ELECTING MEMBERS TO THE JOINT COMMITTEE ON PRINTING AND THE JOINT 
                  COMMITTEE OF CONGRESS ON THE LIBRARY

  Mr. BRADY of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I send a resolution to the 
desk and ask unanimous consent for its immediate consideration in the 
House.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
  The text of the resolution is as follows:

                              H. Res. 304

       Resolved,

     SECTION 1. ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO JOINT COMMITTEE ON PRINTING 
                   AND JOINT COMMITTEE OF CONGRESS ON THE LIBRARY.

       (a) Joint Committee on Printing.--The following Members are 
     hereby elected to the

[[Page H4193]]

     Joint Committee on Printing, to serve with the chair of the 
     Committee on House Administration:
       (1) Mr. Capuano.
       (2) Mrs. Davis of California.
       (3) Mr. Daniel E. Lungren of California.
       (4) Mr. McCarthy of California.
       (b) Joint Committee of Congress on the Library.--The 
     following Members are hereby elected to the Joint Committee 
     of Congress on the Library, to serve with the chair of the 
     Committee on House Administration:
       (1) Ms. Zoe Lofgren of California.
       (2) Mr. Daniel E. Lungren of California.
       (3) Mr. Harper.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
