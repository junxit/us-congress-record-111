# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 1664

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

       The amendment to be offered by Representative Frank of 
     Massachusetts, or a designee, to H.R. 1664, to amend the 
     executive compensation provisions of the Emergency Economic 
     Stabilization Act of 2008 to prohibit unreasonable and 
     excessive compensation and compensation not based on 
     performance standards, does not contain any congressional 
     earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as 
     defined in clause 9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of rule XXI.
```
