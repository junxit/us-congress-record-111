# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Isakson, Johnny (I000055) [R-GA]

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Mr. ISAKSON. Mr. President, on March 12, 2009, I was attending the 
funeral of a very close friend and was unable to cast votes on rollcall 
vote No. 97 and rollcall No. 98. I ask that the Record reflect that had 
I been present I would have cast my vote as follows: rollcall vote No. 
97, confirmation David W. Ogden, of Virginia, to be Deputy Attorney 
General: NO; rollcall vote No. 98, confirmation Thomas John Perrelli, 
of Virginia, to be Associate Attorney General: NO.
```
