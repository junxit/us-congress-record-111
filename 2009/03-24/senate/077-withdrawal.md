# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on March 
24, 2009 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       STUART GORDON NASH, OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, TO BE AN 
     ASSOCIATE JUDGE OF THE SUPERIOR COURT OF THE DISTRICT OF 
     COLUMBIA FOR THE TERM OF FIFTEEN YEARS, VICE RUFUS GUNN KING, 
     III, RETIRED, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON JANUARY 8, 
     2009.
```
