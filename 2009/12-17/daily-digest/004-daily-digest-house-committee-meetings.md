# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
GOVERNMENT AS A SHAREHOLDER
Committee on Oversight and Government Reform: Subcommittee on Domestic 
Policy continued hearings entitled ``The U.S. Government as Dominant 
Shareholder: How Should the Taxpayers' Ownership Rights be Exercised?'' 
Testimony was heard from Herbert M. Allison, Jr., Assistant Secretary, 
Financial Stability, Department of the Treasury.

Joint Meetings
RUSSIA'S MUSLIM POPULATION
Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe. Commission concluded 
a hearing to examine Russia's Muslim population, after receiving 
testimony from Paul Globe, Institute of World Politics, and Shireen 
Hunter, Georgetown University School of Foreign Service, both of 
Washington, DC.
```
