# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3326

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, following leader remarks, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the motion to concur with respect to H.R. 3326, 
the Defense Appropriations Act. The first hour will be equally divided 
and controlled between the two leaders or their designees. The 
Republicans will control the first 30 minutes and the majority will 
control the next 30 minutes. I filed cloture on the motion to concur. 
That vote will occur sometime in the next 10 or 12 hours.
```
