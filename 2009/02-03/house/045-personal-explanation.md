# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Kilpatrick, Carolyn C. (K000180) [D-MI]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

was unable to attend to several votes today. Had I been present, I 
would have voted ``yea'' on final passage of H. Res. 82, Raising

January 2009 as National Stalking Awareness Month; ``yea'' on final 
passage of H. Res. 103, Supporting the goals and ideals of National 
Teen Dating Violence Awareness and Prevention Week; and ``yea'' on 
final passage of H.R. 559--Fair, Accurate, Secure, and Timely Redress 
Act.
```
