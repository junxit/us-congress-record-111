# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF THE SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 2, CHILDREN'S HEALTH INSURANCE PROGRAM REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2009

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF THE SENATE 
       AMENDMENT TO H.R. 2, CHILDREN'S HEALTH INSURANCE PROGRAM 
                      REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2009
```
