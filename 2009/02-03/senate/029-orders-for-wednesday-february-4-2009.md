# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 4, 2009

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 4, 2009

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 
10:30 a.m. tomorrow, Wednesday, February 4; that following the prayer 
and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 
1, the Economic Recovery and Reinvestment Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
