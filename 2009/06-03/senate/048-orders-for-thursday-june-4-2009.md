# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 4, 2009

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 4, 2009

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., 
tomorrow, Thursday, June 4; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and there be a period of morning business for 1 hour, with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each with the time 
equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the Republicans controlling the first half and the 
majority controlling the second half; further, I ask following morning 
business the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 1256, the Family 
Smoking Prevention and Tobacco Control Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
