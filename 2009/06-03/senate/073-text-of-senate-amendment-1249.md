# Text of Senate Amendment 1249

**Speaking:** Hagan, Kay R. (H001049) [D-NC]

```
  SA 1249. Mrs. HAGAN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
her to the bill H.R. 1256, to protect the public health by providing 
the Food and Drug Administration with certain authority to regulate 
tobacco products, to amend title 5, United States Code, to make certain 
modifications in the Thrift Savings Plan, the Civil Service Retirement 
System, and the Federal Employees' Retirement System, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       In section 907(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic 
     Act (as added by section 101), insert after paragraph (4) the 
     following:
       ``(5) Technological feasability.--A tobacco product 
     standard adopted under this section shall be based on a 
     finding by the Secretary that technology is available to 
     achieve the reductions required by such standard.''.
                                 ______
```
