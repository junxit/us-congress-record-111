# SUPPORTING THE GOALS AND IDEALS OF AMERICAN HEART MONTH AND NATIONAL WEAR RED DAY

**Measures:** H.Res. 112

```
 SUPPORTING THE GOALS AND IDEALS OF AMERICAN HEART MONTH AND NATIONAL 
                              WEAR RED DAY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and agreeing to the resolution, H. Res. 112.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Massachusetts (Mr. Lynch) that the House suspend the 
rules and agree to the resolution, H. Res. 112.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
