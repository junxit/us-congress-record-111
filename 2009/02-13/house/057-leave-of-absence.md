# LEAVE OF ABSENCE

```
                            LEAVE OF ABSENCE

  By unanimous consent, leave of absence was granted to:
  Mr. Lee of New York (at the request of Mr. Boehner) for today on 
account of helping to coordinate the Federal response and to provide 
assistance to the families of the victims of the tragic crash of 
Continental Airlines Flight 3407 in his district.
  Mr. Clyburn (at the request of Mr. Hoyer) for today after 1:30 p.m. 
on account of his daughter's wedding in South Carolina.
```
