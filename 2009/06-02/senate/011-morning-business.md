# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business until 11 
a.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with 
the time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees.
```
