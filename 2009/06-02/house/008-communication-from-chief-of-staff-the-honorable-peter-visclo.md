# COMMUNICATION FROM CHIEF OF STAFF, THE HONORABLE PETER VISCLOSKY, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
   COMMUNICATION FROM CHIEF OF STAFF, THE HONORABLE PETER VISCLOSKY, 
                           MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Charles E. Brimmer, Chief of Staff, the Honorable 
Peter Visclosky, Member of Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                     Washington, DC, June 1, 2009.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I have been served with a grand jury 
     subpoena for documents issued by the U.S. District Court for 
     the District of Columbia.
       After consultation with counsel, I will make the 
     determinations required by Rule VIII.
           Sincerely,
                                               Charles E. Brimmer,
     Chief of Staff.

                          ____________________
```
