```
  COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE PETER VISCLOSKY, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

communication from the Honorable Peter Visclosky, Member of Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                     Washington, DC, June 1, 2009.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
      Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the

     Columbia.

     Member of Congress.
```
