# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE PETER VISCLOSKY, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
  COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE PETER VISCLOSKY, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Peter Visclosky, Member of Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                     Washington, DC, June 1, 2009.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
      Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that my office has been served with two 
     grand jury subpoenas for documents issued by the U.S. 
     District Court for the District of Columbia.
       After consultation with counsel, I will make the 
     determination required by Rule VIII.
           Sincerely,
                                               Peter J. Visclosky,
     Member of Congress.

                          ____________________
```
