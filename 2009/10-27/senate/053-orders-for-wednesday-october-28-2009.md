# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 28, 2009

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 28, 2009

  Mrs. SHAHEEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Wednesday, October 28; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate then proceed to a period for the 
transaction of morning business for 2 hours, with Senators permitted to 
speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally divided 
and controlled between the two leaders or their designees, with the 
majority controlling the first half and the Republicans controlling the 
final half; that following morning business, the Senate resume 
consideration of the motion to proceed to H.R. 3548, the Unemployment 
Compensation Extension Act of 2009, postcloture; and that time during 
any period of morning business, recess or adjournment count 
postcloture.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
