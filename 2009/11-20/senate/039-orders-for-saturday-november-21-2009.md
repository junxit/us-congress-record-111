# ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 21, 2009

```
                 ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 21, 2009

  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:45 a.m., 
tomorrow, Saturday, November 21; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the motion to 
proceed to H.R. 3590, with debate as provided for under the previous 
order. Finally, I ask that the Republicans control the time from 8 p.m. 
until 9:30 p.m. tonight.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
