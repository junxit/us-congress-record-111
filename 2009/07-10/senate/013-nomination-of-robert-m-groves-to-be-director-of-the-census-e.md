# NOMINATION OF ROBERT M. GROVES TO BE DIRECTOR OF THE CENSUS (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
      NOMINATION OF ROBERT M. GROVES TO BE DIRECTOR OF THE CENSUS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider Calendar No. 169, the 
nomination of Robert M. Groves to be the Director of the Census for our 
country.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report.
  The bill clerk read the nomination of Robert M. Groves, of Michigan, 
to be Director of the Census.
```
