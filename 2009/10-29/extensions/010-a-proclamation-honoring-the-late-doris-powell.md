# A PROCLAMATION HONORING THE LATE DORIS POWELL

```
             A PROCLAMATION HONORING THE LATE DORIS POWELL

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2009

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker,
   Whereas, Doris Powell served honorably in the United States Armed 
Services; and
   Whereas, Doris Powell bravely served on active duty during World War 
II; and
   Whereas, she was a lifelong member of the Veterans of Foreign War 
(VFW) Post #4713; and
   Whereas, she served on the Morgan County School Board for 22 years; 
be it
   Resolved that along with friends, family, and the residents of the 
18th Congressional District, I congratulate the late Doris Powell on 
her induction to the Ohio Veterans Hall of Fame.

                          ____________________
```
