# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Conyers, John, Jr. (C000714) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.Res. 299, H.Res. 338, H.Res. 353

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. CONYERS. Mr. Speaker, due to events in my congressional district, 
I was unable to vote today. If I were present, I would have voted in 
favor of the following bills: H. Res. 299, expressing the sense of the 
House of Representatives that public servants should be commended for 
their dedication and continued service to the Nation during Public 
Service Recognition Week, May 4 through 10, 2009; H. Res. 338, 
supporting the goals and ideals of National Community College Month; H. 
Res. 353, supporting the goals and ideals of Global Youth Service Days.
```
