            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were

     considered and agreed to.

       S. Res. 130. A resolution to constitute the majority 
     party's membership on certain committees for the One Hundred 
     Eleventh Congress, or until their successors are chosen; 
     considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. McCONNELL:
       S. Res. 131. A resolution making minority party 
     appointments for certain committees for the 111th Congress; 
     considered and agreed to.
