# MAJORITY PARTY APPOINTMENT

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Res. 18

```
                       MAJORITY PARTY APPOINTMENT

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, under S. Res. 18, I have the authority to 
make a majority party appointment to the HELP Committee. I now ask 
unanimous consent that the appointment be made on a temporary basis and 
that I still retain the authority to make a permanent appointment in 
the 111th Congress.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. I now temporarily appoint Sheldon Whitehouse of Rhode 
Island.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Record will so note.

                          ____________________
```
