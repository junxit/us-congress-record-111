# REPORT DETAILING PLAN TO EXERCISE AUTHORITY UNDER EMERGENCY ECONOMIC STABILIZATION ACT OF 2008--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES (H. DOC. NO. 111-5)

```
 REPORT DETAILING PLAN TO EXERCISE AUTHORITY UNDER EMERGENCY ECONOMIC 
  STABILIZATION ACT OF 2008--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED 
                       STATES (H. DOC. NO. 111-5)

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on Financial Services and ordered to be printed:

To the Congress of the United States:
  Consistent with section 115(a)(3) of the Emergency Economic 
Stabilization Act of 2008 (Public Law 110-343) (the ``Act''), I hereby 
transmit a report detailing the plan of the Secretary of the Treasury 
to exercise the authority under the Act.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, January 12, 2009.

                          ____________________
```
