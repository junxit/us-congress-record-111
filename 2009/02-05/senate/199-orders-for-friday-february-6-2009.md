# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 6, 2009

**Speaking:** Casey, Robert P., Jr. (C001070) [D-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 6, 2009

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
Friday, February 6; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 1, the Economic 
Recovery and Reinvestment Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
