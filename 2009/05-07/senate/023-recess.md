# RECESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I now ask unanimous consent that the

have a number of things, and it would be better if we are not in 
session. I appreciate everyone allowing this consent to go forward.

1:45 p.m. and reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer
```
