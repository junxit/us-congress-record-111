# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to a period of morning business until 10:30 a.m., 
with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the majority controlling the first half and the 
Republicans controlling the second half.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Nebraska is 
recognized.

                          ____________________
```
