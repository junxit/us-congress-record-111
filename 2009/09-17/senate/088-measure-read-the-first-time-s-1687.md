# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1687

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1687

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1687

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at the 
desk. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.

       A bill (S. 1687) to prohibit the Federal Government from 
     awarding contracts, grants, or other agreements, or providing 
     other Federal funds to or engaging in activities that promote 
     the Association of Community Organizations for Reform Now.

  Mr. REID. I ask for a second reading but object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
the second time the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
