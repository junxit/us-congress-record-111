# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period of morning business for 2 hours with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the time 
equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the Republicans controlling the first half and the 
majority controlling the final half.
  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, I had propounded a unanimous consent request. 
Has that been agreed to?
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. It has been.
  Mr. KYL. Thank you, Mr. President.

                          ____________________
```
