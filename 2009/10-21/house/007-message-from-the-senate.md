# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 1818

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a bill of the following title in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 1818. An act to amend the Morris K. Udall Scholarship 
     and Excellence in National Environmental and Native American 
     Public Policy Act of 1992 to honor the legacy of Stewart L. 
     Udall, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 110-315, the 
Chair announces, on behalf of the President pro tempore, the 
appointment of the following individuals to be members of the National 
Advisory Committee on Institutional Quality and Integrity:
  Daniel Klaich of Nevada,
  Cameron Staples of Connecticut, and
  Larry Vanderhoef of California.

                          ____________________
```
