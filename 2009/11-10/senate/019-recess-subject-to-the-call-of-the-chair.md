# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate be 
in recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 7:46 p.m., recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 7:57 p.m. when called to 
order by the Presiding Officer (Ms. Landrieu).

                          ____________________
```
