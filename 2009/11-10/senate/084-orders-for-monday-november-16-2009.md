# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, NOVEMBER 16, 2009

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 210, H.R. 3082

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, NOVEMBER 16, 2009

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn under the provisions of 
H. Con. Res. 210 until 2 p.m., Monday, November 16; that following the 
prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate proceed to a 
period for the transaction of morning business until 3 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each; that following 
morning business, the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 3082, the 
Military Construction and Veterans Affairs appropriations bill, as 
provided for under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
