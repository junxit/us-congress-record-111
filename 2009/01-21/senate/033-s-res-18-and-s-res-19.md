# S. RES. 18 AND S. RES. 19

```
                       S. RES. 18 AND S. RES. 19

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration en bloc of S. Res. 18 and S. 
Res. 19, submitted earlier today; that the resolutions be agreed to, 
and the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table en bloc. They have 
been approved by the Republican leader.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolutions (S. Res. 18 and S. Res. 19) were agreed to, as 
follows:
```
