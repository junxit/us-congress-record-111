# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period of morning business for up to 1 hour, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
time to be equally divided and controlled between the two leaders, or 
their designees, with the Republicans controlling the first half and 
the majority controlling the second half.
  The Senator from Oklahoma is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
