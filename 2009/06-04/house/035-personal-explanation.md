# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Capuano, Michael E. (C001037) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.R. 626

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

my district for family reasons, I was unable to take rollcall votes

rollcall vote 308; ``no'' on rollcall vote 309; and ``aye'' on rollcall

Paid Parental Leave Act of 2009.
```
