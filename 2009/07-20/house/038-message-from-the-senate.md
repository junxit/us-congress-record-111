# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate notifies the House of Representatives that the Senate 
shall convene as a Court of Impeachment at 2 p.m., on Wednesday, July 
22, 2009, for the purpose of receiving the Managers on the part of the 
House of Representatives in the matter of the Impeachment proceedings 
against Samuel B. Kent, formerly a Judge of the United States District 
Court for the Southern District of Texas.

                          ____________________
```
