# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 586, H.R. 1626

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House reports that on April 30, 2009 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills.

       H.R. 1626. To make technical amendments to laws containing 
     time periods affecting judicial proceedings.
       H.R. 586. To direct the Librarian of Congress and the 
     Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution to carry out a joint 
     project at the Library of Congress and the National Museum of 
     African American History and Culture to collect video and 
     audio recordings of personal histories and testimonials of 
     individuals who participated in the Civil Rights movement, 
     and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
