# BIRTHDAY GREETINGS TO MALINDA WRIGHT

**Speaking:** Paul, Ron (P000583) [R-TX]

```
                  BIRTHDAY GREETINGS TO MALINDA WRIGHT

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. RON PAUL

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 4, 2009

  Mr. PAUL. Madam Speaker, Malinda Smith Wright will turn 100 on May 
17, 2009. Malinda was born and raised in Brazoria County, TX, which is 
in my congressional district, and has spent all of her life there.
  Malinda was married to Alex C. Wright for over seventy years. 
Together, Malinda and Alex raised six children. A lifelong lover of 
reading, Malinda continues to read the newspaper every day, and I 
understand that she is particularly interested in the stock market.
  Madam Speaker, I am pleased to take this opportunity to extend my 
congratulations and best wishes to Malinda Wright as she prepares to 
celebrate her 100th birthday with her friends and family.

                          ____________________
```
