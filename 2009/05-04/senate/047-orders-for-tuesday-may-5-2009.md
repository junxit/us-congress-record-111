# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 5, 2009

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 896

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 5, 2009

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on 
Tuesday, May 5; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and that the Senate resume consideration of S. 896, the Helping 
Families Save Their Homes Act of 2009; further, I ask unanimous consent 
that the Senate recess from 12:30 until 2:15 to allow for the weekly 
caucus luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
