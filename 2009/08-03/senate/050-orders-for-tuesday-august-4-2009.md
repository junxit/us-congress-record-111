# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, AUGUST 4, 2009

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, AUGUST 4, 2009

  Mr. DODD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. tomorrow, 
Tuesday, August 4; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of Calendar No. 105, H.R. 
2997, the Agriculture appropriations bill, with the time until 10:30 
equally divided and controlled between the managers and Senator McCain 
or their designees; further, I ask that the filing deadline for second-
degree amendments be 10:15 a.m. tomorrow.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
