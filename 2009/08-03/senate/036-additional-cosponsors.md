                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 252

  At the request of Mr. Reid, the name of the Senator from West 
Virginia (Mr. Byrd) was added as a cosponsor of S. 252, a bill to amend 
title 38, United States Code, to enhance the capacity of the Department

health-care professionals, to improve the provision of health care to 
veterans, and for other purposes.
