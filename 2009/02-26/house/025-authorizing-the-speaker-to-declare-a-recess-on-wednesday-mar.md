# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, MARCH 4, 2009, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING THE RIGHT HONORABLE GORDON BROWN, PRIME MINISTER OF THE UNITED KINGDOM OF...

**Speaking:** Wasserman Schultz, Debbie (W000797) [D-FL]

```
  AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, MARCH 4, 
2009, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING THE RIGHT HONORABLE 
GORDON BROWN, PRIME MINISTER OF THE UNITED KINGDOM OF GREAT BRITAIN AND 
                            NORTHERN IRELAND

  Ms. WASSERMAN SCHULTZ. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it 
may be in order at any time on Wednesday, March 4, 2009, for the 
Speaker to declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the 
purpose of receiving in joint meeting the Right Honorable Gordon Brown, 
Prime Minister of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern 
Ireland.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Florida?
  There was no objection.
```
