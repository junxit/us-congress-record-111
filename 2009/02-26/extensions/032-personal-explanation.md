# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Larson, John B. (L000557) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.Res. 184, H.R. 1105

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN B. LARSON

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, February 26, 2009

  Mr. LARSON of Connecticut. Madam Speaker, on February 25, 2009, I 
missed two votes regarding H. Res. 184, a resolution setting forth the 
rule for consideration of H.R. 1105 to consider making omnibus 
appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2009. Had I 
been present, I would have voted ``yes'' for both H. Res. 184, Ordering 
the previous question (Roll call vote 84), and H. Res. 184, On Agreeing 
to the Resolution (Roll call vote 85).
```
