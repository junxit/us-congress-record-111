# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 2:02 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that pursuant to 
section 2(a) of the National Cultural Center Act (20 U.S.C. 76h(a)), 
amended by Public Law 107-117, and the order of the House of January 6, 
2009, the Speaker appoints the following members of the House of 
Representatives to the Board of Trustees of the John F. Kennedy Center 
for the Performing Arts: Mr. Kennedy of Rhode Island, Ms. DeLauro of 
Connecticut, Mr. Blunt of Missouri.
```
