# Text of Senate Amendment 22

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
  SA 22. Mr. REID (for Mr. Nelson, of Florida) proposed an amendment to 
the resolution S. Res. 13, congratulating the University of Florida 
football team for winning the 2008 Bowl Championship Series (BCS) 
national championship; as follows:

       On page 3, strike lines 11 through 18 and insert the 
     following:
       (A) President of the University of Florida, J. Bernard 
     Machen;
       (B) Athletic Director of the University of Florida, Jeremy 
     N. Foley; and
       (C) Head Coach of the University of Florida football team, 
     Urban Meyer.

                          ____________________
```
