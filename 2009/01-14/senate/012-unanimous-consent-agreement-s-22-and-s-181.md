# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 22 AND S. 181

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 22, S. 181

```
             UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 22 AND S. 181

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 12 noon 
tomorrow, Thursday, January 15, all postcloture time be considered 
yielded back except for 10 minutes to be equally divided and controlled 
between Senators Bingaman and Coburn or their designees; that upon the 
use or yielding back of that time, the pending amendments be withdrawn, 
that the managers' amendments which have been cleared by the leaders 
and managers be in order, and that if cleared, the amendments be 
considered and agreed to; that the bill, as amended, be read a third 
time and the Senate proceed to vote on passage of the bill; that upon 
passage, the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table; and the 
Senate then vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the motion to 
proceed to S. 181.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
