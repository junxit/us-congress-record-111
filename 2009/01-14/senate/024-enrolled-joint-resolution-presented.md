# ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

```
                  ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, January 14, 2009, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled joint resolution:

       S.J. Res. 3. Joint resolution ensuring that the 
     compensation and other emoluments attached to the office of 
     Secretary of the Interior are those which were in effect on 
     January 1, 2005.
```
