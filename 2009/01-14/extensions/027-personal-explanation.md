# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. STEVE KAGEN

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 14, 2009

  Mr. KAGEN. Madam Speaker, I regret that I was unable to participate 
in four votes on the floor of the House of Representatives on January 
9, 2009, as I was recovering from knee surgery.
  The first vote was rollcall vote No. 7, a motion to recommit H.R. 12, 
the Paycheck Fairness Act. Had I been present, I would have voted 
``no.''
  The second vote was rollcall vote No. 8, on final passage of H.R. 12. 
Had I been present, I would have voted ``yes.''
  The third vote was rollcall vote No. 9, on final passage of H.R. 11, 
the Lily Ledbetter Fair Pay Act. Had I been present I would have voted 
``yes.''

                          ____________________
```
