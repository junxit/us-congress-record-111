# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 384, TARP REFORM AND ACCOUNTABILITY ACT OF 2009

**Measures:** H.Res. 62, H.R. 384

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 384, 
               TARP REFORM AND ACCOUNTABILITY ACT OF 2009

  Mr. Perlmutter, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 111-3) on the resolution (H. Res. 62) providing for 
further consideration of the bill (H.R. 384) to reform the Troubled 
Assets Relief Program of the Secretary of the Treasury and ensure 
accountability under such Program, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
