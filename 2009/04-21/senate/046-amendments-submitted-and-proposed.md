# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

```
                    AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 981. Mr. THUNE submitted an amendment intended to be 
     proposed by him to the bill S. 371, to amend chapter 44 of 
     title 18, United States Code, to allow citizens who have 
     concealed carry permits from the State in which they reside 
     to carry concealed firearms in another State that grants 
     concealed carry permits, if the individual complies with the 
     laws of the State; which was referred to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
```
