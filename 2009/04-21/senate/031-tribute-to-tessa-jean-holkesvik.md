# TRIBUTE TO TESSA JEAN HOLKESVIK

```
                    TRIBUTE TO TESSA JEAN HOLKESVIK

● Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I wish to recognize Tessa 
Jean Holkesvik, an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the 
hard work she has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota 
over the past several months.
  Tessa Jean Holkesvik is a graduate of Central High School in 
Aberdeen, SD. Currently she is attending George Washington University, 
where she is majoring in political science. She is a hard worker who 
has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Tessa for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●
```
