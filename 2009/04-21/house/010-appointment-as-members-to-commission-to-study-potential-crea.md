# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COMMISSION TO STUDY POTENTIAL CREATION OF A NATIONAL MUSEUM OF THE AMERICAN LATINO

```
 APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COMMISSION TO STUDY POTENTIAL CREATION OF A 
                 NATIONAL MUSEUM OF THE AMERICAN LATINO

  Pursuant to section 333(a)(2) of the Consolidated Natural Resources 
Act of 2008 (P.L. 110-229), and the order of the House of January 6, 
2009, the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of the following 
members on the part of the House to the Commission to Study the 
Potential Creation of a National Museum of the American Latino:
  as voting members:
  Mr. Luis Cancel, San Francisco, CA
  Ms. Eva Longoria Parker, San Antonio, TX
  Mr. Henry Munoz, San Antonio, TX
  as a nonvoting member:
  Ms. Lorraine Garcia-Nakata, San Francisco, CA
```
