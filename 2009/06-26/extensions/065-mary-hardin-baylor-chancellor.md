# MARY HARDIN-BAYLOR CHANCELLOR

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
                     MARY HARDIN-BAYLOR CHANCELLOR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER-

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2009

  Mr. CARTER. Madam Speaker, I would like to congratulate to Dr. Jerry 
G. Bawcom, President at the University of Mary Hardin-Baylor, in Belton 
Texas, as Dr. Bawcom transitions into the office of the Chancellor. As 
the 21st President of UMHB, he has served as President for 18 years and 
will become Chancellor on June 1, 2009. I would like to thank Dr. 
Bawcom for his leadership and service as well as congratulate him and 
wish him well in his new position as Chancellor.

                          ____________________
```
