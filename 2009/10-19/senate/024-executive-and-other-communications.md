       EC-3420. A communication from the Director of the

     No. 8959-7) received in the Office of the President of the 
     Senate on October 15, 2009; to the Committee on Environment 
     and Public Works.
       EC-3421. A communication from the Secretary of the 
     Department of Health and Human Services, transmitting the 
     report of proposed legislation relative to Multilateral Child 
     Support Convention Implementation; to the Committee on 
     Finance.
       EC-3422. A communication from the Assistant Secretary,
