# CELEBRATING SAN YSIDRO'S CENTENNIAL YEAR!

```
               CELEBRATING SAN YSIDRO'S CENTENNIAL YEAR!

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BOB FILNER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 9, 2009

  Mr. FILNER. Madam Speaker and colleagues, I rise today to commemorate 
the Centennial of the great community of San Ysidro--the gateway to 
America. San Ysidro is not only home to the world's busiest land border 
crossing, but is also a multicultural tight-knit community with a rich 
history and culture like no other. As California's Border Congressman, 
I am very proud to represent San Ysidro and will continue to be a vocal 
advocate for our border community. Please join me in this year-long 
celebration. ``<up-sd-dn !>Adelante San Ysidro!''

                          ____________________
```
