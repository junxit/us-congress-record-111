Joint Meetings
MOLDOVA ELECTIONS
Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe: Commission received a 
briefing to examine Moldova's recent parliamentary elections and their 
implications for United States-Moldova relations and regional policy, 
after receiving testimony from Andrei Galbur, Embassy of Moldova, 
Washington, D.C.; Valentina Cusnir, former Member of Moldova's 
Parliament, and Nadine Gogu, Independent Journalism Center, both of 
Chisinau, Moldova; and Louis O'Neil, former Head of the OSCE Mission to 
Moldova, New York, New York.
