# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. PRYOR. Madam President, Senators should expect at least two 
rollcall votes tomorrow to begin at approximately 3:15 p.m. The first 
two votes will be in relation to the Lincoln amendment No. 2905 
regarding executive compensation, to be followed by a vote in relation 
to the Ensign amendment No. 2927 regarding attorneys fees. We are also 
working on the next amendments that will be offered, and we are hopeful 
we will be able to vote on those tomorrow after the 3:15 p.m. votes. 
Senators will be notified when any additional votes are scheduled.
```
