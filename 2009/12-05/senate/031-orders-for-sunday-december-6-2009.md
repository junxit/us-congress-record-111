# ORDERS FOR SUNDAY, DECEMBER 6, 2009

**Speaking:** Pryor, Mark L. (P000590) [D-AR]

**Measures:** H.R. 3590

```
                  ORDERS FOR SUNDAY, DECEMBER 6, 2009

  Mr. PRYOR. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 12:30 p.m., 
Sunday, December 6; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 3590, the 
health care reform legislation, as provided for under the previous 
order, with the majority controlling the first 60 minutes and the 
Republicans controlling the next 60 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
