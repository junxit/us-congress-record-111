# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 1 of Rule XXII,

       70. The SPEAKER presented a petition of North Carolina 
     State Council of the Junior Order United American Mechanics, 
     relative to Resolution 8 urging the President and Congress to 
     act in every possible manner to defend the freedoms that have 
     been promised to us by the United States Constitution; which 
     was referred to the Committee on Armed Services.
```
