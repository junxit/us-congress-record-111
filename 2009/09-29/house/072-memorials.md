# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 7, S.Res. 69, S.Res. 70

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 4 of Rule XXII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       191. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of the 
     State of California, relative to Senate Joint Resolution No. 
     7 urging the President and the Congress of the United States 
     to expand federally funded research efforts aimed at 
     developing a reliable means of detecting pancreatic cancer in 
     its early stages and more effective means of treatment 
     through legislative measures; to the Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce.
       192. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Michigan, relative to Senate Resolution No. 69 urging the 
     Congress of the United States to enact legislation to waive 
     for two years the requirement that Michigan match federal 
     Highway funds; to the Committee on Transportation and 
     Infrastructure.
       193. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Michigan, relative to Senate Resolution No. 70 urging the 
     President and Congress to grant Michigan a two-year waiver 
     from federal matching requirements for federal-aid bridge and 
     highway projects; to the Committee on Transportation and 
     Infrastructure.

                          ____________________
```
