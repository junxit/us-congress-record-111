# NOTICES OF HEARINGS-

**Speaking:** Bingaman, Jeff (B000468) [D-NM]

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS-


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a hearing previously 
announced for Thursday, October 1, 2009, at 9:45 p.m., is postponed 
until a later date.
  The purpose of the hearing was to receive testimony on Energy and 
Related Economic Effects of Global Climate Change Legislation.
  For further information, please contact Jonathan Black at (202) 224-
6722 or Gina Weinstock at (202) 224-5684.


                subcommittee on public lands and forests

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a hearing previously 
announced before the Subcommittee on Public Lands and Forests on 
Thursday, October 1, 2009, at 2:30 p.m., is postponed until a later 
date.
  The purpose of the hearing was to receive testimony on managing 
Federal forests in response to climate change, including for natural 
resource adaptation and carbon sequestration.
  For further information, please contact Scott Miller at (202) 224-
5488 or Allison Seyferth at (202) 224-4905.

                          ____________________
```
