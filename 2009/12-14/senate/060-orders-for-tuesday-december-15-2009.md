# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 15, 2009

**Speaking:** Klobuchar, Amy (K000367) [D-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3590

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 15, 2009

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., Tuesday, 
December 15; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 3590, the health care 
reform legislation, as provided for under the previous order.
  Finally, I ask the Senate recess from 12:45 p.m. until 3:15 p.m. to 
allow for the weekly caucus luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
