# BEAVER CREEK RESERVOIR PROJECT

```
                     BEAVER CREEK RESERVOIR PROJECT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. GLENN THOMPSON

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 15, 2009

  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, pursuant to the 
Republican standards on earmarks, I am submitting the following 
information regarding earmarks I received as a part of H.R. 3183, the 
Energy and Water Development and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 
2010. The entity to receive funding is Clarion County Commissioners, 
421 Main Street, Clarion, PA 16214, in the amount of $100,000. Funding 
will be used to provide a major water source, recreation, aquatic, 
avian, ecological, and environmental education endeavors with the 
inclusion of a major water supply source (1.3 million gallons/day). 
Enhancements to the Clarion County Commerce Center (KOZ Zone) would be 
afforded a water supply for industrial growth in the western section of 
the county.
```
