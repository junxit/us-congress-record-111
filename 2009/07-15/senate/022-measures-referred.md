# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 402, H.R. 1037

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 402. An act to designate the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs Outpatient Clinic in Knoxville, Tennessee, as the 
     ``William C. Tallent Department of Veterans Affairs 
     Outpatient Clini''; to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.

     Affairs to conduct a five-year pilot project to test the 
     feasibility and advisability of expanding the scope of 
     certain qualifying work-study activities under title 38, 
     United States Code; to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
```
