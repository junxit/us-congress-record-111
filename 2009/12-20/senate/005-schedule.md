# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, following leader remarks, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the health care legislation, with the time 
until 1:30 p.m. equally divided and controlled between the two leaders 
or their designees. Beginning at 1:30 p.m., and until 11:30 p.m. 
tonight, the time will be controlled in alternating hours, with the 
Republicans controlling the first hour.
  At 11:30 p.m., the Senate will recess until 12:01 a.m., with the time 
until 1 a.m. equally divided and controlled between the leaders or 
their designees, with the majority leader controlling the final 10 
minutes and the Republican leader controlling the 10 minutes prior to 
that.
  At 1 a.m., tomorrow, the Senate will vote on the motion to invoke 
cloture on the managers' amendment to the health care bill.
  Madam President, the time I have until 1:30 p.m., I designate to the 
majority whip, the senior Senator from Illinois.

                          ____________________
```
