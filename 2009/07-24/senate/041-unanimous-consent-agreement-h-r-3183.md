# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 3183

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1436, H.R. 3183

```
                 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 3183

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 3 p.m. 
Monday, July 27, the Senate proceed to the consideration of calendar 
No. 116, H.R. 3183, Energy and Water Appropriations; that immediately 
after the bill is reported, Senator Dorgan be recognized to offer a 
substitute amendment, the text of which is S. 1436 as reported by the 
committee.
```
