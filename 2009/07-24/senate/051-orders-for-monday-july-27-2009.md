# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 27, 2009

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3183

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 27, 2009

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m. on Monday, July 
27; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and there be a 
period of morning business until 3 p.m. with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each; further, that following morning 
business, the Senate proceed to the consideration of Calendar No. 116, 
H.R. 3183, the Energy and Water Appropriations Act for Fiscal Year 
2010.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
