Courthouse, Alboin IL 62806.

County.
  The Account: Transportation & Community & System Preservation--
Harrisburg Missouri Street Hospital Access Project.
  Requesting Entity: City of Harrisburg at 110 East Locust Harrisburg, 
IL 62946.
  The funding will go towards construction of a secondary access route 
to Harrisburg Medical Center that was inaccessible during floods of 
2008.
