# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 3, 2009

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 3, 2009

  Mr. KAUFMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 10 
a.m. Tuesday, February 3; that following the prayer and the pledge the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 1, the 
Economic Recovery and Reinvestment Act; further, that the Senate stand 
in recess from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly caucus 
luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
