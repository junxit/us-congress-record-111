# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

```
                      HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

  Mr. HOYER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at noon tomorrow; and, further, when 
the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 12:30 p.m. on 
Monday next for morning-hour debate.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Lujan). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Maryland?
  There was no objection.
```
