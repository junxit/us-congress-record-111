# PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING PROCEEDINGS TODAY

**Speaking:** Polis, Jared (P000598) [D-CO]

```
  PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING PROCEEDINGS 
                                 TODAY

  Mr. POLIS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during 
proceedings today in the House and in the Committee of the Whole, the 
Chair be authorized to reduce to 2 minutes the minimum time for 
electronic voting on any questions that otherwise could be subjected to 
5-minute voting under clause 8 or 9 of rule XX or under clause 6 of 
rule XVIII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Colorado?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
