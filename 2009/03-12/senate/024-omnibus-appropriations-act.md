# OMNIBUS APPROPRIATIONS ACT

```
                       OMNIBUS APPROPRIATIONS ACT

  Mr. INOUYE. Mr. President, last week when considering H.R. 1105, the 
Omnibus Appropriations Act, 2009, I filed technical corrections to the 
table of congressionally directed spending items contained in the 
explanatory statement offered by the chairman of the Committee on 
Appropriations of the House of Representatives which accompanies the 
bill H.R. 1105.
  I wish to add the following technical correction to the joint 
explanatory statement that accompanied H.R. 1105:
  On page 5144 of the Congressional Record of February 23, 2009, the 
words ``Perkins Career and Technical Education Act'' should read 
``Higher Education Opportunity Act'' and the Senate requesters 
associated with this item should be changed to ``Conrad; Domenici; 
Dorgan.''

                          ____________________
```
