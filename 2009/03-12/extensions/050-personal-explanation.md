                         HON. CAROLYN McCARTHY

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 12, 2009

  Mrs. McCARTHY of New York. Madam Speaker, today, I was unexpectedly 
detained at a doctor's appointment and missed one vote. I would like 
the Record to reflect how I would have voted.
  Rollcall No. 116, on the motion to suspend the rules and agree to H.

Administration (NASA), the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), and Cornell

Opportunity, on the 5th anniversary of their successful landing, I
