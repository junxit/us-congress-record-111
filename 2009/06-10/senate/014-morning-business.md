# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 
1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, 
with the time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees, with the Republicans controlling the first half and 
the majority controlling the second half.
  The Senator from Nevada.

                          ____________________
```
