# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider en bloc Calendar Nos. 480 and 
522; that the nominations be confirmed, and the motions to reconsider 
be laid upon the table en bloc; that no further motions be in order; 
that any statements be printed in the Record; and that the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action.
```
