# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, NOVEMBER 9, 2009

**Speaking:** Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND]

**Measures:** H.R. 3082

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, NOVEMBER 9, 2009

  Mr. DORGAN. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m. Monday, November 9; that 
following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
proceed to a period of morning business until 3 p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak up to 10 minutes each; that following morning 
business, the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 3082, Military 
Construction and Veterans Affairs appropriations.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
