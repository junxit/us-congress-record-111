# NOMINATION DISCHARGED

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                         NOMINATION DISCHARGED

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs be discharged of PN704 
and that the Senate then proceed to the nomination; that the nomination 
be confirmed and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table; that 
no further motions be in order; that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action and the Senate return to legislative 
session, and that any statements relating thereto be printed in the 
Record.

  The nomination considered and confirmed is as follows:


                     small business administration
```
