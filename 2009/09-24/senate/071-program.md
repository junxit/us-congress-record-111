# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. As I announced earlier tonight, Mr. President, there will 
be no rollcall votes during Friday's session of the Senate. On Monday, 
which is Yom Kippur, the most significant and highest Holy Day of those 
of the Jewish faith, we will not be in session. Therefore, the next 
vote will occur around 5:30 p.m., Tuesday, September 29.
  As a reminder to all Senators, Paul Kirk will be sworn in as the new 
Senator from the State of Massachusetts, replacing Senator Kennedy. 
That will be at 3:30 tomorrow afternoon.
```
