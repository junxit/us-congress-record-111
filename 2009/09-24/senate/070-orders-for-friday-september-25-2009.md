# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 25, 2009

```
                 ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 25, 2009

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until tomorrow morning at 9:30 
a.m., Friday, September 25; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 3326, the 
Defense appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
