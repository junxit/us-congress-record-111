# A PROCLAMATION HONORING ERIN METZGER FOR WINNING THE GIRLS' DIVISION IV STATE SOFTBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING ERIN METZGER FOR WINNING THE GIRLS' DIVISION IV 
                      STATE SOFTBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2009

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker,
  Whereas, Erin Metzger showed hard work and dedication to the sport of 
softball; and
  Whereas, Erin Metzger was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Erin Metzger always displayed sportsmanship on and off the 
field; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with her friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Erin Metzger on winning 
the Girls' Division IV State Softball Championship. We recognize the 
tremendous hard work and sportsmanship she has demonstrated during the 
2008-2009 softball season.

                          ____________________
```
