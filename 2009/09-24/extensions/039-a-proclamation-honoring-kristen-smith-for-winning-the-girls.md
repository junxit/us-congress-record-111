# A PROCLAMATION HONORING KRISTEN SMITH FOR WINNING THE GIRLS' DIVISION IV STATE SOFTBALL CHAMPIONSHIP.

**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

```
 A PROCLAMATION HONORING KRISTEN SMITH FOR WINNING THE GIRLS' DIVISION 
                    IV STATE SOFTBALL CHAMPIONSHIP.

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2009

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker,
  Whereas, Kristen Smith showed hard work and dedication to the sport 
of softball; and
  Whereas, Kristen Smith was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Kristen Smith always displayed sportsmanship on and off of 
the field; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with her friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Kristen Smith on 
winning the Girls' Division IV State Softball Championship. We 
recognize the tremendous hard work and sportsmanship she has 
demonstrated during the 2008-2009 softball season.

                          ____________________
```
