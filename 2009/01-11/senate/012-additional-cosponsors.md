# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 197

  At the request of Mr. Feingold, the name of the Senator from Florida 
(Mr. Martinez) was added as a cosponsor of S. 197, a bill to assist in 
the conservation of cranes by supporting and providing, through 
projects of persons and organizations with expertise in crane 
conservation, financial resources for the conservation programs of 
countries the activities of which directly or indirectly affect cranes 
and the ecosystem of cranes.
```
