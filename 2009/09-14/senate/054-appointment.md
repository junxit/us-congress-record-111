# APPOINTMENT

```
                              APPOINTMENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to 14 U.S.C. 194(a), as amended by Public Law 101-595, and 
upon the recommendation of the Chairman of the Committee on Commerce, 
Science and Transportation, appoints the following Senators to the 
Board of Visitors of the U.S. Coast Guard Academy: the Senator from 
West Virginia (Mr. Rockefeller), ex officio, as Chairman of the 
Committee on Commerce, Science and Transportation; and the Senator from 
Washington (Ms. Cantwell), Committee on Commerce, Science and 
Transportation.
```
