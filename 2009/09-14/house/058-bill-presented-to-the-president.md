# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House reports that on September 10, 
2009 she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bill.

       H.R. 3325. To amend title XI of the Social Security Act to 
     reauthorize for 1 year the Work Incentives Planning and 
     Assistance program and the Protection and Advocacy for 
     Beneficiaries of Social Security program.
```
