# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 1388

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, we expect to extend morning business until 4 
o'clock. We only have consent now to do it until 3 o'clock. We will 
return later for that.
  During the time of morning business, we will have 10-minute speeches 
by Senators. Following morning business, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the motion to proceed to H.R. 1388, a bill to 
reauthorize and reform national service laws. At 6 p.m. this evening we 
will vote on a motion to invoke cloture on a filibuster preventing us 
from proceeding to H.R. 1388.
```
