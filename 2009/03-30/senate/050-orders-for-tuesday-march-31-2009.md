# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 31, 2009

**Speaking:** Conrad, Kent (C000705) [D-ND]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 13

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 31, 2009

  Mr. CONRAD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. tomorrow, 
Tuesday, March 31; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of the proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed to have expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 
Con. Res. 13, the concurrent resolution on the budget, under the 
previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
