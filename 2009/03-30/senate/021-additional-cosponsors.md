# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 42

  At the request of Mr. Ensign, the name of the Senator from Oklahoma 
(Mr. Inhofe) was added as a cosponsor of S. 42, a bill to amend title 
II of the Social Security Act to preserve and protect Social Security 
benefits of American workers and to help ensure greater congressional 
oversight of the Social Security system by requiring that both Houses 
of Congress approve a totalization agreement before the agreement, 
giving foreign workers Social Security benefits, can go into effect.

interest with respect to railroads.

Tester) was added as a cosponsor of S. 146, supra.
