# PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

**Speaking:** Conrad, Kent (C000705) [D-ND]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 13

```
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. CONRAD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Michael 
Feldman, a congressional fellow on the Budget Committee, be granted the 
privilege of the floor for the duration of the consideration of S. Con. 
Res. 13.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

Budget Committee staff members John Righter, Steve Posner, Joel 
Friedman, and Republican Budget Committee staff members Jim Hearn, 
David Fisher, and Jim Carter be granted floor privileges and floor 
passes during the consideration of S. Con. Res. 13.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
