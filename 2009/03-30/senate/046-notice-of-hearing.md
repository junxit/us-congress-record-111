# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND]

**Measures:** S. 313, H.R. 326, S. 443, S. 633

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                      COMMITTEE ON INDIAN AFFAIRS

  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the 
Committee on Indian Affairs will meet on Thursday, April 2, 2009, at 
2:15 p.m. in room 628 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building to conduct 
a hearing on the following bills: S. 313, White Mountain Apache Tribe 
Water Right Quantification Act, S. 443, the Hoh Indian Tribe Safe 
Homelands Act, S. 633, the Tribal Health Promotion and Tribal Colleges 
and Universities Advancement Act, and H.R. 326, the Cocopah Lands Act.
  Those wishing additional information may contact the Indian Affairs 
Committee at 202-224-2251.

                          ____________________
```
