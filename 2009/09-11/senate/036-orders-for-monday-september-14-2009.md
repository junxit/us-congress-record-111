# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 14, 2009

```
                 ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 14, 2009

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until Monday at 2 p.m., 
September 14; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate proceed to a period of morning business until 3:00 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each; that following morning business, the Senate resume consideration 
of Calendar No. 153, H.R. 3288, the Transportation and related agencies 
appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
