# Text of Senate Amendment 2343

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

```
  SA 2343. Mr. McCAIN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 3288, making appropriations for the Departments of 
Transportation, and Housing and Urban Development, and related agencies 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2010, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds in this Act may be used to fund 
     the City of Charlotte, Charlotte Area Transit System's (CATS) 
     Blue Line Extension-Northeast Corridor Project, SC.
                                 ______
```
