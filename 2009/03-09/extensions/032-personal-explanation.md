# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GENE GREEN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, March 9, 2009

  Mr. GENE GREEN of Texas. Madam Speaker, I rise today to state how I 
would have voted on March 6, 2009. With the expectation the Senate 
would pass the omnibus appropriations bill, and it would not be 
necessary to pass a continuing resolution on Friday, I returned home to 
Houston on Thursday night to attend meetings in our district.
  On Rollcall vote No. 107, to approve the journal, I would have voted 
``Yea.''
  On Rollcall vote No. 108, on the motion to recommit on H.J. Res. 38, 
I would have voted ``Aye.''
  On Rollcall vote No. 109, on the final passage of H.J. Res. 38, I 
would have voted ``Aye.''
```
