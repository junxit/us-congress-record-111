# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 10, 2009

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 1105

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 10, 2009

  Mr. DURBIN. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. on Tuesday, March 
10; that following the prayer and pledge the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date; the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
resume consideration of H.R. 1105, the Omnibus appropriations bill, as 
provided under the previous order; further, that the Senate recess from 
12:30 to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly part conference luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
