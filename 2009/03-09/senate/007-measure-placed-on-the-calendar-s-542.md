# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 542

```
                 MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 542

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I believe S. 542 is due for a second 
reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the title of 
the bill for the second time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 542) to repeal the provision of law that 
     provides automatic pay adjustments for Members of Congress.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I object to any further proceedings on this 
legislation at this time.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
