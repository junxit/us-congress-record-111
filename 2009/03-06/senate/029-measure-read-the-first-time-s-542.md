# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 542

**Speaking:** Warner, Mark R. (W000805) [D-VA]

**Measures:** S. 542

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 542

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I understand that S. 542, introduced 
earlier today by Senator Reid, is at the desk and I ask for its first 
reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 542) to repeal the provision of law that 
     provides automatic pay adjustments for Members of Congress.

  Mr. WARNER. I now ask for its second reading and object to my own 
request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be read for the second time on the next legislative 
day.
```
