# A PROCLAMATION HONORING MICHAEL EVANS FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION IV STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING MICHAEL EVANS FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION IV 
                     STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 29, 2009

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Michael Evans showed hard work and dedication to the sport 
of basketball; and
  Whereas, Michael Evans was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Michael Evans always displayed sportsmanship on and off of 
the court: Now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Michael Evans on 
winning the Boys' Division IV State Basketball Championship. We 
recognize the tremendous hard work and sportsmanship he has 
demonstrated during the 2008-2009 basketball season.

                          ____________________
```
