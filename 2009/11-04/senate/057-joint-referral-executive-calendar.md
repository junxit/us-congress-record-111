# JOINT REFERRAL--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Casey, Robert P., Jr. (C001070) [D-PA]

```
                   JOINT REFERRAL--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that the nomination of Suresh Kumar, to be Assistant Secretary 
of Commerce and Director General of the United States and Foreign 
Commercial Service, received in the Senate on October 29, 2009, and 
referred to the Banking Committee on November 2, now be jointly 
referred to the Commerce Committee.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
