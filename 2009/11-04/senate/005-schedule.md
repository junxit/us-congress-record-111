# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, the Senate will 
proceed to a period of morning business for 2 hours. Senators will be 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each. The Republicans 
will control the first half and the majority will control the second 
hour.
  Following morning business, the Senate will resume consideration of 
the Worker, Home Ownership, and Business Assistance Act of 2009. Under 
an agreement reached last night, we will agree to a substitute 
amendment and at 12:15 proceed to a cloture vote on the bill. At 12:15, 
we will have a vote. If cloture is invoked, the postcloture debate time 
will be considered to have begun running as if cloture had been invoked 
at 11:45 p.m. last night.

                          ____________________
```
