# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 11, 2009

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 11, 2009

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 11 a.m., 
Wednesday, March 11; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later

[[Page S2987]]

in the day, and the Senate proceed to a period of morning business 
until 11:30 a.m. with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each with the time controlled by the Republicans; further, that 
following morning business the Senate proceed to executive session 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
