# NOTICE OF HEARING

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a business meeting has 
been scheduled before Committee on Energy and Natural Resources. The 
business meeting will be held on Thursday, April 30, 2009 at 2:30 p.m., 
in room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate office building.
  The purpose of the business meeting is to consider the nominations of 
Kristina M. Johnson, to be the Under Secretary of Energy, Steven Elliot 
Koonin, to be the Under Secretary for Science, Department of Energy, 
Ines R. Triay, to be an Assistant Secretary of Energy, Environmental 
Management, Hilary Chandler Tompkins, to be Solicitor of the Department 
of the Interior, and Scott Blake Harris, to be the General Counsel of 
the Department of Energy. The Committee may also consider legislation 
on a Renewable Electricity Standard and on Siting of Interstate 
Electric Transmission Facilities.
  For further information, please contact Sam Fowler at (202) 224-7571 
or Amanda Kelly at (202) 224-6836.

                          ____________________
```
