# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  God of our destinies, guide our Senators this day by Your higher 
wisdom. Watch over them and use their work for Your glory. Lord, 
replace fear with faith, pessimism with hope, and error with truth. May 
these lawmakers become Your instruments for enabling justice to roll 
down like waters in this land we love. Give them serenity to accept 
what can't be changed, the courage to change what they can, and the 
wisdom to know one from the other.
  Lord, we also ask You to comfort the many who mourn Steve Mosley's 
death. Be near to Michelle and Steven, Jr., and all of his loved ones.
  We pray in the Redeemer's Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
