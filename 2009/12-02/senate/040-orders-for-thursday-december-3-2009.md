# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 3, 2009

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 3, 2009

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Thursday, December 3; that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 
3590, the health care reform legislation, as provided for under the 
previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
