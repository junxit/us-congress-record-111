# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, the minority leader and I have some things 
to discuss, so I ask unanimous consent that we recess subject to the 
call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 7:35 a.m., recessed until 
8:26 a.m., and reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Ms. Cantwell).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Nebraska.
```
