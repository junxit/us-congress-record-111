# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

**Measures:** S. 1963

```
                                PROGRAM

  Ms. CANTWELL. Mr. President, at 2:30 p.m. tomorrow the Senate will 
proceed to a series of three rollcall votes. The votes will be on the 
confirmation of the nomination of David Hamilton to be a U.S. circuit 
judge for the Seventh Circuit; in relation to the Coburn amendment No. 
2785, relating to spending priorities; and passage of S. 1963, the 
Caregivers and Veterans Omnibus Health Services Act, as amended, if 
amended.
  Finally, I ask unanimous consent that following the remarks of 
Senator Sessions, Senator Harkin, and Senator Alexander, the Senate 
adjourn under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Alabama is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
