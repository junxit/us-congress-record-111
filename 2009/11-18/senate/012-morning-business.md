# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period of morning business, with the Senator from West 
Virginia recognized to speak first and the next hour under the control 
of the majority leader or his designee and the next hour under the 
control of the Republican leader or his designee, with Senators, after 
Senator Rockefeller, permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes.
  The Senator from West Virginia is recognized.
```
