# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

**Speaking:** Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND]

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, at the request of the majority leader, I 
ask unanimous consent that the Senate recess subject to the call of the 
Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 5:18 p.m., recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 6:28 p.m. when called to 
order by the Presiding Officer (Ms. Cantwell).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Kansas is recognized.
```
