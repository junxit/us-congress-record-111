# HONORING THE LIFE OF KEITH ROMAINE

**Speaking:** Israel, Steve (I000057) [D-NY]

```
                   HONORING THE LIFE OF KEITH ROMAINE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEVE ISRAEL

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2009

  Mr. ISRAEL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to pay special tribute to the 
life and legacy of Keith Romaine, Brookhaven Councilman who we lost too 
soon on November 14, 2009 at the age of 36.
  Keith will be remembered by family, friends, and constituents as a 
devoted public servant who worked tirelessly to improve his 
neighborhood and community. I offer my support to his family and 
friends upon the loss of a bright, intelligent, and dedicated community 
leader who will be sorely missed by the people of Long Island.

                          ____________________
```
