# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4173, WALL STREET REFORM AND CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT OF 2009

**Measures:** H.Res. 964, H.R. 4173

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4173, 
         WALL STREET REFORM AND CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT OF 2009

  Mr. ARCURI, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 111-370) on the resolution (H. Res. 964) providing 
for further consideration of the bill (H.R. 4173) to provide for 
financial regulatory reform, to protect consumers and investors, to 
enhance Federal understanding of insurance issues, to regulate the 
over-the-counter derivatives markets, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
