# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 86, H.R. 3603, H.R. 3951, H.R. 4213

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 86. An act to eliminate an unused lighthouse 
     reservation, provide management consistency by incorporating 
     the rocks and small islands along the coast of Orange County, 
     California, into the California Coastal National Monument 
     managed by the Bureau of Land Management, and meet the 
     original Congressional intent of preserving Orange County's 
     rocks and small islands, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
       H.R. 3603. An act to rename the Ocmulgee National Monument; 
     to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
       H.R. 3951. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 2000 Louisiana Avenue in New 
     Orleans, Louisiana, as the ``Roy Rondeno, Sr. Post Office 
     Building''; to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 4213. An act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to extend certain expiring provisions, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Finance.

                          ____________________
```
