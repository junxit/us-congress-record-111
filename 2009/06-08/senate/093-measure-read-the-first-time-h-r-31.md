**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 31

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 31

  Mr. REID. Madam President, H.R. 31 is at the desk and has been 
received from the House; is that correct?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The leader is correct.
  Mr. REID. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.

[[Page S6318]]

       A bill (H.R. 31) to provide for the recognition of the

but object to my own request.
```
