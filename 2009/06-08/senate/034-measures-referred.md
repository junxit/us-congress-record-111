# MEASURES REFERRED

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 626. An act to provide that 4 of the 12 weeks of 
     parental leave made available to a Federal employee shall be 
     paid leave, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 1817. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 116 North West Street in 
     Somerville, Tennessee, as the ``John S. Wilder Post Office 
     Building''; to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 2200. An act to authorize the Transportation Security 
     Administration's programs relating to the provision of 
     transportation security, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
```
