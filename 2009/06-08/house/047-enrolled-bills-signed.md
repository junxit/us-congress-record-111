# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 663, H.R. 918, H.R. 1284, H.R. 1595

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly 
enrolled bills of the House of the following titles, which were 
thereupon signed by the Speaker:

       H.R. 663. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 12877 Broad Street in 
     Sparta, Georgia, as the ``Yvonne Ingram-Ephraim Post Office 
     Building''.
       H.R. 918. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 300 East 3rd Street in 
     Jamestown, New York, as the ``Stan Lundine Post Office 
     Building''.
       H.R. 1284. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 103 West Main Street in 
     McLain, Mississippi, as the ``Major Ed W. Freeman Post 
     Office''.
       H.R. 1595. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 3245 Latta Road in 
     Rochester, New York, as the ``Brian K. Schramm Post Office 
     Building''.

                          ____________________
```
