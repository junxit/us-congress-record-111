# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period of morning business for 1 hour, with the time 
equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the majority controlling the first half and the 
Republicans controlling the final half, with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Illinois.

                          ____________________
```
