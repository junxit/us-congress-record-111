by him to the bill H.R. 2892, making appropriations for the Department 
of Homeland Security for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2010, and

     inserting ``September 30, 2012''.
       Sec. 546.  Section 143 of the Continuing Appropriations 
     Resolution, 2009 (division A of Public Law 110-329; 122 Stat. 
     3580), as amended by section 101 of division J of the Omnibus 
     Appropriations Act, 2009 (Public Law 111-8; 123 Stat. 988), 
     is amended by striking ``shall'' and all that follows through 
     the end and inserting ``is further amended by striking `11-
     year' and inserting `17-year'.''.
