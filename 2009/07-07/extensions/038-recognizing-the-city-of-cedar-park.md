**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
                   RECOGNIZING THE CITY OF CEDAR PARK

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 7, 2009
```
