# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

       The Manager's Amendment to be offered by Chairman Reyes, or 
     a designee, to H.R. 2701, the Intelligence Authorization Act 
     for Fiscal Year 2010, does not contain any congressional 
     earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits, 
     as defined in clause 9 of Rule XXI.
       The amendment to be offered by Representative Velazquez, or 
     a designee, to H.R. 2965, the Enhancing Small Business 
     Research and Innovation Act of 2009, does not contain any 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of Rule XXI.
       The amendment to be offered by Representative DeLauro, or a 
     designee, to H.R. 2997, the Agriculture, Rural Development, 
     Food and Drug Administration, and Related Agencies 
     Appropriations Act, 2010, contains no congressional earmarks, 
     limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined 
     in clause 9(e), 9(f) or 9(g) of rule XXI.
```
