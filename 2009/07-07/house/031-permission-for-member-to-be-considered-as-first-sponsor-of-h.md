# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1283

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1283

  Mr. PATRICK J. MURPHY of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous 
consent that I may hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of 
H.R. 1283, the Military Readiness Enhancement Act, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Ellen Tauscher of California, for the 
purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
