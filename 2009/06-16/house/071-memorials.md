# MEMORIALS

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 4 of rule XXII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:
       95. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the State Senate of 
     Louisiana, relative to SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION NO. 51 
     memorializing the United States Congress to take such actions 
     as are necessary to appropriate funds to be used for storm-
     proofing interior pump stations in St. Bernard and 
     Plaquemines parishes; to the Committee on Appropriations.
       96. Also, a memorial of the State Senate of Louisiana, 
     relative to SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION NO. 15 memorializing 
     the Congress of the United States to take such actions as are 
     necessary to maintain the current incentives for the 
     exploration and production of domestic oil and natural gas; 
     jointly to the Committees on Natural Resources and 
     Appropriations.
```
