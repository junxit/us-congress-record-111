# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, the Senate will be 
in a period for the transaction of morning business for an hour. 
Senators will be permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each. The 
majority will control the first 30 minutes and the Republicans will 
control the next 30 minutes. Following morning business, the Senate 
will resume consideration of the motion to proceed to S. 1023, the 
Travel Promotion Act. There will be 1 hour for debate prior to a 
cloture vote on that motion to proceed. Senators should expect the vote 
to begin as early as quarter to 12 today. The Senate will recess, as we 
do on every Tuesday, from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for our weekly caucus 
luncheons.

                          ____________________
```
