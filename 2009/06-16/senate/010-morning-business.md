# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period of morning business for 1 hour, with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders or their designees, with 
the majority controlling the first half and the Republicans controlling 
the second half.
  The Senator from New Jersey.

                          ____________________
```
