# DISCHARGED NOMINATION

```
                         DISCHARGED NOMINATION

Executive Calendar:

       *JO-ELLEN DARCY, OF MARYLAND, TO BE AN ASSISTANT SECRETARY 
     OF THE ARMY.

before any duly constituted committee of the Senate.
```
