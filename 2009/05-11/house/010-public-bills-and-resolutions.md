# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 2342

                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

             title 38, United States Code, to direct the Secretary 
             of Veterans Affairs to establish a family caregiver 
             program to furnish support services to family members
