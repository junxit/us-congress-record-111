# MEASURES REFERRED

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1275. An act to direct the exchange of certain land in 
     Grand, San Juan, and Uintah Counties, Utah, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
       H.R. 1945. An act to require the Secretary of the Interior 
     to conduct a study on the feasibility and suitability of 
     constructing a storage reservoir, outlet works, and a 
     delivery system for the Tule River Indian Tribe of the Tule 
     River Reservation in the State of California to provide a 
     water supply for domestic, municipal, industrial, and 
     agricultural purposes, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.

  The following concurrent resolution was read, and referred as 
indicated:

       H. Con. Res. 142. Concurrent resolution supporting National 
     Men's Health Week; to the Committee on Health, Education, 
     Labor, and Pensions.
```
