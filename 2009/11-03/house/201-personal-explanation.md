# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Conyers, John, Jr. (C000714) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.Res. 398, H.Res. 736, H.Res. 866, H.Res. 867, H.R. 3157, H.R. 3949

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. CONYERS. Madam Speaker, on November 3, 2009, I was unable to cast 
votes due to personal reasons. I was not present for rollcall votes 835 
through 840. Had I been present, I would have cast a ``yea'' vote for 
final passage of H.R. 3949. I would have cast a ``yea'' vote for final 
passage of H. Res. 398. I would have cast a ``yea'' vote for final 
passage of H. Res. 866. I would have cast a ``nay'' vote for the final 
passage of H. Res. 867. I would have cast a ``yea'' vote for final 
passage of H.R. 3157. Also, I would have cast a ``yea'' vote for H. 
Res. 736.
```
