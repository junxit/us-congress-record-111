  Mrs. BACHMANN. Madam Speaker, on November 3, 2009, I was unexpectedly

further consideration of the ``Report of the United Nations Fact 
Finding Mission on the Gaza Conflict'' in multilateral fora. Had I been 
present, as a cosponsor of this resolution, I would have voted ``yea.''
