# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2868, CHEMICAL FACILITY ANTI-TERRORISM ACT OF 2009

**Measures:** H.Res. 885, H.R. 2868

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2868, CHEMICAL 
                  FACILITY ANTI-TERRORISM ACT OF 2009

  Mr. McGOVERN, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 111-327) on the resolution (H. Res. 885) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2868) to amend the Homeland 
Security Act of 2002 to extend, modify, and recodify the authority of 
the Secretary of Homeland Security to enhance security and protect 
against acts of terrorism against chemical facilities, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
