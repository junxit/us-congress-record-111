# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 4, 2009

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 3548

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 4, 2009

  Mr. DURBIN. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. Wednesday, November 4; 
that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
proceed to a period of morning business for 2 hours with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the time 
equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the Republicans controlling the first half and the 
majority controlling the final half; and that following morning 
business the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 3548, the Worker, 
Homeownership, and Business Assistance Act of 2009, as provided for 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
