# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 7, 2009

**Speaking:** Kaufman, Edward E. (K000373) [D-DE]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 7, 2009

  Mr. KAUFMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Wednesday, October 7; that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate proceed to a period of 
morning business for up to 1 hour with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally divided and 
controlled between the two leaders or their designees, with the 
majority controlling the first half and the Republicans controlling the 
final half; that following morning business, the Senate resume 
consideration of H.R. 2847, Commerce-Justice-Science appropriations.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
