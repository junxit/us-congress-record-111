# MORNING BUSINESS

```

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
proceed to a period of morning business until 3:15 p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the time 
equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees.
  The Senator from Tennessee is recognized.
  Mr. ALEXANDER. Mr. President, would the Chair let me know when 9 
minutes has expired?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair is happy to do that.
```
