# A PROCLAMATION HONORING LOGAN GRAY FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION III STATE BASEBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

```
 A PROCLAMATION HONORING LOGAN GRAY FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION III 
                      STATE BASEBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2009

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Logan Gray showed hard work and dedication to the sport of 
baseball; and
  Whereas, Logan Gray was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Logan Gray always displayed sportsmanship on and off of the 
field; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Logan Gray on winning 
the Boys' Division III State Baseball Championship. We recognize the 
tremendous hard work and sportsmanship he has demonstrated during the 
2008-2009 baseball season.
```
