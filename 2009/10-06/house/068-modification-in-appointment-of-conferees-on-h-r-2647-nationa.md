# MODIFICATION IN APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON H.R. 2647, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2010

**Measures:** H.R. 2647

```
MODIFICATION IN APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON H.R. 2647, NATIONAL DEFENSE 
                 AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2010

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Nye). Without objection and pursuant to 
clause 11 of rule I, the Chair removes the gentleman from Texas (Mr. 
Reyes) as a conferee from the Permanent Select Committee on 
Intelligence on H.R. 2647 and appoints the gentleman from Florida (Mr. 
Hastings) to fill the vacancy.
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will notify the Senate of the 
change in conferees.
```
