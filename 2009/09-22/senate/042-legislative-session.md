# LEGISLATIVE SESSION

```
                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate will now resume legislative 
session.
  Mr. SANDERS. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, are we in a period of morning business?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Yes, we are.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that we terminate morning business 
and move to the legislation that is before the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
