# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, in an effort to get to the Chamber, I was in 
a little bit too big of a hurry. I should have made my very brief 
statement with the Republican leader here, but I didn't, so I apologize 
to him for that. I have discussed it with the Republican leader.
  I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess until 6:30 
tonight.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Thereupon, at 5:27 p.m., the Senate recessed until 6:30 p.m., and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Brown).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Illinois is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
