# ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, FEBRUARY 7, 2009

```
                 ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, FEBRUARY 7, 2009

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 12 noon, 
Saturday, February 7; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
to have expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the H.R. 1, as 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
