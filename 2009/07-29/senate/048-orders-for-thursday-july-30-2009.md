# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 30, 2009

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 30, 2009

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., on 
Thursday, July 30; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
to have expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate then proceed to a period of morning 
business, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each; further; I ask that the Senate recess from 2 p.m. until 3 p.m. to 
allow for the Members-only briefing with Secretary of State Clinton and 
Secretary of Defense Gates.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
