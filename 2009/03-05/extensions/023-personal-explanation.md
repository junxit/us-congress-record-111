**Speaking:** Boyd, Allen (B000716) [D-FL]

                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ALLEN BOYD

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 5, 2009

  Mr. BOYD. Madam Speaker, due to personal reasons, I was unable to 
attend to several votes. Had I been present, my vote would have been

Nation and particularly to the survivors and families of the September 
11, 2001 attacks; ``yea'' on H. Res. 195, Recognizing and honoring the 
employees of the Department of Homeland Security on its sixth 
anniversary for their continuous efforts to keep the Nation safe; and 
``yea'' on H. Res. 45, Raising awareness and promoting education on the 
criminal justice system by establishing March as ``National Criminal 
Justice Month''.
