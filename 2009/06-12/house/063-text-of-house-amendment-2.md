# Text of House Amendment 2

**Speaking:** Paulsen, Erik (P000594) [R-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2847

```
H.R. 2847

                        Offered By: Mr. Paulsen

       Amendment No. 2. At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following new section:

       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to try an individual who is detained at Naval 
     Station, Guantanamo Bay, Cuba, as of the date of the 
     enactment of this Act, in any United States court.
```
