```
PERMISSION TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON COMMERCE, JUSTICE, SCIENCE, AND 
               RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS BILL, 2010

  Mr. OBEY. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Wisconsin?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 1 of rule XXI, all points 
of order are reserved.
```
