# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 39, S. 338

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 39. An act to repeal section 10(f) of Public Law 93-531, 
     commonly known as the ``Bennett Freeze''; to the Committee on 
     Natural Resources.
       S. 338. An act to amend the Omnibus Indian Advancement Act 
     to modify the date as of which certain tribal land of the 
     Lytton Rancheria of California is deemed to be held in trust 
     and to provide for the conduct of certain activities on the 
     land; to the Committee on Natural Resources.

                          ____________________
```
