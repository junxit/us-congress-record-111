# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 17, 2009

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 17, 2009

  Ms. MIKULSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. tomorrow, 
Tuesday, March 17; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and there then be a period for the transaction of morning 
business for up to 1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 
10 minutes each, with the time equally divided and controlled between 
the two leaders or their designees, with the majority controlling the 
first half and the Republicans controlling the second half; further, 
that following morning business, the Senate resume consideration of the 
motion to proceed to H.R. 146, the legislative vehicle for the lands 
package;

[[Page 7327]]

and, finally, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate recess from 12:30 
p.m. to 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly caucus luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
