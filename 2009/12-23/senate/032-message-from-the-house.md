                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  At 1:42 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered

tempore (Mr. Van Hollen) has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 4284. An act to extend the Generalized System of 
     Preferences and the Andean Trade Preference Act, and for 
     other purposes.

tempore (Mr. Byrd).
