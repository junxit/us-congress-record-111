# DISCHARGE AND REFERRAL--EXECUTIVE NOMINATION

```
              DISCHARGE AND REFERRAL--EXECUTIVE NOMINATION

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that the Committee on Environment and Public Works be 
discharged from further consideration of the following nomination and 
that the nomination be referred to the Committee on Commerce, Science, 
and Transportation:

       Timothy McGee, of Louisiana, to be an Assistant Secretary 
     of Commerce.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
