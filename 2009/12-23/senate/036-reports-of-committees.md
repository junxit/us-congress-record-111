# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

**Measures:** S. 69, S. 1178

```
                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

       By Mr. LIEBERMAN, from the Committee on Homeland Security 
     and Governmental Affairs, without amendment:
       S. 69. A bill to establish a fact-finding Commission to 
     extend the study of a prior Commission to investigate and 
     determine facts and circumstances surrounding the relocation, 
     internment, and deportation to Axis countries of Latin 
     Americans of Japanese descent from December 1941 through 
     February 1948, and the impact of those actions by the United 
     States, and to recommend appropriate remedies, and for other 
     purposes (Rept. No. 111-112).
       By Mr. DORGAN, from the Committee on Indian Affairs, with 
     amendments:
       S. 1178. A bill to extend Federal recognition to the 
     Chickahominy Indian Tribe, the Chickahominy Indian Tribe-
     Eastern Division, the Upper Mattaponi Tribe, the Rappahannock 
     Tribe, Inc., the Monacan Indian Nation, and the Nansemond 
     Indian Tribe (Rept. No. 111-113).
```
