# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 223, H.Res. 976

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 11(b) of House 
Resolution 976, the House shall stand adjourned until noon today unless 
the conditions specified in section 11(c) of that resolution have been 
met, in which case the House shall stand adjourned sine die pursuant to 
House Concurrent Resolution 223.
  Accordingly (at 11 o'clock and 33 minutes a.m.), the House adjourned 
until today, Wednesday, December 23, 2009, at noon.
```
