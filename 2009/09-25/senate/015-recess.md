# RECESS

**Speaking:** Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND], Inouye, Daniel K. (I000025) [D-HI], Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess until 3:15.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:10 p.m., recessed until 
3:15 p.m. and reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Hawaii.
  Mr. INOUYE. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
