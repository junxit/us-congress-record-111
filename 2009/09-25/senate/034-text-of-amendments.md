# TEXT OF AMENDMENTS

**Measures:** H.R. 3326, H.R. 3614

```
                           TEXT OF AMENDMENTS

  SA 2556. Mr. REID (for Ms. Landrieu (for herself and Ms. Snowe)) 
proposed an amendment to the bill H.R. 3614, to provide for an 
additional temporary extension of programs under the Small Business Act 
and the Small Business Investment Act of 1958, and for other purposes; 
as follows:

       Strike sections 2 and 3.
                                 ______
                                 
  SA 2557. Mr. INHOFE submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 3326, making appropriations for the Department of 
Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2010, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. __.  Of the amount appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by title IV under the heading ``Research, 
     Development, Test, and Evaluation, Army'' and available for 
     Program Element #0708045A, up to $1,000,000 may be available 
     for Advanced Ultrasonic Inspection of Helicopter Rotor Blades 
     and Other Composite Components.
```
