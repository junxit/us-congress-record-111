# Text of Senate Amendment 2228

**Speaking:** Nelson, Bill (N000032) [D-FL]

```
  SA 2228. Mr. NELSON of Florida (for himself, Mr. Reid, Mr. Martinez, 
Mr. Akaka and Mr. Ensign) submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill S. 1023, to establish a non-profit 
corporation to communicate United States entry policies and otherwise 
promote leisure, business, and scholarly travel to the United States; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end, add the following:

     SEC. _. LIMITATION ON CERTAIN TRAVEL AND CONFERENCES 
                   POLICIES.

       No agency or department of the United States may establish 
     a travel or conference policy that takes into account the 
     perception of a location as a resort or vacation destination 
     in determining the location for an event.
                                 ______
```
