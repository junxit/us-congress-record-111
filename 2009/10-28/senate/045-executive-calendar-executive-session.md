                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate proceed to executive session to consider Calendar Nos. 504, 505, 
506 to and including 511, except the nomination of BG Michael J. Walsh, 
512 to and including 514, 519, 520, and all nominations on the 
Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, Coast Guard, Marine Corps, and 
Navy; that the nominations be confirmed en bloc; the motions to 
reconsider be laid upon the table en bloc; that no further motions be 
in order; that any statements relating to the nominations be printed in 
the Record; provided further that the President be immediately notified 
of the Senate's action; and the Senate then return to legislative 
session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nominations considered and confirmed en bloc are as follows:
