# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY V. JOHNSON

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 28, 2009

  Mr. JOHNSON of Illinois. Madam Speaker, unfortunately on October 26, 
2009, I was unable to cast my votes on H. Res. 368 and H. Res. 562 and 
wish the Record to reflect my intentions had I been able to vote.
  Had I been present for rollcall No. 814 on the motion to suspend the 
rules and agree to H. Res. 368, Congratulating the University of Iowa 
Hawkeyes wrestling team on winning the 2009 NCAA Division I National 
Wrestling Championships, I would have voted ``aye.''
  Had I been present for rollcall No. 815 on the motion to suspend the 
rules and pass H. Res. 562, Congratulating Syracuse University for 
winning the National Collegiate Athletic Association Division I Mens 
Lacrosse Tournament, I would have voted ``aye.''
```
