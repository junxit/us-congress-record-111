# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 28

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 4 of rule XXII,

       218. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Alaska, relative to House 
     Joint Resolution No. 28 urging the President and the Congress 
     of the United States not to adopt any policy, rule, or 
     administrative action or enact legislation that would 
     restrict energy exploration, development, and production in 
     federal and state waters around Alaska; to the Committee on 
     Natural Resources.
```
