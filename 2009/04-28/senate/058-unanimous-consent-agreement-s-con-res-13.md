# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. CON. RES 13

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 13

```
              UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. CON. RES 13

  Mr. DURBIN. I ask unanimous consent that on Wednesday, April 29, 
following a period of morning business, the Senate begin the statutory 
debate with respect to the conference report to accompany S. Con. Res. 
13, notwithstanding the receipt of papers from the House; further, that 
when the Senate receives a message from the House regarding S. Con. 
Res. 13, the Senate then proceed to the consideration of the conference 
report.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
