                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 182

182, a bill to amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to provide 
more effective remedies to victims of discrimination in the payment of 
wages on the basis of sex, and for other purposes.

year budget authority, and for other purposes.

  At the request of Mr. Wyden, the name of the Senator from Washington 
(Mrs. Murray) was added as a cosponsor of S. 559, a bill to provide

death of a Federal employee in fire protection activities caused by any

                                 S. 614
