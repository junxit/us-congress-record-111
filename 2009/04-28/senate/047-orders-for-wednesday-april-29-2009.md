# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 29, 2009

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 29, 2009

  Mr. DURBIN. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. tomorrow, Wednesday, 
April 29; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and there be a period of morning business for up to 1 hour, with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes, with the time equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders or their designees, with 
the Republicans controlling the first half and the majority controlling 
the final half; further, I ask unanimous consent that following morning 
business, the Senate proceed as previously ordered.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
