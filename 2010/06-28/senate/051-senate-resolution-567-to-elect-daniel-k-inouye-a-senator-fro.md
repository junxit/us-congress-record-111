# SENATE RESOLUTION 567--TO ELECT DANIEL K. INOUYE, A SENATOR FROM THE STATE OF HAWAII, TO BE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATES

**Measures:** S.Res. 567

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

 SENATE RESOLUTION 567--TO ELECT DANIEL K. INOUYE, A SENATOR FROM THE 
   STATE OF HAWAII, TO BE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE OF THE 
                             UNITED STATES

  Mr. REID (for himself and Mr. McConnell) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 567

       Resolved, That Daniel K. Inouye, a Senator from the State 
     of Hawaii, be, and he is hereby, elected President of the 
     Senate pro tempore.
```
