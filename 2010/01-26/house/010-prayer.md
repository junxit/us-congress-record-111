# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, Washington, D.C., The Catholic 
University of America, offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, in these times of difficulty and confusion, 
our hearts remind us to turn back to You. You are the light; You are 
the truth. We trust that You will guide our paths during these troubled 
times; You keep us safe from all evil; and one day, You will lead us 
safely home. May we open our eyes, now, to see You in our lives; may we 
open our ears, this day, to hear Your voice. And thus may we follow You 
with steadfastness and courage. We ask this in the power of Your Spirit 
and in Your Word present and among us. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
