# PENDING INVESTIGATION INTO THE PMA SCANDAL

**Speaking:** Flake, Jeff (F000444) [R-AZ]

```
               PENDING INVESTIGATION INTO THE PMA SCANDAL

  (Mr. FLAKE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. FLAKE. Mr. Speaker, a week ago I introduced a privileged 
resolution asking the Committee on Standards of Official Conduct to 
report back to the House as to the extent of their investigation into 
the PMA scandal. A motion to refer that resolution to the committee was 
adopted unanimously. Every Member in this body voted for it a week ago; 
yet here we are a week later and we haven't heard anything from the 
Standards Committee as to the extent of their investigation.
  I'll be introducing this resolution again today, and I hope that we 
can finally get an answer as to the extent of the investigation into 
the PMA scandal.

                          ____________________
```
