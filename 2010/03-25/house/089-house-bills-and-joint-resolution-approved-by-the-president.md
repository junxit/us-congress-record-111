       HOUSE BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTION APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

  The President notified the Clerk of the House that on the following 
dates he had approved and signed bills and a joint resolution of the 
following titles:

           January 22, 2010:
       H.R. 4462. An Act to accelerate the income tax benefits for 
     charitable cash contributions for the relief of victims of 
     the earthquake in Haiti.
           January 29, 2010:
       H.R. 1817. An Act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 116 North West Street in 
     Somerville, Tennessee, as the ``John S. Wilder Post Office 
     Building''.

     States Postal Service located at 76 Brookside Avenue in 
     Chester, New

       H.R. 730. An Act to strengthen efforts in the Department of

       H.R. 4891. An Act to provide a temporary extension of

       H.R. 1299. An Act to make technical corrections to the laws

       H.R. 2847. An Act making appropriations for the Departments

     for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2010, and for other

           March 23, 2010:
