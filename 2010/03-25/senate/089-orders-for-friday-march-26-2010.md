# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MARCH 26, 2010

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                   ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MARCH 26, 2010

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until 9:30 a.m., 
tomorrow, Friday, March 26; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
proceed to a period of morning business, with the time until 12:30 p.m. 
equally divided and controlled between Senators Stabenow and Coburn or 
their designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
