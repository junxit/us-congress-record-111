# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Landrieu, Mary L. (L000550) [D-LA]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


       subcommittee on international trade, customs, and global 
                            competitiveness

  Ms. LANDRIEU. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Subcommittee on International Trade, Customs, and Global 
Competitiveness of the Committee on Finance be authorized to meet 
during the session of the Senate on March 25, 2010, at 2:30 p.m., in 
room 215 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building, to conduct a hearing 
entitled ``Doubling U.S. Exports: Are U.S. Sea Ports Ready for the 
Challenge?''
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
