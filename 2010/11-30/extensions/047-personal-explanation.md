# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Sullivan, John (S001155) [R-OK]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOHN SULLIVAN

                              of oklahoma

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 30, 2010

  Mr. SULLIVAN. Madam Speaker, I rise to state for the record that I 
intended to vote ``nay'' on rollcall vote 584 taken on November 30, 
2010. The Congressional Record currently lists me as an ``aye'' vote on 
this measure. As a fiscal conservative, I cannot support $4.6 billion 
in government spending in a period of record federal deficits and 
budget constraints facing American families.
```
