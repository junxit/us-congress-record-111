# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 23, 2010

**Speaking:** Rockefeller, John D., IV (R000361) [D-WV]

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 23, 2010

  Mr. ROCKEFELLER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the Senate completes its business today, it recess until 2:15 p.m. on 
Tuesday, March 23; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
