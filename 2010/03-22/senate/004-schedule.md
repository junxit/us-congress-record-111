# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, the Senate will be 
in a period for the transaction of morning business until 3 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the time 
equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees.
  At 3 p.m., the Senate will resume consideration of H.R. 1586, the 
Federal Aviation Administration legislation. At 5:30 p.m., the Senate 
will proceed to a series of up to three rollcall votes in relation to 
the FAA bill. Senators will be notified when we know exactly how many 
rollcall votes will be necessary before we complete action on the bill 
tonight.

                          ____________________
```
