# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Begich, Mark (B001265) [D-AK]

**Measures:** S. 3150

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senate will be in a period of 
morning business until 3 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein 
for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally controlled and divided 
between the two leaders or their designees.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Alaska is 
recognized.
  Mr. BEGICH. I thank the Chair.
  (The remarks of Senator Begich pertaining to the introduction of S. 
3150 are located in today's Record under ``Statements on Introduced 
Bills and Joint Resolutions.'')
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Tennessee.
```
