# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Father of love, source of all blessings, help us pass from our old 
life of brokenness and sin to the new life of healing and grace.
  May Your word today bring good news to those most in need of Your 
mercy.
  In our service to others and this Nation may we find You in our midst 
as our saving Lord with redeeming power.
  Prepare us for the glory of Your kingdom now and forever. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
