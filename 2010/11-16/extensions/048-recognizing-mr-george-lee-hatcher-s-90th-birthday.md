# RECOGNIZING MR. GEORGE LEE HATCHER'S 90TH BIRTHDAY

```
           RECOGNIZING MR. GEORGE LEE HATCHER'S 90TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DAVID P. ROE

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 16, 2010

  Mr. ROE of Tennessee. Madam Speaker, today I recognize the 90th 
birthday of Mr. George Lee Hatcher. This significant milestone is 
certainly an incredible accomplishment.
  Over the years, George has dedicated his life to serving his country 
and his family.
  Madam Speaker, as a fellow veteran, I want to thank Mr. George Lee 
Hatcher for his service to this country. I want to thank him for all he 
has done, and I wish him nothing but the very best.
  May the experience and wisdom George has attained and shared over the 
years encourage and teach those around him, lasting for generations to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
