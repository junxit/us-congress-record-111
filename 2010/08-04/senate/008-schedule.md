# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 1586

```
                                SCHEDULE

will resume consideration of the motion to concur with respect to the 
House message on H.R. 1586, with an amendment dealing with FMAP and 
teacher funding.
```
