# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 12, 2010

```
          CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 12, 2010

  Mr. LARSEN of Washington. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the House adjourns today on a motion offered pursuant to this 
order, it adjourn to meet at 1 p.m. on Friday, February 12, 2010, 
unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting 
its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 235, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Ms. Edwards of Maryland). Is there objection 
to the request of the gentleman from Washington?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
