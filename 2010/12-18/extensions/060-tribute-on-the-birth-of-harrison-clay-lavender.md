# TRIBUTE ON THE BIRTH OF HARRISON CLAY LAVENDER

**Speaking:** Bachus, Spencer (B000013) [R-AL]

```
             TRIBUTE ON THE BIRTH OF HARRISON CLAY LAVENDER

                                 ______

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives
```
