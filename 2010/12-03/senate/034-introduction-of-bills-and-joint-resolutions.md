# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. BURR (for himself and Mr. Coburn):
       S. 4006. A bill to provide for the use of unobligated 
     discretionary stimulus dollars to address AIDS Drug 
     Assistance Program waiting lists and other cost containment 
     measures impacting State ADAP programs; read the first time.
           By Mr. AKAKA:
       S. 4007. A bill to amend the Humane Methods of Livestock 
     Slaughter Act of 1958 to ensure the humane slaughter of 
     nonambulatory livestock, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry.
           By Mr. CASEY (for himself and Mr. Brown of 
             Massachusetts):
       S. 4008. A bill to enhance United States diplomatic efforts 
     with respect to Iran by imposing additional economic 
     sanctions against Iran, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Finance.
