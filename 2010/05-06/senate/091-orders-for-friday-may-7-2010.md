# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 7, 2010

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 3217

```
                     ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 7, 2010

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., 
Friday, May 7; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 3217, Wall Street 
reform.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
