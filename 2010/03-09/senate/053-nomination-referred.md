# NOMINATION REFERRED

```
                          NOMINATION REFERRED

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that the nomination of Robert A. Harding to be Assistant 
Secretary of Homeland Security, received by the Senate on Monday, March 
8, be referred to the Senate Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation; that upon the reporting out or discharge of the 
nomination, it then be referred to the Committee on Homeland Security 
and Governmental Affairs for a period not to exceed 30 calendar days; 
that if the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs has 
not reported the nomination at that time, then the committee be 
discharged and the nomination be placed on the Executive Calendar.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
