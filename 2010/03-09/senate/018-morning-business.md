# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY], Burris, Roland (B001266) [D-IL]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that upon 
disposition of the amendments in order this morning, the Senate then 
proceed to a period for the transaction of morning business until 12:30 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each, and that at 12:30 p.m., the Senate stand in recess until 2:15 
p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Illinois is recognized.
  Mr. BURRIS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to speak as in 
morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate is in morning business.

                          ____________________
```
