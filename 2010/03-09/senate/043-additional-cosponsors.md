                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 118

  At the request of Mr. Kohl, the name of the Senator from New York

purposes.

connected with the news media.

Legal Services Corporation Act to meet special needs

Dakota (Mr. Johnson) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1700, a bill to

the commercial development of oil, natural gas, and minerals, to 
express the sense of Congress that the President should disclose any 
payment relating to the commercial development of oil, natural gas, and 
minerals on Federal land, and for other purposes.

                                S. 2888

  At the request of Mr. Cardin, the name of the Senator from Wisconsin 
(Mr. Feingold) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2888, a bill to amend 
section 205 of title 18, United States Code, to exempt qualifying law

                                S. 2993

  At the request of Mr. Sanders, the name of the Senator from Michigan

the quantity of solar photovoltaic electricity by providing rebates for 
the purchase and installation of an additional 10,000,000 solar roofs
