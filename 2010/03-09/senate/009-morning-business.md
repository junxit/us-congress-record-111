# MORNING BUSINESS

```

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there

permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders, with the Republicans 
controlling the first portion and the majority controlling the second 
portion.
```
