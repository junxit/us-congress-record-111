# CONGRATULATING THE 482ND FIGHTER WING

```
                 CONGRATULATING THE 482ND FIGHTER WING

  (Ms. ROS-LEHTINEN asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. ROS-LEHTINEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise tonight to congratulate 
Homestead's Air Reserve Base 482nd Fighter Wing for receiving the 
Department of Defense Reserve Family Readiness Award. Through the 
vigilance of these brave men and women in uniform every day, Americans 
can live with a greater peace of mind. The safety of our families is 
dependent on them. And it is heartwarming to know that our military 
families are given the extra support that they need.
  The strong leadership of Wing Commander BG William B. Binger has made 
this distinction possible. He serves as an inspiration and motivation 
for such a remarkable unit and support personnel.
  Again, congratulations to the 482nd Fighter Wing of the Homestead Air 
Reserve Base for this well-deserved honor. Congratulations, ladies and 
gentlemen.

                          ____________________
```
