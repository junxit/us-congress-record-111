# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 4, 2010

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 4, 2010

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., Thursday, March 4; that 
following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
resume consideration of H.R. 4213, the Tax Extenders Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
