# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, DECEMBER 13, 2010

**Speaking:** Jackson, Jesse L., Jr. (J000283) [D-IL]

```
                ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, DECEMBER 13, 2010

  Mr. JACKSON of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 10 a.m. on Monday 
next; and further, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to 
meet at 12:30 p.m. on Tuesday, December 14, 2010, for morning-hour 
debate.

request of the gentleman from Illinois?
```
