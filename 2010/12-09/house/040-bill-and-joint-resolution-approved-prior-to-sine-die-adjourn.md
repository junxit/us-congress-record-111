# BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION APPROVED PRIOR TO SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

```
    BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION APPROVED PRIOR TO SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

  The President notified the Clerk of the House that on the following 
dates, he had approved and signed bill and joint resolution of the 
following titles:

           December 19, 2009:
       H.R. 3326. An Act making appropriations for the Department 
     of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2010, and 
     for other purposes.
           December 22, 2009:
       H.J. Res. 62. A joint resolution appointing the day for the 
     convening of the second session of the One Hundred Eleventh 
     Congress.
```
