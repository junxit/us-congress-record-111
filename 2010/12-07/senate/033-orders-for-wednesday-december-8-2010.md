# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 8, 2010

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 8, 2010

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday, 
December 8; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and that following any leader remarks, the Senate resume the Court 
of Impeachment of Judge G. Thomas Porteous, Jr.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
