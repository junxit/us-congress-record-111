# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
proceed to legislative session for a period of morning business with 
the Senator from Florida, Mr. LeMieux, recognized to speak therein for 
up to 15 minutes.
  Senator LeMieux.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. Hagan). The Senator from Tennessee.
  Mr. CORKER. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LeMIEUX. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
