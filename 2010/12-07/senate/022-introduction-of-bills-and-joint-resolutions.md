# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 4011, S. 4012, S. 4013, S. 4014

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. MENENDEZ (for himself, Mr. Lugar, and Mr. 
             Leahy):
       S. 4011. A bill to establish the Western Hemisphere Drug 
     Policy Commission; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
           By Mr. KOHL:
       S. 4012. A bill to improve the employability of older 
     Americans; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Ms. KLOBUCHAR (for herself, Mr. Thune, and Ms. 
             Stabenow):
       S. 4013. A bill to direct the Secretary of Transportation 
     to promulgate a rule to improve the daytime and nighttime 
     visibility of agricultural equipment that may be operated on 
     a public road; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
           By Ms. CANTWELL (for herself, Mrs. Murray, Ms. 
             Murkowski, and Mr. Begich):
       S. 4014. A bill to provide for the replacement or 
     rebuilding of a vessel for the non American Fisheries Act 
     trawl catcher processors that comprise the Amendment 80 
     fleet; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
```
