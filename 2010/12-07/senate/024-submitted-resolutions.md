                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

 SENATE RESOLUTION 696--MAKING MINORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS FOR CERTAIN 
                   COMMITTEES FOR THE 111TH CONGRESS

  Mr. McCONNELL submitted the following resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 696

       Resolved, That the following be the minority membership on 
     the following committees for the remainder of the 111th 
     Congress, or until their successors are appointed:
       COMMITTEE ON ARMED SERVICES: Mr. McCain, Mr. Inhofe, Mr. 
     Sessions, Mr. Chambliss, Mr. Graham, Mr. Thune, Mr. Wicker, 
     Mr. LeMieux, Mr. Brown, Mr. Burr, Mr. Vitter, Ms. Collins, 
     and Mr. Kirk.
       COMMITTEE ON HOMELAND SECURITY AND GOVERNMENTAL AFFAIRS: 
     Ms. Collins, Mr. Coburn, Mr. Brown, Mr. McCain, Mr. 
     Voinovich, Mr. Ensign, Mr. Graham, and Mr. Kirk.
       COMMITTEE ON VETERANS' AFFAIRS: Mr. Burr, Mr. Isakson, Mr. 
     Wicker, Mr. Johanns, Mr. Brown, Mr. Graham, and Mr. Kirk.
