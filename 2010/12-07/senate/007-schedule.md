# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, Senators should be prepared to be in the 
Chamber throughout the day on the impeachment trial of Judge G. Thomas 
Porteous, Jr. At 12:30 p.m., the Senate will proceed to legislative 
session for a period of morning business, with Senator LeMieux 
permitted to speak for up to 15 minutes. Following his remarks, the 
Senate will recess until 2:30 p.m. to allow for the weekly caucus 
meetings. When the Senate reconvenes, there will be a mandatory live 
quorum to resume the court of impeachment. There may be another live 
quorum at 5:30 this evening to begin the closed session deliberations.

                          ____________________
```
