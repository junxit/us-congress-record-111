# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Hastings, Alcee L. (H000324) [D-FL]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ALCEE L. HASTINGS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 7, 2010

  Mr. HASTINGS of Florida. Madam Speaker, on December 2, 2010, I was on 
official leave to attend to a medical matter. I was unable to

[[Page E2076]]

cast votes on the extension of the middle class tax cuts, as well as 
the censure of Representative Charles Rangel. However, I strongly 
support extending tax cuts to the middle class, and would have voted 
favorably. On the matter of Representative Rangel, I would have voted 
``yes'' on the reprimand but ``no'' on the censure.
```
