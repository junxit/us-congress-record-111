# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 28, 2010

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 28, 2010

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until 10 a.m. on Friday, 
May 28; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate proceed to a 
period of morning business, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 
10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
