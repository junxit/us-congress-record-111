# NATIONAL SECURITY STRATEGY--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
 NATIONAL SECURITY STRATEGY--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED 
                                 STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Andrews) laid before the House the 
following message from the President of the United States; which was 
read and, together with the accompanying papers, referred to the 
Committee on Armed Services:

To the Congress of the United States:
  Consistent with section 108 of the National Security Act of 1947, as 
amended (50 U.S.C. 404a), I am transmitting the National Security 
Strategy of the United States.
                                                        Barack Obama.  
The White House. May 27, 2010.

                          ____________________
```
