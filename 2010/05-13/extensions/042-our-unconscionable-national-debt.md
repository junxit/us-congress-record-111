                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE COFFMAN

                              of colorado

                  in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 13, 2010

  Mr. COFFMAN of Colorado. Madam Speaker, today our national debt is 
$12,923,161,977,864.28.
