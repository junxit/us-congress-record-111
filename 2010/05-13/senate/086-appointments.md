# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the majority and 
minority leaders of the Senate and the Speaker and minority leader of 
the House of Representatives, pursuant to Section 301 of Public Law 
104-1, as amended by Public Law 108-349, and further amended by Public 
Law 111-114, announces the joint re-appointment of the following

L. Holzwarth of Illinois.

93-642, appoints the Senator from Alaska (Mr. Begich) to be a member of 
the Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation Board of Trustees, vice the 
Senator from Montana (Mr. Baucus).
```
