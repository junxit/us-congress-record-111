                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. REICHERT. Mr. Speaker, as indicated in the Leave of Absence 
request granted by the U.S. House of Representatives, I was not in 
attendance for votes February 22-26, 2010, so that I could support my 
family through the tragic and unexpected death of my 16-year-old niece.
  Were I in attendance, I would have voted in favor of H.R. 4425 (RC 
No. 49); H.R. 4238 (RC No. 50), H. Res. 1066 (RC No. 52), H. Res. 1059 
(RC No. 53), H. Res. 1039 (RC No. 54), H. Res. 1046 (RC No. 55),

64), H. Res. 1085 (RC No. 65), Concurring with the Senate Amendments to 
H.R. 3961 (RC No. 67), H. Con. Res. 227 (RC No. 68), Hastings (FL) 
Amendment to H.R. 2701 (RC No. 70), Schauer Amendment to H.R. 2701 (RC 
No. 71,), the Motion to Recommit H.R. 2701 (RC No. 72), and H. Con.
