# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
OFFICE OF PROFESSIONAL RESPONSIBILITY INVESTIGATION
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
the Office of Professional Responsibility Investigation into the Office 
of Legal Counsel Memoranda, after receiving testimony from Gary G. 
Grindler, Acting Deputy Attorney General, Department of Justice.
DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS BUDGET
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine the President's proposed budget request for fiscal year 2011 
for the Department of Veterans Affairs, after receiving testimony from 
Eric K. Shinseki, Secretary of Veterans Affairs; John Wilson, Disabled 
American Veterans, Eric A. Hilleman, Veterans of Foreign Wars of the 
United States, Steve A. Robertson, American Legion, and Carl Blake, 
Paralyzed Veterans of America, all of Washington, D.C.; Raymond C. 
Kelley, AMVETS, Lanham, Maryland; and Rick Weidman, Vietnam Veterans of 
America, Silver Spring, Maryland.
```
