# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.R. 3961

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  Under the order of the Senate of January 6, 2009, the Secretary of 
the Senate, on today, February 26, 2010, during the adjournment of the 
Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives announcing 
that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 3961. An act to extend expiring provisions of the USA 
     PATRIOT Improvement and Reauthorization Act of 2005 and 
     Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004 
     until February 28, 2011.

                          ____________________
```
