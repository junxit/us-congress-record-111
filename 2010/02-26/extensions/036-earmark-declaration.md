  Mr. BROWN of South Carolina. Madam Speaker, in accordance with the

explanation of the Fuel Oil Barge.
  Bill Number: H.R. 3326, Department of Defense Appropriations Act, 
2010
  Account: Navy Other Procurement
  Legal Name of Requesting Entity: Maybank Industries
  Address of Requesting Entity: 525 East Bay Street, Suite 200,

  Description of Project: The Navy needs to replace the existing fleet 
of aging Fuel Oil Barges (YON) with new vessels to meet fleet 
requirements and to comply with EPA requirements. The current fleet of 
Fuel Oil Barges (YONs) is nearly 40 years old, which is well beyond the 
intended service life, and maintenance costs are increasing 
substantially.
