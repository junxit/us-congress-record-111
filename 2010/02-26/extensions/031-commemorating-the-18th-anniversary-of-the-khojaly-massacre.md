# COMMEMORATING THE 18TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE KHOJALY MASSACRE

**Speaking:** Lofgren, Zoe (L000397) [D-CA]

```
       COMMEMORATING THE 18TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE KHOJALY MASSACRE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ZOE LOFGREN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 26, 2010

  Ms. ZOE LOFGREN of California. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
recognize the 18th anniversary of the massacre at Khojaly and to honor 
the 613 Azerbaijanis who died under these tragic circumstances. I ask 
that my colleagues join me in remembering those who lost their lives 
that day.
```
