# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 21, 2010

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 21, 2010

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m. on Monday, June 
21; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day; and following 
any leader remarks, there be a period of morning business until 5:15 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with 
no motions in order; that following morning business, the Senate 
proceed to executive session as provided for under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
