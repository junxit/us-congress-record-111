# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Bayh, Evan (B001233) [D-IN]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. BAYH. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that Senator Bayh 
be authorized to sign any duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions on 
Wednesday, December 22, and that Senator Lincoln be authorized to sign 
any duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions on Thursday, December 23, 
and Friday, December 24.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
