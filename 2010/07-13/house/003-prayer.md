# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord God without beginning or end, in the passing scene of life, help 
the Members of Congress to keep focused on the public trust they have 
been given.
  May they make just and prudent decisions that will strengthen this 
Nation in its constitutional integrity and bring peace and prosperity 
in our day.
  With Your blessing, may each moment of this session of Congress be 
dedicated to justice, and may our public service give You glory, both 
now and forever.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
