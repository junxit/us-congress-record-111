# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, tomorrow the Senate, as I indicated, will 
resume consideration of the START treaty. Senators are encouraged to 
come forth to offer and debate their amendments. Rollcall votes are 
possible before and after the closed session.

again--we must complete the funding resolution on Tuesday because after 
midnight, the funding runs out for our country. So we will have those 
cloture votes Tuesday unless we can expedite them with unanimous 
consent prior to that time.
```
