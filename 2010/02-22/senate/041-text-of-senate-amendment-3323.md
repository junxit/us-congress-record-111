# Text of Senate Amendment 3323

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

```
  SA 3323. Mr. BROWN of Ohio (for Mrs. Boxer (for herself and Mr.

political protesters; as follows:

     rape and assault of women in Guinea and the killing of
```
