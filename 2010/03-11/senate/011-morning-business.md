# MORNING BUSINESS

```

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there

controlling the first 30 minutes and the Republicans controlling the 
next 30 minutes.

  Mr. KAUFMAN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent to speak for up 
to 25 minutes.
```
