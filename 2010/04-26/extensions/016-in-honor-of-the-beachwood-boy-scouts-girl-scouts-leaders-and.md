# IN HONOR OF THE BEACHWOOD BOY SCOUTS, GIRL SCOUTS, LEADERS AND PARENTS

```
 IN HONOR OF THE BEACHWOOD BOY SCOUTS, GIRL SCOUTS, LEADERS AND PARENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOHN H. ADLER

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 26, 2010

  Mr. ADLER of New Jersey. Madam Speaker, I would like to congratulate 
the Boy Scouts, Girl Scouts, Leaders and Parents of Beachwood for 
receiving the 2010 Beachwood Citizen of the Year Award.
  Through their excellence in giving back to the Beachwood community 
and committing to their personal development and aspirations, the 
Beachwood Girl and Boy Scouts have displayed dedication to the highest 
standards and best traditions of American citizenship. I am confident 
that they will continue to accomplish great things in the future and 
prove to be successful and productive members of society.
  I am proud to have such hardworking constituents and I thank them for 
their dedication and tremendous service throughout the years. Madam 
Speaker, I hope you will join me in congratulating these exceptional 
Scouts, their leaders, and their parents for all the contributions as 
they are honored as ``Citizens of the Year.''

                          ____________________
```
