WALL STREET AND THE FINANCIAL CRISIS
Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: On Friday, 
April 23, 2010, Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations concluded a 
hearing to examine Wall Street and the financial crisis, focusing on 
the role of credit rating agencies, after receiving testimony from 
Susan Barnes, and Peter D'Erchia, both of Standard and Poor's, Yuri 
Yoshizawa, and Raymond W. McDaniel, both of Moody's Investors Service, 
and Kathleen A. Corbet, all of New York, New York; Frank L. Raiter, 
Woodville, Virginia; Richard Michalek, Brooklyn, New York; Eric 
Kolchinsky, Westfield, New Jersey; and Arturo Cifuentes, Santiago, 
Chile.
