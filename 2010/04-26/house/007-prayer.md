# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Ever-present God, who knows us through and through, hasten to help us 
and strengthen the faith and unity of Your people.
  Give us courage to attack what is evil and surrounds itself with 
negativity. History shows us You will fortify the just, lift up the 
lowly, and cleanse the pure of heart.
  Empower us to accomplish what is good and give You the glory now and 
forever. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
