# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 30, 2010

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 30, 2010

  Ms. LANDRIEU. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on 
Friday, July 30; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that following any leader remarks, the Senate proceed to a period 
of morning business, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 
10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
