# TRIBUTE TO ROBERT BERRY

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ROBERT BERRY

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I wish to recognize Robert 
Berry, an intern in my Aberdeen, SD, office, for all of the hard work 
he has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the 
past several months.
  Robert is a graduate of Aberdeen Central High School in Aberdeen, SD. 
Currently, he is attending the University of Minnesota, where he is 
majoring in political science. He is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Robert 
for all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
