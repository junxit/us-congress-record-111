# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, following leader remarks, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the small business jobs bill. There will be an 
hour of debate prior to a rollcall vote on a motion to invoke cloture. 
The hour will be equally divided and controlled between the two leaders 
or their designees, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 
minutes each. The final 10-minute block is reserved for the two 
leaders, with the majority leader controlling the final 5 minutes. 
Senators should expect a cloture vote around 10:40 this morning.
```
