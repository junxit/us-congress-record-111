# SENATE ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 1749, S. 1789

```
                      SENATE ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  The Speaker announced her signature to enrolled bills of the Senate 
of the following titles:

       S. 1749. An act to amend title 18, United States Code, to 
     prohibit the possession or use of cell phones and similar 
     wireless devices by Federal prisoners.
       S. 1789. An act to restore fairness to Federal cocaine
```
