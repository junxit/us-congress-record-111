# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the FAA bill. It is hopeful we will reach an 
agreement on the slots issue, which has created some controversy in

that will allow us to move forward to passage of this bill on Monday.
```
