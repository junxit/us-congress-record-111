# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 3961, H.R. 4532

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D118)
  H.R. 3961, to extend expiring provisions of the USA PATRIOT 
Improvement and Reauthorization Act of 2005 and Intelligence Reform and 
Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004 until February 28, 2011. Signed on 
February 27, 2010. (Public Law 111-141)
  H.R. 4532, to provide for permanent extension of the attorney fee 
withholding procedures under title II of the Social Security Act to 
title XVI of such Act, and to provide for permanent extension of such 
procedures under titles II and XVI of such Act to qualified non-
attorney representatives. Signed on February 27, 2010. (Public Law 111-
142)
```
