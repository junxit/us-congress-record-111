# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Kerry, John F. (K000148) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3082

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. KERRY. Mr. President, Senators should expect the first vote of 
the day to begin at approximately 10:15 a.m. tomorrow. That vote will 
be on the motion to invoke cloture on the motion to concur with respect 
to H.R. 3082 which is the vehicle for the continuing resolution. 
Following the vote, Senator Specter will deliver his farewell remarks 
to the Senate.
  Upon disposition of the CR, the Senate will vote on the motion to 
invoke cloture on the New START treaty. We also have an agreement to 
consider the Pearson and Martinez nominations and we could debate and 
vote on those tomorrow afternoon.

                          ____________________
```
