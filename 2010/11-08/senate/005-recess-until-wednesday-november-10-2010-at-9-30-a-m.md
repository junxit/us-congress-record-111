# RECESS UNTIL WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 10, 2010, AT 9:30 A.M.

```
        RECESS UNTIL WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 10, 2010, AT 9:30 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will stand in recess until 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday, November 10, 
2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12 and 30 seconds p.m. recessed until 
Wednesday, November 10, 2010, at 9:30 a.m.
```
