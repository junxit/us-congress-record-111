# RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, OCTOBER 15, 2010, AT 10 A.M.

```
           RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, OCTOBER 15, 2010, AT 10 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 10 a.m., Friday, October 15, 2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 29 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Friday, October 15, 2010, at 10 a.m.
```
