# BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT PRIOR TO SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

```
BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT PRIOR TO SINE DIE 
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House, reports that prior to sine 
die adjournment of the First Session, 111th Congress, on December 19, 
2009 she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval the following bill and joint resolution.

       H.R. 3326. Making appropriations for the Department of 
     Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2010, and 
     for other purposes.
       H.J. Res. 64. Making further continuing appropriations for 
     fiscal year 2010, and for other purposes.

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House, further reports that on 
December 23, 2009, she presented to the President of the United States, 
for his approval, the following bill.

       H.R. 4284. To extend the Generalized System of Preferences 
     and the Andean Trade Preference Act, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
