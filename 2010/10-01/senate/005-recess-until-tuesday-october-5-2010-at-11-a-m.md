# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 5, 2010, AT 11 A.M.

```
           RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 5, 2010, AT 11 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 11 a.m., Tuesday, October 5, 2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11:30 and 28 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, October 5, 2010, at 11 a.m.
```
