# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 19, 2010, AT 12 NOON

```
           RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 19, 2010, AT 12 NOON

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 12 noon, Tuesday, October 19, 2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 29 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, October 19, 2010, at 12 noon.
```
