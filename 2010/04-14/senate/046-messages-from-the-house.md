# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 4573, H.R. 4887

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  At 10:57 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker has 
signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 4887. An act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to ensure that health coverage provided by the 
     Department of Defense is treated as minimal essential 
     coverage.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Byrd).
                                  ____

  At 4:55 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House 
agrees to the amendments of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 4573) to urge 
the Secretary of the Treasury to instruct the United States Executive 
Directors at the International Monetary Fund, the World Bank, the 
Inter-American Development Bank, and other multilateral development 
institutions to use the voice, vote, and influence of the United States 
to cancel immediately and completely Haiti's debts to such 
institutions, and for other purposes.


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  At 6:59 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker has 
signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 4573. An act to urge the Secretary of the Treasury to 
     instruct the United States Executive Directors at the 
     International Monetary Fund, the World Bank, the Inter-
     American Development Bank, and other multilateral development 
     institutions to use the voice, vote, and influence of the 
     United States to cancel immediately and completely Haiti's 
     debts to such institutions, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
