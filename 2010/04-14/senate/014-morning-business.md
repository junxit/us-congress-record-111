# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period of morning business for 1 hour, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
Republicans controlling the first 30 minutes and the majority 
controlling the final 30 minutes.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Florida is 
recognized.
```
