# TRIBUTE TO MARK LEET

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                          TRIBUTE TO MARK LEET

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I rise to honor Mr. Mark Leet of 
Flemingsburg, KY, for recently receiving the middle school Teacher of 
the Year award presented by the Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United 
States, VFW. Mr. Leet was recognized by the VFW for his dedication to 
educating students about the importance of citizenship and patriotism.
  Today, I wish to honor Mr. Leet's dedication to the children of the 
Commonwealth and congratulate him on this well deserved award.
```
