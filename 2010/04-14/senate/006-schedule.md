# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, today, following the remarks by Senators 
regarding the tragedy in Poland--and we appreciate very much their 
being here--there will be a period of morning business for 1 hour, with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each. The Republicans 
will control the first 30 minutes, and the majority will control the 
final 30 minutes.
  Following morning business, the Senate will resume consideration of 
H.R. 4851, the Continuing Extension Act, with the time until 12:30 p.m. 
equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees. If a point of order is raised against the pending Baucus 
amendment, at 12:30 p.m. the Senate will proceed to a vote on the 
motion to waive the Budget Act.

                          ____________________
```
