# RECOGNITION OF THE MINORITY LEADER

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                   RECOGNITION OF THE MINORITY LEADER

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Republican leader is 
recognized.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I thank the Senator from Tennessee for 
his update on flood damage in Tennessee. The Commonwealth of Kentucky 
was impacted as well, not quite as severely but in a significant 
fashion. It was gratifying to get the report from the Senator from 
Tennessee about the status of the flood damage in his State.
```
