# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 11, 2010

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 11, 2010

  Mr. DODD. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. Tuesday, 
May 11; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, the Senate resume consideration of S. 3217, Wall Street reform, as 
provided for under the previous order; and finally I ask unanimous 
consent that the Senate recess from 12:30 until 2:15 p.m. to allow for 
the weekly caucus luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
