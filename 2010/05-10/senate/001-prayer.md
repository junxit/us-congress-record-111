# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty everlasting God, in Your light we would see life. Open the 
eyes of our lawmakers so that they can see the path on which You want 
them to travel. Lord, strengthen them for their daily work and minister 
to their deepest needs. In their moments of perplexity, fill them with 
the spirit of Your wisdom so that their decisions will reflect Your 
guidance. Use our Senators to discover and communicate Your answers to 
our Nation and world.
  We pray in Your holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
