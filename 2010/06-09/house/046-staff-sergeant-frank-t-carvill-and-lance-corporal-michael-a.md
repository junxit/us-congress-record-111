 STAFF SERGEANT FRANK T. CARVILL AND LANCE CORPORAL MICHAEL A. SCHWARZ 
                          POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and passing the bill (H.R. 5133) to designate the 
facility of the United States Postal Service located at 331 1st Street 
in Carlstadt, New Jersey, as the ``Staff Sergeant Frank T. Carvill and 
Lance Corporal Michael A. Schwarz Post Office Building''.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentlewoman from California (Ms. Chu) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
  The question was taken.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. In the opinion of the Chair, two-thirds 
being in the affirmative, the ayes have it.
