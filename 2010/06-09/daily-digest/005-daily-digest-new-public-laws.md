# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 5128, H.R. 5139

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D629)
  H.R. 5128, to designate the United States Department of the Interior 
Building in Washington, District of Columbia, as the ``Stewart Lee 
Udall Department of the Interior Building''. Signed on June 8, 2010.

and Herzegovina and the International Civilian Office in Kosovo. Signed 
on June 8, 2010. (Public Law 111-177)
```
