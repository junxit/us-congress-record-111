# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S.J. RES. 26

```
               UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S.J. RES. 26

  Mr. SANDERS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
with respect to Senate consideration of S.J. Res. 26 be modified to 
provide that the debate time on the motion to proceed be allotted in 
30-minute alternating blocks, with Senator Murkowski controlling the 
first 30-minute block, and with the first block commencing at 9:45 
a.m., Thursday, June 10.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
