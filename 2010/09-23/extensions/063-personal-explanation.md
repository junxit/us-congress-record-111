# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Maloney, Carolyn B. (M000087) [D-NY]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. CAROLYN B. MALONEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 23, 2010

  Mrs. MALONEY. Madam Speaker, on September 22, 2010, I missed rollcall 
votes Nos. 532 and 533. Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' 
on rollcall votes No. 532, to establish Coltsville National Historical 
Park in the State of Connecticut and, No. 533, to authorize funding for 
the creation and implementation of infant mortality pilot programs in 
standard metropolitan statistical areas with high rates of infant 
mortality.
```
