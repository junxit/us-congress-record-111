# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 3978

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House, reports that on September 22, 
2010, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bill.

       H.R. 3978. To amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to 
     authorize the Secretary of Homeland Security to accept and 
     use gifts for otherwise authorized activities of the Center 
     for Domestic Preparedness that are related to preparedness 
     for a response to terrorism, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
