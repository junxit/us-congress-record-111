# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S. 32, H.Con.Res. 109

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 4 of rule XXII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       291. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Louisiana, relative to House 
     Concurrent Resolution No. 109 memorializing the United States 
     Congress to take such actions as are necessary to enact 
     legislation that will result in meaningful reforms to the 
     regulation of the financial services industry; to the 
     Committee on Financial Services.
       292. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Louisiana, relative to Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 32 
     supporting efforts whether state, local or private, to 
     utilize outreach methods to contact, counsel, and refer 
     veterans and their family members; to the Committee on 
     Veterans' Affairs.
```
