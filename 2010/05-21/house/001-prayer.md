# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  In You, Lord, we find the fullness of life. You guide us daily on the 
path that leads to salvation.
  Send the power of Your Holy Spirit upon us, that this Congress will 
prove faithful to its constitutional commitments, and bring peace and 
security to the people of this Nation.
  Empower Your people to do Your will so that we will place all the 
more our trust in You, now and forever.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
