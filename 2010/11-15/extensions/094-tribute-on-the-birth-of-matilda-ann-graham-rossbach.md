# TRIBUTE ON THE BIRTH OF MATILDA ANN GRAHAM ROSSBACH

```
          TRIBUTE ON THE BIRTH OF MATILDA ANN GRAHAM ROSSBACH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, November 15, 2010

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Madam Speaker, I am happy to 
congratulate Jason Rossbach and his wife Laurie on the birth of their 
new daughter Matilda Ann Graham Rossbach. Matilda was born on November 
2, 2010, and is named after her two grandmothers.
  She is 7 pounds and 20 inches of pride and joy to her parents, who I 
wish all the best with this new blessing.

                          ____________________
```
