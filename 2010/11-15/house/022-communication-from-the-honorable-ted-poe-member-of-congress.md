# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE TED POE, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
      COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE TED POE, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Ted Poe, Member of Congress:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, November 3, 2010.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to formally notify you, 
     pursuant to rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I have received a subpoena for 
     documents issued by the United States District Court for the 
     Southern District of Texas.
       After consulting with the Office of General Counsel, I will 
     make the determinations required by Rule VIII.
           Sincerely,
                                                          Ted Poe,
     Member of Congress, Texas.

                          ____________________
```
