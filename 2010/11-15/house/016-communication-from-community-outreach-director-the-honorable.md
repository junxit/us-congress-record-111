```
   COMMUNICATION FROM COMMUNITY OUTREACH DIRECTOR, THE HONORABLE TOM 
                     McCLINTOCK, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

Honorable Tom McClintock, Member of Congress:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,
```
