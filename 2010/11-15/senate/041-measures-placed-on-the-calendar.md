# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** H.R. 847, H.R. 4168, H.R. 4337

```
                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following bills were read the second time, and placed on the 
calendar:

       H.R. 847. An act to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     extend and improve protections and services to individuals 
     directly impacted by the terrorist attack in New York City on 
     September 11, 2001, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 4168. An act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to expand the definition of cellulosic biofuel to 
     include algae-based biofuel for purposes of the cellulosic 
     biofuel producer credit and the special allowance for 
     cellulosic biofuel plant property.
       H.R. 4337. An act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to modify certain rules applicable to regulated 
     investment companies, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
