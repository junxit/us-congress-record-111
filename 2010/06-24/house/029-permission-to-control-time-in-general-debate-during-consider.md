# PERMISSION TO CONTROL TIME IN GENERAL DEBATE DURING CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5175

**Speaking:** Brady, Robert A. (B001227) [D-PA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1468, H.R. 5175

```
 PERMISSION TO CONTROL TIME IN GENERAL DEBATE DURING CONSIDERATION OF 
                               H.R. 5175

  Mr. BRADY of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that, during consideration of H.R. 5175 pursuant to House Resolution 
1468, the gentleman from Michigan (Mr. Conyers), or his designee, may 
control 10 minutes of the general debate time allocated to the chair of 
the Committee on House Administration.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
