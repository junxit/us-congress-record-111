# RACHEL OLSSON

```
                             RACHEL OLSSON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 24, 2010

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Rachel Olsson who has received the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service 
Ambassadors for Youth award. Rachel Olsson is a 9th grader at Faith 
Christian Academy and received this award because her determination and 
hard work have allowed her to overcome adversities.
  The dedication demonstrated by Rachel Olsson is exemplary of the type 
of achievement that can be attained with hard work and perseverance. It 
is essential students at all levels strive to make the most of their 
education and develop a work ethic which will guide them for the rest 
of their lives.
  I extend my deepest congratulations once again to Rachel Olsson for 
winning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth award. I 
have no doubt she will exhibit the same dedication and character to all 
her future accomplishments.
```
