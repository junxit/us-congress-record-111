# DUPREE, SD

```
                               DUPREE, SD

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Dupree, SD. 
Founded in 1910, the town of Dupree celebrated its 100th anniversary 
this year.
  Located in Ziebach County, Dupree possesses the strong sense of 
community that makes South Dakota an outstanding place to live and 
work. Throughout its rich history, Dupree has continued to be a strong 
reflection of South Dakota's greatest values and traditions. The 
community of Dupree has much to be proud of and I am confident that 
Dupree's success will continue well into the future.
  The town of Dupree commemorated its 100th anniversary July 6 through 
11 with a variety of events such as a Centennial Wagon/Trail Ride, a 
rodeo and a fireworks display. I would like to offer my congratulations 
to the citizens of Dupree on this milestone anniversary and send best 
wishes to them in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
