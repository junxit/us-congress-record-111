# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Lord of life, we magnify Your Name this day, for Your mercies are new 
every morning.
  Take our Senators by the hand and lead them on the road You desire 
them to travel. Help them to seek Your guidance as they establish their 
priorities, always remembering their accountability to You. May this 
accountability motivate them to never deviate from the path of 
integrity but to seek to ensure that Your will is done on Earth even as 
it is done in heaven. Remind them that You are with them and will guide 
them to a desired destination.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
