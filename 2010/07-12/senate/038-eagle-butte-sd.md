# EAGLE BUTTE, SD

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                            EAGLE BUTTE, SD

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Eagle Butte, SD. 
Founded in 1910, the town of Eagle Butte will celebrate its 100th 
anniversary this year.
  Located in Dewey and Ziebach Counties, Eagle Butte possesses the 
strong sense of community that makes South Dakota an outstanding place 
to live and work. Eagle Butte began 100 years ago as a railroad town; 
and throughout its rich history, Eagle Butte has continued to be a 
strong reflection of South Dakota's greatest values and traditions. The 
community of Eagle Butte has much to be proud of and I am confident 
that Eagle Butte's success will continue well into the future.
  The town of Eagle Butte will commemorate the 100th anniversary of its 
founding with celebrations held on July 15 through July 18. I would 
like to offer my congratulations to the citizens of Eagle Butte on this 
milestone anniversary and wish them continued prosperity in the years 
to come.●

                          ____________________
```
