                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BOB ETHERIDGE

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 15, 2010

  Mr. ETHERIDGE. Madam Speaker, I was unable to cast recorded votes on 
Tuesday, December 14, on rollcall votes 628, 629, and 630. Had I been

Headquarters National Historic Site; S. 3167, the Census Oversight 
Efficiency and Management Reform Act of 2010; and H.R. 6510, which 
allows the Military Museum of Texas to purchase the property on which 
it operates.
