# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENT TO HOUSE AMENDMENT TO SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 4853, TAX RELIEF, UNEMPLOYMENT INSURANCE REAUTHORIZATION, AND JOB CREATION ACT OF 2010

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENT TO 
     HOUSE AMENDMENT TO SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 4853, TAX RELIEF, 
  UNEMPLOYMENT INSURANCE REAUTHORIZATION, AND JOB CREATION ACT OF 2010

  Mr. POLIS (during consideration of S. 987), from the Committee on 
Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 111-682) on the 
resolution (H. Res. 1766) providing for consideration of the Senate 
amendment to the House amendment to the Senate amendment to the bill 
(H.R. 4853) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the 
funding and expenditure authority of the Airport and Airway Trust Fund, 
to amend title 49, United States Code, to extend authorizations for the 
airport improvement program, and for other purposes, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
