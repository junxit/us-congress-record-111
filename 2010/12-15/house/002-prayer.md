# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord God, in this season filled with Your Spirit, enable Your people 
to manifest love in their deeds. Strengthen them to hold onto the truth 
both in their minds and in their speech. May their joyful convictions 
and personal commitments be proven in every decision and external 
behavior and not merely expressed in talk.
  No matter what conscience may charge them with, You, Eternal God, are 
greater than any human longing. All is known to You, both now and 
forever.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
