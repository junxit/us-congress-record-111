# ORDER FOR PRINTING OF TRIBUTES

```
                     ORDER FOR PRINTING OF TRIBUTES

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the printing of tributes be modified to provide that Members have until 
sine die of the 111th Congress, 2d session, to submit tributes and that 
the order for printing remain in effect.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
