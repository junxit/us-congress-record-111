# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 12, 2010, AT 10 A.M.

```
           RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 12, 2010, AT 10 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 10 a.m., on Tuesday, October 12, 2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11:30 and 45 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, October 12, 2010, at 10 a.m.
```
