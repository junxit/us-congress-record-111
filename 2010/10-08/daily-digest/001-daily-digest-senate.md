# Daily Digest/Senate

```
                                            Friday, October 8, 2010

[[Page D1057]]

                              Daily Digest

                                 Senate


Chamber Action
  Senate met at 11:30:16 a.m. in pro forma session, and recessed at 
11:30:45 a.m. until 10 a.m., on Tuesday, October 12, 2010.
(Committees not listed did not meet)
VETERANS' AFFAIRS IT PROGRAM
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: On Wednesday, October 6, 2010, 
committee concluded an oversight hearing to examine Veterans' Affairs 
Information Technology (IT) program, focusing on looking ahead, after 
receiving testimony from Roger W. Baker, Assistant Secretary for 
Information and Technology, and Belinda J. Finn, Assistant Inspector 
General for Audits and Evaluations, Office of Inspector General, both 
of the Department of Veterans' Affairs; Edward F. Meagher, Computer 
Sciences Corporation, Falls Church, Virginia; Glenn Tullman, 
Allscripts, Raleigh, North Carolina; and Tom Munnecke, Encinitas, 
California.
RETIREMENT SECURITY IN AMERICA
Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions: On Thursday, 
October 7, 2010, committee concluded a hearing to examine retirement 
security in America, after receiving testimony from Phyllis Borzi, 
Assistant Secretary of Labor for Employee Benefits Security 
Administration; Jack VanDerhei, Employee Benefit Research Institute; 
and Ross Eisenbrey, Economic Policy Institute, both of Washington, DC; 
and Shareen Miller, Falls Church, Virginia.
```
