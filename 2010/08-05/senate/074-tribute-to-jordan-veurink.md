# TRIBUTE TO JORDAN VEURINK

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO JORDAN VEURINK

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I wish to recognize Jordan 
Veurink, an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard 
work he has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over 
the past several weeks.
  Jordan is a graduate of the University of Sioux Falls in Sioux Falls, 
SD. Currently, he is attending Texas Wesleyan University School of Law. 
He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out of 
his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Jordan for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
