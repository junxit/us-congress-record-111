# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider en bloc Calendar Nos. 1025, 
1026, and 1029; that the nominations be confirmed en bloc, the motions 
to reconsider be laid upon the table en bloc, the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action; further, that the action 
under rule XXXI reflect that Calendar No. 948 should be included, not 
958; and that the Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

       Peter Michael McKinley, of Virginia, a Career Member of the
```
