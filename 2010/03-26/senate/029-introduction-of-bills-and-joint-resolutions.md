# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. BROWN of Ohio (for himself and Mr. Wyden):
       S. 3189. A bill to amend title 49, United States Code, to 
     allow for additional transportation assistance grants; to the 
     Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.
           By Ms. LANDRIEU (for herself and Mr. Durbin):
       S. 3190. A bill to reaffirm that the Small Business 
     Reauthorization Act of 1997 does not limit a contracting 
     officer's discretion regarding whether to make a contract 
     available for award pursuant to any of the restricted 
     competition programs authorized by the Small Business Act; to 
     the Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship.
           By Mrs. HUTCHISON:
       S. 3191. A bill to reauthorize the Satellite Home Viewer 
     Extension and Reauthorization Act of 2004, and for other 
     purposes; considered and passed.
```
