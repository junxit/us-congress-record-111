DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION REQUEST AND FUTURE YEARS DEFENSE PROGRAM
Committee on Armed Services: Committee concluded a hearing to examine U.S. 
Pacific Command, U.S. Strategic Command, and U.S. Forces Korea in review of 
the Defense Authorization request for fiscal year 2011 and the Future Years 
Defense Program, after receiving testimony from Admiral Robert F. Willard, 
U.S. Navy, Commander, U.S. Pacific Command, General Kevin P. Chilton, 
Commander, United States Strategic Command, and General Walter L. Sharp, 
Commander, United Nations Command, Commander, Republic of Korea-United 
States Combined Forces, and Commander, United States Forces Korea, all of 
the Department of Defense.
