# PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE PRESIDENT

```
      PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE PRESIDENT

  Mr. REYES, from the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence, 
submitted an adverse privileged report (Rept. No. 111-402) on the 
resolution (H. Res. 978) requesting the President to transmit to the 
House of Representatives all documents in the possession of the 
President relating to the inventory and review of intelligence related 
to the shooting at Fort Hood, Texas, described by the President in a 
memorandum dated November 10, 2009, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
