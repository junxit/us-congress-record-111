# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO BOARD OF TRUSTEES FOR JOHN C. STENNIS CENTER FOR PUBLIC SERVICE TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

```
 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO BOARD OF TRUSTEES FOR JOHN C. STENNIS CENTER 
              FOR PUBLIC SERVICE TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 114(b) of the John C. 
Stennis Center for Public Service Training and Development Act (2 
U.S.C. 1103), and the order of the House of January 6, 2009, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's appointment of the following Member on the part 
of the House to the Board of Trustees for the John C. Stennis Center 
for Public Service Training and Development for a term of 6 years:
  Mr. Travis Childers, Booneville, Mississippi
```
