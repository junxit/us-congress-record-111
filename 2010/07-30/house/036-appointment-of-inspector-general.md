# APPOINTMENT OF INSPECTOR GENERAL

```
                    APPOINTMENT OF INSPECTOR GENERAL

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 6(b) of rule II, and the 
order of the House of January 6, 2009, the Chair announces that the 
Speaker, majority leader and minority leader jointly appoint Ms. 
Theresa M. Grafenstine, Manassas, Virginia, to the position of 
Inspector General for the U.S. House of Representatives effective July 
30, 2010.
```
