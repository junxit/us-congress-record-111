# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO CONGRESSIONAL AWARD BOARD

```
          APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO CONGRESSIONAL AWARD BOARD

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 803(a) of the 
Congressional Recognition for Excellence in Arts Education Act (2 
U.S.C. 803(a)), and the order of the House of January 6, 2009, the

the Congressional Award Board:

of the term ending September 25, 2011 and in addition,
```
