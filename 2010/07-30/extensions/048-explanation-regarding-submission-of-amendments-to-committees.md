# EXPLANATION REGARDING SUBMISSION OF AMENDMENTS TO COMMITTEES

**Speaking:** Sherman, Brad (S000344) [D-CA]

```
      EXPLANATION REGARDING SUBMISSION OF AMENDMENTS TO COMMITTEES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRAD SHERMAN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 30, 2010

  Mr. SHERMAN. Madam Speaker, I should note that I sometimes submit 
amendments to committees so that they are available for discussion. I 
do not necessarily support any amendment drafted by myself and my staff 
unless I formally offer the amendment. Accordingly, no conclusion can 
be drawn from the process of simply providing the text of a possible 
amendment to the clerk of any committee.

                          ____________________
```
