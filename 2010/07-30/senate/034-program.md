# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 1586

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. So, Mr. President, at approximately a quarter to 6 on 
Monday, the Senate will proceed to a cloture vote on the motion to 
concur with respect to H.R. 1586, the legislative vehicle for FMAP and 
teacher funding. Next week we have a lot of work to accomplish. In 
addition to the FMAP and education funding, we need to consider an 
energy bill, the nomination of Elena Kagan to be an Associate Justice 
of the Supreme Court, and there are other matters we are going to try 
to clear for action on the legislative and Executive Calendars. We feel 
hopeful we can complete business on the Small Business Administration 
legislation we have spent so much time on early next week.
```
