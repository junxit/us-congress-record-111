# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 1586

```
                 UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 1586

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I now ask unanimous consent that the cloture 
vote on the motion to concur in the House amendment to the Senate
```
