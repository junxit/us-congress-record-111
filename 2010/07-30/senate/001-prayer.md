# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, who gives life to the world, who breathed Your spirit into 
humanity, infuse the Members of this body with the spirit of Your 
wisdom. May this wisdom lead them to serve others with an awareness of 
their accountability to You. Help them to make it their primary goal to 
please You, using their talents for the good of others.
  Lord, be with those Senators who are experiencing ill health. Enable 
them to feel Your healing touch. May Your goodness and mercy follow us 
all the days of our lives.
  We pray in Your righteous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
