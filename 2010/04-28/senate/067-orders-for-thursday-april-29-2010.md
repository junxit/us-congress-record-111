# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 29, 2010

**Speaking:** Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

**Measures:** S. 3217

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 29, 2010

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 12:15 p.m., 
Thursday, April 29; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 3217, as provided for 
under the previous order.
```
