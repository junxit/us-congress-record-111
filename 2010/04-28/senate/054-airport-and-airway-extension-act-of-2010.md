# AIRPORT AND AIRWAY EXTENSION ACT OF 2010

```
                AIRPORT AND AIRWAY EXTENSION ACT OF 2010

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 5147, received from the 
House and at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 5147) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to extend the funding and expenditure authority of the 
     Airport and Airway Trust Fund, to amend title 49, United 
     States Code, to extend authorizations for the airport 
     improvement program, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
read three times, passed, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, and that any statements relating to the bill be printed in the 
Record, with no intervening action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 5147) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.

```
