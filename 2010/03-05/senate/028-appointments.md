                              APPOINTMENTS

minority leaders of the Senate and the Speaker and minority leader of

104-1, as amended by Public Law 108-349, and further amended by Public 
Law 111-114, announces the joint designation of the following

Compliance: Barbara L. Camens of the District of Columbia.

Senate and the Speaker and minority leader of the House of 
Representatives, pursuant to Section 301 of Public Law 104-1, as 
amended by Public Law 108-349, and further amended by Public Law 111-
114, announces the joint re-appointment of the following individuals as 
members of the Board of Directors of the Office of Compliance: Alan V. 
Friedman of California, Susan S. Robfogel of New York, and Barbara 
Childs Wallace of Mississippi.
