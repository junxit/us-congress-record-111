# PRINTING OF TRIBUTES TO RETIRING SENATORS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
               PRINTING OF TRIBUTES TO RETIRING SENATORS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there be 
printed as a Senate document a compilation of materials from the 
Congressional Record in tribute to retiring Members of the 111th 
Congress, and that Members have until Thursday, December 16, to submit 
such tributes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
