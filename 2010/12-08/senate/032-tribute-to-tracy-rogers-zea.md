# TRIBUTE TO TRACY ROGERS ZEA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO TRACY ROGERS ZEA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Tracy Rogers Zea, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Tracy is a graduate of Seton Catholic High School in Chandler, AZ. He 
is a recent graduate of South Dakota State University, where he majored 
in political science. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to 
getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Tracy 
for all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
