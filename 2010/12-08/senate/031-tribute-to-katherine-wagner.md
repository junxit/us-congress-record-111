# TRIBUTE TO KATHERINE WAGNER

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO KATHERINE WAGNER

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Katherine Wagner, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she 
has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Katherine is a graduate of Spearfish High School in Spearfish, SD. 
Currently, she is attending the University of South Dakota, where she 
is majoring in political science and mass communications. She is a hard 
worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to 
Katherine for all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued 
success in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
