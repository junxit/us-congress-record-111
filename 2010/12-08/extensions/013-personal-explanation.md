# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Chu, Judy (C001080) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1642

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JUDY CHU

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 8, 2010

  Ms. CHU. Madam Speaker, yesterday, I was unable to participate in 
rollcall vote No. 609. Had I been present, I would have voted ``yes'' 
on H. Res. 1642, Recognizing the centennial of the City of Lilburn, 
Georgia, and supporting the goals and ideals of a City Lilburn Day. 
This year the City of Lilburn celebrated its centennial anniversary and 
I am proud to honor its history.
```
