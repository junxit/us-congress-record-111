# RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, OCTOBER 22, 2010, AT 1 P.M.

```
            RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, OCTOBER 22, 2010, AT 1 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 1 p.m., Friday, October 22, 2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12 and 39 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Friday, October 22, 2010, at 1 p.m.
```
