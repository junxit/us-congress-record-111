# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE CAROLYN C. KILPATRICK, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
   COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE CAROLYN C. KILPATRICK, MEMBER OF 
                                CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Carolyn C. Kilpatrick, Member of 
Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, March 1, 2010.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I have been served with a grand jury 
     subpoena for testimony by the United States District Court 
     for the Eastern District of Michigan.
       After consulting with my attorney, I will make the 
     determinations required by Rule VIII.
           Sincerely,
                                            Carolyn C. Kilpatrick,
     Member of Congress.

                          ____________________
```
