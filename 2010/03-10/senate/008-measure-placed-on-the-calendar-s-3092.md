# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 3092

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 3092

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 3092

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, the bill, S. 3092, is at the desk. I 
understand it is due for a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the bill by 
title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3092) to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 5070 Vegas Valley Drive in 
     Las Vegas, Nevada, as the ``Joseph A. Ryan Post Office 
     Building.''

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I object to any further proceedings with 
respect to this bill.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be placed on the calendar.
  Mr. REID. Will the Chair now announce morning business.

                          ____________________
```
