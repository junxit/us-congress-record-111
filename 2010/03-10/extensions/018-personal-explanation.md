# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Woolsey, Lynn C. (W000738) [D-CA]

```

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LYNN C. WOOLSEY

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 10, 2010

  Ms. WOOLSEY. Madam Speaker, on March 9, 2010, I was unavoidably 
detained and was unable to record my vote for rollcall Nos. 92-94. Had 
I been present I would have voted:
  Rollcall No. 92 ``yes''--Harmful Algal Blooms and Hypoxia Research 
and Control Amendments Act.
  Rollcall No. 93 ``yes''--Congratulating Willard S. Boyle and George 
E. Smith for being awarded the Nobel Prize in physics.
  Rollcall No. 94 ``yes''--Honoring John E. Warnock, Charles M. 
Geschke, Forrest M. Bird, Esther Sans Takeuchi, and IBM Corporation for 
receiving the 2008 National Medal of Technology and Innovation.
```
