# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

**Measures:** H.R. 1299, H.R. 4154

```
                   AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 3333. Ms. LANDRIEU (for herself, Mr. Vitter, Mr. Wicker, 
     and Mr. Shelby) submitted an amendment intended to be 
     proposed by her to the bill H.R. 4154, to amend the Internal 
     Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the new carryover basis rules 
     in order to prevent tax increases and the imposition of 
     compliance burdens on many more estates than would benefit 
     from repeal, to retain the estate tax with a $3,500,000 
     exemption, to reinstitute and update the Pay-As-You-Go 
     requirement of budget neutrality on new tax and mandatory 
     spending legislation, enforced by the threat of annual, 
     automatic sequestration, and for other purposes; which was 
     ordered to lie on the table.
       SA 3334. Mr. DeMINT submitted an amendment intended to be 
     proposed to amendment SA 3326 proposed by Mr. Reid to the 
     bill H.R. 1299, to make technical corrections to the laws 
     affecting certain administrative authorities of the United 
     States Capitol Police, and for other purposes; which was 
     ordered to lie on the table.
```
