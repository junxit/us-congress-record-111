# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Res. 406, S.Res. 860, H.Res. 2499

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 4 of rule XXII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       232. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of the 
     Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, relative to Senate Resolution 
     860 urging the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) 
     to show temperance in the application of asset valuation to 
     minority owned banks; to the Committee on Financial Services.
       233. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, relative to House 
     Resolution No. 406 urging the Congress of the United States 
     to immediately consider House Resolution No. 2499; to the 
     Committee on Natural Resources.
```
