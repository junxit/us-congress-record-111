# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF THE SENATE AMENDMENTS TO H.R. 3961, MEDICARE PHYSICIAN PAYMENT REFORM ACT OF 2009

**Measures:** H.Res. 1109, H.R. 3961

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF THE SENATE 
 AMENDMENTS TO H.R. 3961, MEDICARE PHYSICIAN PAYMENT REFORM ACT OF 2009

  Mr. HASTINGS of Florida, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 111-420) on the resolution (H. Res. 1109) 
providing for consideration of the Senate amendments to the bill (H.R. 
3961) to amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to reform the 
Medicare SGR payment system for physicians and to reinstitute and 
update the Pay-As-You-Go requirement of budget neutrality on new tax 
and mandatory spending legislation, enforced by the threat of annual, 
automatic sequestration, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
