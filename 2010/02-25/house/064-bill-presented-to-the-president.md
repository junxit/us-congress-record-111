# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 4532

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House reports that on February 25, 
2010 she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bill.

       H.R. 4532. To provide for permanent extension of the 
     attorney fee withholding procedures under title II of the 
     Social Security Act to title XVI of such Act, and to provide 
     for permanent extension of such procedures under titles II 
     and XVI of such Act to qualified non-attorney 
     representatives.

                          ____________________
```
