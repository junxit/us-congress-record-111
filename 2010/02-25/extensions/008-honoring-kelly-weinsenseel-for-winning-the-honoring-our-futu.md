# HONORING KELLY WEINSENSEEL FOR WINNING THE HONORING OUR FUTURE LEADERS COMPETITION

**Speaking:** Israel, Steve (I000057) [D-NY]

```
HONORING KELLY WEINSENSEEL FOR WINNING THE HONORING OUR FUTURE LEADERS 
                              COMPETITION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEVE ISRAEL

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, February 25, 2010

  Mr. ISRAEL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge a student in 
my district, Kelly Weinsenseel, from West Babylon High School.
   Kelly will receive the Honoring Our Future Leaders Award on February 
27, 2010. To win this award, she wrote her own rendition of the ``I 
Have a Dream'' speech delivered by Martin Luther King, Jr.
   I am proud to honor Kelly for her academic and personal achievements 
and congratulate her upon the receipt of this prestigious award.

                          ____________________
```
