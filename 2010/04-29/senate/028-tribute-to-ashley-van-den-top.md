# TRIBUTE TO ASHLEY VAN DEN TOP

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO ASHLEY VAN DEN TOP

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Ashley Van Den 
Top, an intern in my Sioux Falls, SD, office, for all of the hard work 
she has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the 
past several months.
  Ashley is a graduate of Western Christian High School in Rock Valley, 
IA. Currently she is attending National American University, where she 
is majoring in paralegal studies. She is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of her internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Ashley 
for all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
