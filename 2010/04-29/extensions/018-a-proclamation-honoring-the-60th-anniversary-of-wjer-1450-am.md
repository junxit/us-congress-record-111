# A PROCLAMATION HONORING THE 60TH ANNIVERSARY OF WJER 1450 AM

**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

```
      A PROCLAMATION HONORING THE 60TH ANNIVERSARY OF WJER 1450 AM

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 29, 2010

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker,
  Whereas, WJER 1450 AM was founded and began broadcasting in 1950 and 
is celebrating its 60th anniversary; and
  Whereas, WJER AM has served as ``the Voice of the Valley'' by 
connecting the citizens of Dover, New Philadelphia, and Tuscarawas 
County for sixty years; and
  Whereas, WJER is committed to continuing their work of bringing news 
and entertainment to the communities of Tuscarawas County in years to 
come; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved that along with the residents of the 18th Congressional 
District, I commend WJER for six decades of service to the Tuscarawas 
Valley through the broadcast of national and local news, sports, and 
entertainment.

                          ____________________
```
