# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Mighty and wonderful are Your works, Lord God Almighty. Just and true 
are Your ways, O King of all the nations.
  Who would dare not to give You the honor and glory due Your Holy 
Name, O Lord.
  For You alone are holy, all nations shall come and worship in Your 
presence.
  Your mighty deeds are clearly seen both now and forever. Amen.
```
