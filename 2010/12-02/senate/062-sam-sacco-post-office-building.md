# SAM SACCO POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                     SAM SACCO POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6387) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 337 West Clark Street in Eureka, California, 
as the ``Sam Sacco Post Office Building,'' was ordered to a third 
reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
