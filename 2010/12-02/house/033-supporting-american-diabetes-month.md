# SUPPORTING AMERICAN DIABETES MONTH

```
                   SUPPORTING AMERICAN DIABETES MONTH

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Kirkpatrick of Arizona). The unfinished 
business is the question on suspending the rules and agreeing to the 
resolution (H. Res. 1690) supporting the observance of American 
Diabetes Month, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from New Jersey (Mr. Pallone) that the House suspend the 
rules and agree to the resolution, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the resolution, as amended, was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
