# COMMENDING THE NATO SCHOOL

**Measures:** H.Res. 527

```
                       COMMENDING THE NATO SCHOOL

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and agreeing to the resolution (H. Res. 527) 
commending the NATO School for its critical support of North Atlantic 
Treaty Organization (NATO) efforts to promote global peace, stability, 
and security, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Tennessee (Mr. Tanner) that the House suspend the rules 
and agree to the resolution, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the resolution, as amended, was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
