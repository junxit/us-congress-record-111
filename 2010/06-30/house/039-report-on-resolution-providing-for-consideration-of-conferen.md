# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 4173, DODD-FRANK WALL STREET REFORM AND CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 1490, H.R. 4173

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF CONFERENCE REPORT 
ON H.R. 4173, DODD-FRANK WALL STREET REFORM AND CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT

  Mr. McGOVERN, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 111-518) on the resolution (H. Res. 1490) providing 
for consideration of the conference report to accompany the bill (H.R. 
4173) to provide for financial regulatory reform, to protect consumers 
and investors, to enhance Federal understanding of insurance issues, to 
regulate the over-the-counter derivatives markets, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
