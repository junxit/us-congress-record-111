# NOTICE OF HEARING

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                    SUBCOMMITTEE ON WATER AND POWER

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that the hearing before the 
Subcommittee on Water and Power previously announced for July 1, has 
been rescheduled and will now be held on Wednesday, July 14, 2010, at 
3:30 p.m., in room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building.
  The purpose of this oversight hearing is to examine the Federal 
response to the discovery of the aquatic invasive species Asian carp in 
Lake Calumet, Illinois.
  Because of the limited time available for the hearing, witnesses may 
testify by invitation only. However, those wishing to submit written 
testimony for the hearing record should send it to the Committee on 
Energy and Natural Resources, United States Senate, Washington, DC 
20510-6150, or by email to Gina_W<span class="__cf_email__" data-cfemail="1c7975726f68737f775c7972796e7b65326f79727d6879327b736a">[email protected]</span>.
  For further information, please contact Tanya Trujillo or Gina 
Weinstock.

                          ____________________
```
