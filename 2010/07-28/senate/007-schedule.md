# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, there will be a 
period of morning business for 1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak 
for up to 10 minutes each. The Republicans will control the first 30 
minutes and the majority will control the final 30 minutes.
  Following morning business, we will resume consideration of the small 
business jobs bill. I will continue to work with the Republican leader 
today on an agreement to consider amendments to the bill. If we are 
able to reach an agreement, we will have votes on amendments today.
  Last night, I filed cloture on the substitute and the underlying 
bill, two cloture motions. As a result, the filing deadline for germane 
first-degree amendments is at 1 p.m. today.
  Senators will be notified when an agreement is reached and votes are 
scheduled.

                          ____________________
```
