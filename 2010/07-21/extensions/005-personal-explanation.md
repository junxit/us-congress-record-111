# PERSONAL EXPLANATION-

**Speaking:** Visclosky, Peter J. (V000108) [D-IN]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1491, H.Res. 1516, H.R. 5604

```
                         PERSONAL EXPLANATION-

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. PETER J. VISCLOSKY

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 21, 2010

  Mr. VISCLOSKY. Madam Speaker, on the evening of Tuesday, July 20, 
2010, I was absent from the House and missed rollcall votes 451 through 
453.
   Had I been present for rollcall 451, on a motion to suspend the 
rules and agree to H. Res. 1491, Congratulating the University of South 
Carolina Gamecocks on winning the 2010 NCAA Division I College World 
Series, I would have voted ``Aye.''
  Had I been present for rollcall 452, on a motion to suspend the rules 
and agree to H.R. 5604, the Surface Transportation Savings Act of 2010, 
I would have voted ``Aye.''
  Had I been present for rollcall 453, on a motion to suspend the rules 
and agree to H. Res. 1516, Recognizing the 65th Anniversary of the end 
of World War II, honoring the service members who fought in World War 
II and their families, and honoring the servicemembers who are 
currently serving in combat operations, I would have voted ``Aye.''
```
