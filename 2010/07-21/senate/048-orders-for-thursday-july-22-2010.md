# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 22, 2010

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 22, 2010

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 on Thursday, July 
22; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that following any leader remarks, the Senate proceed to S. Res. 
591, a resolution recognizing and honoring the 20th anniversary of the 
enactment of the Americans With Disabilities Act, as provided for under 
the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
