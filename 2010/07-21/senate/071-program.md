# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 83, S.Res. 591, H.R. 5297

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, there will be up to 2 hours for debate on 
the Americans With Disabilities resolution to be followed by up to 20 
minutes for debate on the Burmese Freedom and Democracy Act.
  Upon the use or yielding back of time, at approximately 12 noon 
tomorrow, the Senate will proceed to a series of two stacked rollcall 
votes on adoption of S. Res. 591, to be followed by a vote on passage 
of H.J. Res. 83, the Burmese Freedom and Democracy Act.
  Upon disposition of those matters, the Senate will resume 
consideration of H.R. 5297.

                          ____________________
```
