# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** H.R. 5301

```
                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following bill was read the first and second times by unanimous 
consent, and placed on the calendar:

       H.R. 5301. An act to extend the period during which the 
     Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency and 
     States are prohibited from requiring a permit under section 
     402 of the Federal Water Control Act for certain discharges 
     that are incidental to normal operation of vessels, to 
     reauthorize the National Estuary Program, and for other 
     purposes.
```
