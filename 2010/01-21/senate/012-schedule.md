# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 45

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, this morning, following leader remarks, 
the Senate will proceed to a period of morning business for an hour, 
with Senators allowed to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each. That 
time will be equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees. The Republicans will control the first half; the 
majority will control the final half. Following morning business, the 
Senate will resume consideration of H.J. Res. 45, a joint resolution 
increasing the statutory limit on the public debt. Currently, we have 
three amendments pending. We hope we can reach short time agreements so 
we can schedule votes on these amendments.

                          ____________________
```
