# PERSONAL STATEMENT ON REMOVAL AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 2499

```
        PERSONAL STATEMENT ON REMOVAL AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 2499

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. LYNN A. WESTMORELAND

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 12, 2010

  Mr. WESTMORELAND. Madam Speaker, I would like the Record to reflect 
my desire to be removed as a cosponsor of H.R. 2499, the Puerto Rico 
Democracy Act of 2009. Due to H.R. 2499's placement on the Union 
Calendar, I am unable to officially remove myself from this legislation 
according to the Rules of the U.S. House of Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
