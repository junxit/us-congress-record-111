# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 562

**Speaking:** Berkley, Shelley (B001231) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 562

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 562

  Ms. BERKLEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 562, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Neil Abercrombie of Hawaii, for 
the purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant 
to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Nevada?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
