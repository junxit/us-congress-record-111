# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 3951

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 3951. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 2000 Louisiana Avenue in New 
     Orleans, Louisiana, as the ``Roy Rondeno, Sr. Post Office 
     Building''.

  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 111-5, the 
Chair, on behalf of the Republican Leader, appoints the following 
individual to the Health Information Technology Policy Committee:
  Richard Chapman of Kentucky.

                          ____________________
```
