# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5297, SMALL BUSINESS JOBS AND CREDIT ACT OF 2010

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5297, 
               SMALL BUSINESS JOBS AND CREDIT ACT OF 2010

  Mr. ARCURI, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 111-508) on the resolution (H. Res. 1448) providing 
for further consideration of the bill (H.R. 5297) to create the Small 
Business Lending Fund Program to direct the Secretary of the Treasury 
to make capital investments in eligible institutions in order to 
increase the availability of credit for small businesses, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
