# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period of morning business for 1 hour, with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each and with the time equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders or their designees, with 
the Republicans controlling the first half and the majority controlling 
the final half.
  The Senator from Illinois.

                          ____________________
```
