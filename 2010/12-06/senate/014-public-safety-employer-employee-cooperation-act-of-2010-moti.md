# PUBLIC SAFETY EMPLOYER-EMPLOYEE COOPERATION ACT OF 2010--MOTION TO PROCEED

```
  PUBLIC SAFETY EMPLOYER-EMPLOYEE COOPERATION ACT OF 2010--MOTION TO 
                                PROCEED


                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I move to proceed to Calendar No. 662, S. 
3991, the Public Safety Employer-Employee Cooperation Act, and I have a 
cloture motion at the desk I wish reported.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close debate on the motion to 
     proceed to Calendar No. 662, S. 3991, the Public Safety 
     Employer-Employee Cooperation Act of 2010.
         Harry Reid, Patrick J. Leahy, Tom Harkin, Carl Levin, 
           Daniel K. Inouye, Richard J. Durbin, Byron L. Dorgan, 
           Jack Reed, Jeff Bingaman, Dianne Feinstein, Mark 
           Begich, Robert Menendez, Daniel K. Akaka, Sherrod 
           Brown, Sheldon Whitehouse, Patty Murray, Debbie 
           Stabenow, Barbara Boxer.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I now withdraw that motion.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The motion is withdrawn.
```
