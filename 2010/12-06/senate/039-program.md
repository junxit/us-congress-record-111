# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Let me make sure, Mr. President, that we have this down 
right. We are going to, at 12:30 p.m., proceed to legislative session 
for a period of morning business, and during that time there will only 
be one speaker--Senator LeMieux--who will speak for up to 15 minutes. 
Following his remarks, the Senate will recess until we complete our 
weekly caucus luncheons.
  Mr. President, Senators should be on the floor, as I have indicated, 
for a mandatory live quorum to begin the impeachment of Judge Porteous. 
There will be two additional live quorums throughout the day, one at 
2:30 and one at 5:30.
```
