# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 5297

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, following leader remarks, the Senate will 
turn to a period of morning business, with Senators allowed to speak 
for up to 10 minutes each. The majority will control the first 30 
minutes, the Republicans will control the next 30 minutes. Following 
leader remarks, the Senate will resume consideration of the motion to 
proceed to H.R. 5297, the small business jobs bill. The Senate will 
recess from 12:30 until 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly caucus 
luncheons. At 2:15, the Senate will proceed to a rollcall vote on the 
motion to invoke cloture on the motion to proceed to the Small Business 
Jobs Act.
```
