# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT ON JUNE 28, 2010

```
  MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT ON JUNE 28, 2010


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 6, 2009, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on June 28, 2010, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House announcing that the 
Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 2194. An act to amend the Iran Sanctions Act of 1996 
     to enhance United States diplomatic efforts with respect to 
     Iran by expanding economic sanctions against Iran.

                          ____________________
```
