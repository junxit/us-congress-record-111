# TRIBUTE TO SARAH MARGARET AKER

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO SARAH MARGARET AKER

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Sarah Margaret 
Aker, an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work 
she has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the 
past several weeks.
  Sarah is a graduate of Sturgis Brown High School, Sturgis, SD. 
Currently, she is attending the University of South Dakota, where she 
is majoring in chemistry and political science. She is a hard worker 
who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Sarah 
for all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
