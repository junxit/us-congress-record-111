# ORDER FOR PRINTING OF TRIBUTES

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                     ORDER FOR PRINTING OF TRIBUTES

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that tributes to 
Robert C. Byrd, late a Senator from West Virginia, be printed as a 
Senate document, and that Members have until 12 noon, Friday, August 6, 
2010, to submit said tributes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
