# RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, OCTOBER 29, 2010, AT 11:30 A.M.

```
          RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, OCTOBER 29, 2010, AT 11:30 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will stand in recess until 11:30 a.m. on Friday, October 29, 
2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12 and 31 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Friday, October 29, 2010, at 11:30 a.m.
```
