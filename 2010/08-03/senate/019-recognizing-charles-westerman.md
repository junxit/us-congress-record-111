# RECOGNIZING CHARLES WESTERMAN

```
                     RECOGNIZING CHARLES WESTERMAN

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Charles Westerman for his hard work as an 
intern in my Cheyenne office. I recognize his efforts and contributions 
to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Charles is a native of Wyoming and graduated from Wheatland High 
School. He currently attends Washington State University, where he is 
majoring in journalism and minoring in humanities. Throughout his 
internship, he has demonstrated a strong work ethic which has made him 
an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of his work is reflected 
in his great efforts over the last several months.
  I thank Charles for the dedication he has shown while working for me 
and my staff. It was a pleasure to have him as part of our team. I know 
he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. I wish 
him all my best on his next journey.

                          ____________________
```
