H. Res. 957, Honoring Jimmie Johnson, 2009 NASCAR Sprint Cup Champion.
  Roll 28, February 2, 2010: On Motion to Suspend the Rules and Agree: 
H. Res. 1014, Recognizing and supporting the goals and ideals of North 
American Inclusion Month.
