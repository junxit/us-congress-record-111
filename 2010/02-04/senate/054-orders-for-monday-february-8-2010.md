# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 8, 2010

```
                          ORDERS FOR MONDAY, 
                            FEBRUARY 8, 2010

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m., Monday, February 
8; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
proceed to executive session, as provided for under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
