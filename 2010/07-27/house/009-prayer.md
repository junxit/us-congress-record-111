# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Through Your Word all was created. In Your Word all can be healed and 
brought to the fullness of life. By Your Word we are taught the ways of 
justice and led to peace.
  Speak, Lord, Your Word to this assembly of the 111th Congress, that 
this Nation may be strengthened in virtue, grow in its capacity to 
embrace the diversity of peoples, surround them with security and right 
order, and so give You glory, now and forever. Amen.
```
