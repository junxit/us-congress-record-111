# ON THE BIRTH OF CAMERON ROSE DONAHUE

```
                  ON THE BIRTH OF CAMERON ROSE DONAHUE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 27, 2010

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Madam Speaker, I am happy to 
congratulate Brian Donahue and his wife Julie on the birth of their new 
daughter Cameron Rose Donahue. Cameron was born on Monday, July 26, 
2010, at 3:34 in the afternoon at Arlington, Virginia, with a full head 
of gorgeous red hair.
  Cameron Rose Donahue is 6 pounds of pride and joy to her 
grandparents, R. Scott and Claudia Horner of New Jersey and Marilyn and 
Francis Donahue of Florida and New Jersey.
  I am so excited for this new blessing to the Donahue family and wish 
them all the best.

                          ____________________
```
