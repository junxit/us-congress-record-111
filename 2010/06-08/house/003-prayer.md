# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  God of wisdom and love, You are the source of life and have gifted us 
with many blessings.
  Open our minds and hearts to receive graciously the art of patience 
and the discipline of prudence.
  May all our decisions set us on the path of truth and all our actions 
manifest Your goodness.
  To You be honor and glory both now and forever. Amen.
```
