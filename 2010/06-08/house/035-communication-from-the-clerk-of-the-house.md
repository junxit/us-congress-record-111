# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:
                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                     Washington, DC, June 8, 2010.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     The Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, I have the honor to transmit a sealed 
     envelope received from the White House on Tuesday, June 8, 
     2010 at 3:08 p.m., and said to contain a message from the 
     President whereby he submits a copy of a notice filed earlier 
     with the Federal Register continuing the emergency with 
     respect to Belarus first declared in Executive Order 13405 of 
     June 16, 2006.
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
     Lorraine C. Miller.

                          ____________________
```
