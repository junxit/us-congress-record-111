# COMMUNICATION FROM THE SERGEANT AT ARMS OF THE HOUSE

```
          COMMUNICATION FROM THE SERGEANT AT ARMS OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Sarah Gerber, Office of the Sergeant at Arms:

         Office of the Sergeant at Arms, House of Representatives,
                                     Washington, DC, June 3, 2010.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I have been served with a trial 
     subpoena issued by the Superior Court of the District of 
     Columbia for testimony in a criminal case.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the precedents and privileges of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Sarah Gerber,
     Chamber Support Services.

                          ____________________
```
