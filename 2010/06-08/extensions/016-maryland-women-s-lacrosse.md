# MARYLAND WOMEN'S LACROSSE

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

```
                       MARYLAND WOMEN'S LACROSSE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. STENY H. HOYER

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 8, 2010

  Mr. HOYER. Madam Speaker, I rise to congratulate the women's lacrosse 
team of my alma mater, the University of Maryland, on its record tenth 
national championship. In the March 31st championship game, Maryland 
defeated Northwestern University, a lacrosse dynasty in its own right, 
by a score of 13-11.
  Congratulations are especially due to Coach Cathy Reese, who was 
rewarded with success after returning to coach at her alma mater; to 
senior Caitlyn McFadden, who starred in her last collegiate game with 
two goals and an assist, and was named the tournament's Most 
Outstanding Player; and to the Maryland fans,

[[Page E1023]]

who turned out to support their Terrapins in record numbers. This 
championship is the product of outstanding athletes and coaches, untold 
hours of hard work, and the passionate support of the University of 
Maryland community. The University of Maryland demonstrates excellence 
in both academics and athletics, and I'm proud of this team for 
contributing to that legacy.

                          ____________________
```
