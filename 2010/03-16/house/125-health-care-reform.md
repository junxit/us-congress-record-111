```
                              {time}  1415
                           HEALTH CARE REFORM

entitled: An act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to modify 
the first-time homebuyers credit in the case of members of the Armed 
Forces.
  We're going to pass this on the backs of the Armed Forces. This 
should not
```
