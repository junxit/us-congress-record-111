# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 18, 2010

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 18, 2010

  Mr. BAUCUS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today it adjourn until 9:45 a.m. on 
Friday, June 18; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that following any leader remarks there be a period of morning 
business with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
