# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                          Office of the Clerk,

                                    Washington, DC, June 17, 2010.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     The Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, I have the honor to transmit a sealed 
     envelope received from the White House on Thursday, June 17, 
     2010 at 4:24 p.m., and said to contain a message from the 
     President whereby he submits to the Congress a copy of a 
     notice filed earlier with the Federal Register continuing the 
     emergency with respect to Russian Highly Enriched Uranium 
     first declared in Executive Order 13159 of June 21, 2000.
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
     Lorraine C. Miller.

                          ____________________
```
