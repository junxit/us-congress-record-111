# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 16, 2010

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 4213

```
                         ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, 
                             JUNE 16, 2010

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday, 
June 16; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate then proceed to a period for the transaction of 
morning business for 1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak therein 
for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally divided and controlled 
between the two leaders or their designees, with the Republicans 
controlling the first half and the majority controlling the final half; 
that following morning business, the Senate resume consideration of the 
House message on H.R. 4213, the tax extenders, as provided for under 
the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
