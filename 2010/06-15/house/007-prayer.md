# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  This world was created by You, Almighty God. In You and through You, 
humanity has been freed and brought to a liberty that is powerful 
enough to enable us to learn from mistakes and resolve with firm 
determination to live with compassion for others and bring greater and 
equal justice to all peoples.
  Help this Nation live up to its calling in these historic times. May 
all those who are committed to love others and pursue justice, work 
together, without illusion or deceit, and build a world of true and 
lasting peace.
  This we ask calling upon Your Holy Name, with lasting faith. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
