                        HON. J. GRESHAM BARRETT

  Mr. BARRETT of South Carolina. Madam Speaker, unfortunately, I missed 
the following recorded votes on the House floor the legislative week of

been present I would have voted ``aye'' on Rollcall vote No. 337 (on 
motion to suspend the rules and agree to H.R. 1061), ``aye'' on 
Rollcall vote No. 338 (on motion to suspend the rules and agree to H. 
Res. 518).

been present I would have voted ``no'' on Rollcall vote No. 339 (on 
ordering the previous question on H. Res. 1424), ``no'' on Rollcall 
vote No. 340 (on agreeing to H. Res. 1424, which provides for 
consideration of H.R. 5072), ``no'' on Rollcall vote No. 341 (on motion 
to suspend the rules and agree to H. Res. 989), ``aye'' on Rollcall 
vote No. 342 (on motion to suspend the rules and agree to H. Res. 
1178), ``aye'' on Rollcall vote No. 343 (on motion to instruct 
conferees on H.R. 4173), ``no'' on Rollcall vote No. 344 (on motion to

No. 345 (on motion to suspend the rules and agree to H.R. 5278),

Rollcall vote No. 353 (on passage of H.R. 5072), ``aye'' on Rollcall 
vote No. 354 (on motion to suspend the rules and agree to S. 3473).
