# E.V. WILKINS POST OFFICE

```
                        E.V. WILKINS POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 3892) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 101 West Highway 64 Bypass in Roper, North 
Carolina, as the ``E.V. Wilkins Post Office,'' was ordered to a third 
reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
