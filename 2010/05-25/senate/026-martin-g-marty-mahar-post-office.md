# MARTIN G. `MARTY' MAHAR POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 4425

```
                  MARTIN G. `MARTY' MAHAR POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 4425) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2-116th Street in North Troy, New York, as 
the ``Martin G. `Marty' Mahar Post Office,'' was ordered to a third 
reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
