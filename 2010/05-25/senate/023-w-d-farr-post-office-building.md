# W.D. FARR POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                     W.D. FARR POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4238) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 930 39th Avenue in Greeley, Colorado, as the 
``W.D. Farr Post Office Building,'' was ordered to a third reading, was 
read the third time, and passed.
```
