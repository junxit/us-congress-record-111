# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 4783

```
Committee Meetings
PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF THE SENATE AMENDMENTS TO H.R. 4783, THE 
CLAIMS RESOLUTION ACT OF 2010
Committee on Rules: Granted, by non-record vote, a rule providing for 
the consideration of the Senate amendments to H.R. 4783, the Claims 
Resolution Act of 2010. The rule makes in order a motion offered by the 
chair of the Committee on Natural Resources that the House concur in 
the Senate amendments to H.R. 4783. The rule provides one hour of 
debate on the motion, with 50 minutes equally divided and controlled by 
the chair and ranking minority member of the Committee on Natural 
Resources and 10 minutes equally divided and controlled by the chair 
and ranking minority member of the Committee on Ways and Means. The 
rule waives all points of order against consideration of the motion. 
Finally, the rule provides that the Senate amendments shall be 
considered as read.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
