# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Johnson, Henry C. "Hank" (J000288) [D-GA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1491, H.Res. 1516, H.R. 5604

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. JOHNSON of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to express my 
apologies for my absence on July 20, 2010, and for missing recorded 
votes held on this day. I regret that matters in my district required 
my personal attention and prevented me from being present to cast my 
vote. Although I was unable to vote, I wish to let my constituents and 
my colleagues know how I would have voted had I been present.
  On H. Res. 1491--Congratulating the University of South Carolina 
Gamecocks on winning the 2010 NCAA Division I College World Series--I 
would have voted ``aye.''
  On H.R. 5604--Surface Transportation Savings Act of 2010--I would 
have voted ``aye.''
  On H. Res. 1516--Recognizing the 65th anniversary of the end of World 
War II, honoring the service members who fought in World War II and 
their families, and honoring the service members who are currently 
serving in combat operations--I would have voted ``aye.''

                          ____________________
```
