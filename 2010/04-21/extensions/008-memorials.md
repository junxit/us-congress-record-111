# MEMORIALS

                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 4 of rule XXII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       260. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature of 
     the State of Wyoming, relative to House Joint Resolution No. 
     3 demanding Congress to cease and desist from enacting 
     mandates that are beyond the enumerated powers granted to the 
     Congress by the United States Constitution; to the Committee 
     on the Judiciary.
       261. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Wyoming, relative to House Joint Resolution No. 2 demanding 
     Congress cease and desist from enacting mandates that are 
     beyond the scope of the enumerated powers granted to Congress 
     by the Constitution of the United States; to the Committee on 
     the Judiciary.
