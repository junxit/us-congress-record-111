# OUR UNCONSCIONABLE NATIONAL DEBT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE COFFMAN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 21, 2010

  Mr. COFFMAN of Colorado. Madam Speaker, today our national debt is 
$12,871,255,665,556.84.

and all future Americans.
