# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 3, 2010

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 4213

```
                         ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, 
                             MARCH 3, 2010

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday, March 3; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 4213.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
