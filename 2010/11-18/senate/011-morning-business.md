# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Roberts, Pat (R000307) [R-KS]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period of morning business for 1 hour, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the time 
equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the Republicans controlling the first half, and the 
majority controlling the final half.
  The Senator from Kansas.
  Mr. ROBERTS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
proceed for 15 minutes.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
