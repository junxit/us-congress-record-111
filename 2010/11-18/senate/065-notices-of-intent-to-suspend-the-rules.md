XXII, for the purpose of proposing and considering amendment no. 4696 
to S. 501, including germaneness requirements.

intention to move to suspend rule XXII, including any germaneness

no. 4702 to S. 510 or any related substitute amendment to S. 510.

amendment no. 4713 to bill S. 510.

accordance with rule V of the Standing rules of the Senate, I hereby
