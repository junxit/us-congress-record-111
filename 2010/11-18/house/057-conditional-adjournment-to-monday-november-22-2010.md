# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER 22, 2010

**Speaking:** Engel, Eliot L. (E000179) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 332

```
          CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER 22, 2010

  Mr. ENGEL. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today on a motion offered pursuant to this order, it adjourn 
to meet at noon on Monday, November 22, 2010, unless it sooner has 
received a message from the Senate transmitting its concurrence in 
House Concurrent Resolution 332, in which case the House shall stand 
adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  There was no objection.
```
