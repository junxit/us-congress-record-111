  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 1(c) of rule XIX, 
proceedings will resume on the motion to concur in the Senate amendment 
to the bill (H.R. 1722) to improve teleworking in executive agencies by 
developing a telework program that allows employees to telework at 
least 20 percent of the hours worked in every 2 administrative 
workweeks, and for other purposes.

previous question is ordered.

Massachusetts (Mr. Lynch).

the ayes appeared to have it.
