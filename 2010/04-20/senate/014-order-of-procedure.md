# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the time for 
morning business be 1 hour, that the fact that the Republican leader 
and I took extra time should not count, Republicans having the first

ordered.
```
