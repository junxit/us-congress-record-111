# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

       The amendment to be offered by Representative Flake, or a 
     designee, to H.R. 2314 the Native Hawaiian Government 
     Reorganization Act, does not contain any congressional 
     earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as 
     defined in clause 9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of rule XXI.
       The amendment to be offered by Chairman Silvestre Reyes to 
     H.R. 2701, the Intelligence Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 
     2010, does not contain any congressional earmarks, limited 
     tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits, as defined in 
     clause 9 of rule XXI.
```
