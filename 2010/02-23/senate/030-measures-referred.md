# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 4238, H.R. 4425

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 4238. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 930 39th Avenue in Greeley, 
     Colorado, as the ``W.D. Farr Post Office Building''; to the 
     Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 4425. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 2-116th Street in North 
     Troy, New York, as the ``Martin G. `Marty' Mahar Post 
     Office''; to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.
```
