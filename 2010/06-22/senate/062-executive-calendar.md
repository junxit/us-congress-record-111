# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Casey, Robert P., Jr. (C001070) [D-PA]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed en bloc to Calendar Nos. 945, 946, 947, 949, 950, and 951; that 
the nominations be confirmed en bloc, the motions to reconsider be laid 
upon the table en bloc, any statements relating to the nominations be 
printed in the Record, as if read, the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action, and the Senate then resume legislative 
session.
```
