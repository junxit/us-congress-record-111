# CONGRATULATING THE NEEDVILLE LADY BLUE JAYS

**Speaking:** Olson, Pete (O000168) [R-TX]

```
              CONGRATULATING THE NEEDVILLE LADY BLUE JAYS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 22, 2010

  Mr. OLSON. Madam Speaker, I rise to congratulate the Needville Lady 
Blue Jays on an outstanding season. The Lady Blue Jays fought hard 
giving it everything they had, but lost a heartbreaking game in the 
bottom of the 11th inning in the Texas 3A semifinal matchup.
  Although they did not clinch the title, these athletes have shown 
they have the dedication, determination, and drive to reach their 
goals. I know these ladies are the pride of their school and community. 
The Needville Lady Blue Jays' demonstrate excellence in both academics 
and athletics, and I applaud them on a great season.

                          ____________________
```
