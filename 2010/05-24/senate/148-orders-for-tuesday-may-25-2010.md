# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 25, 2010

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4899

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 25, 2010

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on 
Tuesday, May 25; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 4899, the emergency 
supplemental appropriations bill; finally, I ask that the Senate recess 
from 12:30 until 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
