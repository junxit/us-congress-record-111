# RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 4, 2010, AT 9 A.M.

```
           RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 4, 2010, AT 9 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until the hour of 9 a.m., Thursday, November 4, 
2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9 and 55 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Thursday, November 4, 2010, at 9 a.m.
```
