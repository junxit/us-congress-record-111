# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, guide our lawmakers today with Your higher wisdom, 
helping them to see Your desires and plans for their day. May they seek 
Your guidance throughout this day and remember Your promise to give 
wisdom liberally to all who, by faith, request it from You. Lord, 
remind them that the wisdom You give leads to purity, civility, 
kindness, sincerity, honesty, and peace. May the gift of Your wisdom 
infuse us all with a faith that replaces doubt, until truth arises over 
falsehood, justice triumphs over greed, and love prevails over hate.
  We pray in Your wonderful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
