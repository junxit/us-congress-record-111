# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 6, 2010

**Speaking:** Dodd, Christopher J. (D000388) [D-CT]

**Measures:** S. 3217

```
                    ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 6, 2010

  Mr. DODD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., Thursday, May 
6; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate resume consideration of S. 3217, Wall Street reform, as provided 
for under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
