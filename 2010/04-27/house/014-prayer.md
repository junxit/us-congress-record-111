# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord God, by Your almighty power uphold our commitment to live 
according to Your revealed truths and the constitutional law of this 
great country. Let freedom flourish in the lives of Your people who 
seek justice and prove themselves trustworthy. Shape virtuous 
leadership in government of the people. Remove fear from their minds 
and depression from their spirits. May this body prove creative in 
facing our problems, be decisive in seeking the whole truth; as it 
leads Your people to greater security and peace both now and forever. 
Amen.

                          ____________________
```
