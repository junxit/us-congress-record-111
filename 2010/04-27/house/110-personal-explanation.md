# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 5146

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Ms. JACKSON LEE of Texas. Madam Speaker, I rise to make a correction 
on the vote on passage of H.R. 5146, providing that Members of Congress 
shall not receive a cost of living adjustment in pay during fiscal year 
2011.
  Madam Speaker, I would like to state for the Record that I support 
the denial of a cost of living adjustment for Members. The correct vote 
for rollcall No. 226 would have been ``aye'' instead of ``nay,'' 
providing that Members of Congress shall not receive a cost of living 
adjustment in pay during fiscal year 2011.
  Again, I want to state that Congresswoman Jackson Lee opposes the 
cost of living adjustment for Members of Congress as stated 
appropriately in H.R. 5146.

                          ____________________
```
