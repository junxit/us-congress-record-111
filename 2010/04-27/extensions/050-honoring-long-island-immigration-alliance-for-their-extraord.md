# HONORING LONG ISLAND IMMIGRATION ALLIANCE FOR THEIR EXTRAORDINARY WORK IN THE COMMUNITY

```
HONORING LONG ISLAND IMMIGRATION ALLIANCE FOR THEIR EXTRAORDINARY WORK 
                            IN THE COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEVE ISRAEL

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 27, 2010

  Mr. ISRAEL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge a nonprofit 
organization that serves my district, Long Island Immigration Alliance.
  Long Island Immigration Alliance has demonstrated an overwhelming 
amount of commitment to serving the Long Island community. My 
constituents rely on our nonprofits for the vital services they offer. 
The continuous selfless efforts of those involved with the Long Island 
Immigration Alliance are admirable.
  I am proud to honor Long Island Immigration Alliance for their 
extraordinary work in the community.

                          ____________________
```
