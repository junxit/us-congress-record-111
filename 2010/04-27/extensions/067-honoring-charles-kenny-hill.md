# HONORING CHARLES ``KENNY'' HILL

```
                    HONORING CHARLES ``KENNY'' HILL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY V. JOHNSON

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 27, 2010

  Mr. JOHNSON of Illinois. Madam Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute 
to Charles ``Kenny'' Hill, one of the hard working employees of the 
House Wellness Center. Kenny is retiring from duty as a Members' 
Wellness Center Attendant after 41 years of service to the Architect of 
the Capitol. He and his wife Carole look forward to enjoying his 
retirement with their children Lisa, Kenny, and Tony and their 
grandchildren Christopher, Charlie, Pamela, Tiffany, Elizabeth, Wayne, 
and Kyle. The Members of the 111th Congress would like to thank you 
Kenny for all your years of hard work and dedication. You will be 
missed.

                          ____________________
```
