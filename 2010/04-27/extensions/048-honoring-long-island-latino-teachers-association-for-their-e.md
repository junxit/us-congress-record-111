# HONORING LONG ISLAND LATINO TEACHERS ASSOCIATION FOR THEIR EXTRAORDINARY WORK IN THE COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Israel, Steve (I000057) [D-NY]

```
      HONORING LONG ISLAND LATINO TEACHERS ASSOCIATION FOR THEIR 
                  EXTRAORDINARY WORK IN THE COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEVE ISRAEL

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 27, 2010

  Mr. ISRAEL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge a nonprofit 
organization that serves my district, Long Island Latino Teachers 
Association.
  LILTA has demonstrated an overwhelming amount of commitment to 
serving the Long Island community. My constituents rely on our 
nonprofits for the vital services they offer. The continuous selfless 
efforts of those involved with LILTA are admirable.
  I am proud to honor Long Island Latino Teachers Association for their 
extraordinary work in the community.

                          ____________________
```
