# HONORING VICTIMS INFORMATION BUREAU OF SUFFOLK FOR THEIR EXTRAORDINARY WORK IN THE COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Israel, Steve (I000057) [D-NY]

```
HONORING VICTIMS INFORMATION BUREAU OF SUFFOLK FOR THEIR EXTRAORDINARY 
                         WORK IN THE COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEVE ISRAEL

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 27, 2010

  Mr. ISRAEL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge a nonprofit 
organization that serves my district, Victims Information Bureau of 
Suffolk.
  VIBS has demonstrated an overwhelming amount of commitment to serving 
the Long Island community. My constituents rely on our nonprofits for 
the vital services they offer. VIBS' constant selfless efforts are 
admirable.
  I am proud to honor Victims Information Bureau of Suffolk for their 
extraordinary work in the community.

                          ____________________
```
