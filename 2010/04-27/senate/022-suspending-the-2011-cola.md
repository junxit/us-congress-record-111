# SUSPENDING THE 2011 COLA

**Speaking:** Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

```
                        SUSPENDING THE 2011 COLA

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I support the recent actions by both the 
Senate and House of Representatives to suspend the 2011 cost of living 
adjustment for Members of Congress.
  Although there has been encouraging news on some sectors of our 
economy, too many Californians are unemployed or underemployed. It is 
fitting that we forgo a cost of living adjustment at this time.

                          ____________________
```
