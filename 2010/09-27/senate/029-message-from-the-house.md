# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1517

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 2:03 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the amendment of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 1517) to allow 
certain U.S. Customs and Border protection employees who serve under an 
overseas limited appointment for at least 2 years, and whose service is 
rated fully successful or higher throughout that time, to be converted 
to a permanent appointment in the competitive service.
```
