# RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 12, AT 9:30 A.M.

```
             RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 12, AT 9:30 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 9:30 a.m., Friday, November 12, 2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:30 and 33 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Friday, November 12, 2010, at 9:30 a.m.
```
