# MORNING BUSINESS

```

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there

each, with the majority controlling the first half and the Republicans 
controlling the second half.

  Mrs. MURRAY. Mr. President, I see the Senator from New Jersey is on 
the floor, and I am happy to follow him or precede him, whichever he 
chooses.
```
