# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1508

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1508

  Mr. NADLER of New York. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I 
may hereafter be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 1508, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Wexler of Florida, for the 
purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New York?
  There was no objection.
```
