# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Woolsey, Lynn C. (W000738) [D-CA]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LYNN C. WOOLSEY

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 27, 2010

  Ms. WOOLSEY. Madam Speaker, on May 6, 2010, I was unavoidably 
detained and was unable to record my vote for rollcall No. 302. Had I 
been present I would have voted: Rollcall No. 302: ``yes''--Honoring 
the workers who perished on the Deepwater Horizon offshore oil platform 
in the Gulf of Mexico off the coast of Louisiana, extending condolences 
to their families, and recognizing the valiant efforts of emergency 
response workers at the disaster site.
```
