# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Measures:** H.Res. 957, H.Res. 1014, H.R. 4495

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. STEVE KAGEN

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 3, 2010

  Mr. KAGEN. Madam Speaker, I was detained in Wisconsin due to weather 
and missed votes on Tuesday, February 2, 2010. Had I been present for 
votes on that day, I would have voted ``yes'' on Rollcall 26, on H.R. 
4495--To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service 
located at 100 North Taylor Lane in Patagonia, Arizona, as the ``Jim 
Kolbe Post Office''; ``yes'' on Rollcall 27, on H. Res. 957--Honoring 
Jimmie Johnson, 2009 NASCAR Sprint Cup Champion; and ``yes'' on 
Rollcall 28, on H. Res. 1014--Recognizing and supporting the goals and 
ideals of North American Inclusion Month.
```
