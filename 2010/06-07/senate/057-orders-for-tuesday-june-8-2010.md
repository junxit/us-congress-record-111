# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 8, 2010

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 8, 2010

  Mr. BROWN of Ohio. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on Tuesday, June 
8; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
recess from 12:30 to 2:15 to allow for the weekly caucus luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
