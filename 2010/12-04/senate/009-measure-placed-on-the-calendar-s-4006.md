# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 4006

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 4006

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 4006

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, S. 4006 is at the desk and due for a second 
reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the bill by 
title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 4006) to provide for the use of unobligated 
     discretionary stimulus dollars to address AIDS Drug 
     Assistance Program waiting lists and other cost containment 
     measures impacting State ADAP programs.

  Mr. REID. I object to any further proceeding with respect to this 
bill.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
