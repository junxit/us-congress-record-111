# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DORGAN. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to a 
period of morning business, with Senators permitted to speak therein 
for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DORGAN. Let me ask, if I might, I know Senator Murray and Senator 
Sessions are here. I do not know in what order they would want to go, 
and I believe about 10 minutes each or so.
  I ask unanimous consent that Senator Sessions be recognized, followed 
by Senator Murray, and I be recognized following the presentation of 
Senator Murray.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Alabama.

                          ____________________
```
