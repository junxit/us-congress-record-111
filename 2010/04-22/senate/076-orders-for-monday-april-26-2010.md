# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 26, 2010

**Speaking:** Casey, Robert P., Jr. (C001070) [D-PA]

**Measures:** S. 3217

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 26, 2010

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m., Monday, 
April 26; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and there be a period of morning business until 3 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each; that following 
morning business, the Senate resume the motion to proceed to S. 3217, 
Wall Street reform.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
