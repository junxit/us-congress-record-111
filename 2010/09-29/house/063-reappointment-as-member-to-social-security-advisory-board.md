# REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO SOCIAL SECURITY ADVISORY BOARD

```
       REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO SOCIAL SECURITY ADVISORY BOARD

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 703 of the Social 
Security Act (42 U.S.C. 903), and the order of the House of January 6, 
2009, and upon the recommendation of the minority leader, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's reappointment, effective October 9, 2010, of 
the following member on the part of the House to the Social Security 
Advisory Board for a term of 6 years:
  Mrs. Dorcas R. Hardy, Spotsylvania, Virginia.

                          ____________________
```
