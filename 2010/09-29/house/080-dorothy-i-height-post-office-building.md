# DOROTHY I. HEIGHT POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                 DOROTHY I. HEIGHT POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and passing the bill (H.R. 6118) to designate the 
facility

[[Page 17158]]

of the United States Postal Service located at 2 Massachusetts Avenue, 
N.E., in Washington, D.C., as the ``Dorothy I. Height Post Office 
Building,'' as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentlewoman from California (Ms. Chu) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  The title was amended so as to read: ``A bill to designate the 
facility of the United States Postal Service located at 2 Massachusetts 
Avenue, N.E., in Washington, D.C., as the `Dorothy I. Height Post 
Office'.''.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
