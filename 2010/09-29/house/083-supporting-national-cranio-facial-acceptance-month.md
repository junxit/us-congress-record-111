# SUPPORTING NATIONAL CRANIO- FACIAL ACCEPTANCE MONTH

```
          SUPPORTING NATIONAL CRANIO- FACIAL ACCEPTANCE MONTH

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and agreeing to the resolution (H. Res. 1603) 
expressing support for designation of September 2010 as National 
Craniofacial Acceptance Month.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentlewoman from California (Ms. Chu) that the House suspend the rules 
and agree to the resolution.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
