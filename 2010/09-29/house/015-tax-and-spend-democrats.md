# TAX AND SPEND DEMOCRATS

**Speaking:** Smith, Lamar (S000583) [R-TX]

```
                        TAX AND SPEND DEMOCRATS

  (Mr. SMITH of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SMITH of Texas. Mr. Speaker, Democrats in Congress won't tell the 
American people how much they're going to raise their taxes. They're 
going to wait till after the election when we come back into session.
  And Democrats in Congress won't tell the American people how they're

[[Page H7217]]

going to spend their money. For the first time in 35 years, no budget 
was offered.
  Meanwhile, the Democrats are spending almost $2 for every $1 the 
Federal Government collects. That puts a drag on the economy and kills 
jobs.
  The American people have had enough. It's time to end the one-party 
monopoly in Washington.

                          ____________________
```
