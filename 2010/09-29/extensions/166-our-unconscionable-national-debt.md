# OUR UNCONSCIONABLE NATIONAL DEBT

**Speaking:** Coffman, Mike (C001077) [R-CO]

```
                    OUR UNCONSCIONABLE NATIONAL DEBT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE COFFMAN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 29, 2010

  Mr. COFFMAN. Madam Speaker, today our national debt is 
$13,472,761,083,757.88.
  On January 6th, 2009, the start of the 111th Congress, the national 
debt was $10,638,425,746,293.80.
  This means the national debt has increased by $2,834,335,337,464.00 
so far this Congress.
  This debt and its interest payments we are passing to our children 
and all future Americans.

                          ____________________
```
