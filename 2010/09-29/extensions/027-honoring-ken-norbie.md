# HONORING KEN NORBIE

```
                          HONORING KEN NORBIE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MICHELE BACHMANN

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 29, 2010

  Mrs. BACHMANN. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Mr. Ken Norbie of 
St. Cloud, Minnesota for his contributions as ``Volunteer of the Year'' 
to St. Cloud Hospital.
  Since 1990, Ken has logged in over 4,000 hours of volunteer time with 
Imaging Services, Mended Heart and Facility Tours. He has made a 
lasting impression on everyone at the hospital through his caring and 
compassionate manner. The traits, along with his dedication, make him a 
wonderful choice for the ``Volunteer of the Year'' award from St. Cloud 
Hospital.
  Ken is truly a blessing to the patients, staff and faculty at St. 
Cloud Hospital, and I ask this body join me in recognizing the 
important contributions he has made to the hospital and the surrounding 
community.

                          ____________________
```
