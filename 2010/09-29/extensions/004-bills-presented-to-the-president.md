# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House, reports that on September 28, 
2010 she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bills.

       H.R. 6190. To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to 
     extend the funding and expenditure authority of the Airport 
     and Airway Trust Fund, to amend title 49, United States Code, 
     to extend the airport improvement program, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 1517. To allow certain U.S. Customs and Border 
     Protection employees who serve under an overseas limited 
     appointment for at least 2 years, and whose service is rated 
     fully successful or higher throughout that time, to be 
     converted to a permanent appointment in the competitive 
     service.

                          ____________________
```
