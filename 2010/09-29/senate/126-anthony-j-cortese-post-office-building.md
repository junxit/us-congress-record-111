# ANTHONY J. CORTESE POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                ANTHONY J. CORTESE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4543) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 4285 Payne Avenue in San Jose, California, as 
the ``Anthony J. Cortese Post Office Building'', was ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
