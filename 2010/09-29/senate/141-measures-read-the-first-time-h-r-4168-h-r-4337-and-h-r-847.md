# MEASURES READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 4168, H.R. 4337, AND H.R. 847

```
    MEASURES READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 4168, H.R. 4337, AND H.R. 847

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I understand there are three bills at the 
desk, and I ask for their first reading en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report the bills by title en bloc.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 4168) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to expand the definition of cellulosic biofuel to 
     include algae-based biofuel for purposes of the cellulosic 
     biofuel producer credit and the special allowance for 
     cellulosic biofuel plant property.
       A bill (H.R. 4337) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to modify certain rules applicable to regulated 
     investment companies, and for other purposes.
       A bill (H.R. 847) to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     extend and improve protections and services to individuals 
     directly impacted by the terrorist attack in New York City on 
     September 11, 2001, and so forth and for other purposes.

  Mr. DURBIN. I now ask for a second reading en bloc and I object to my 
own request en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bills will be read for the second time on the next legislative 
day.

                          ____________________
```
