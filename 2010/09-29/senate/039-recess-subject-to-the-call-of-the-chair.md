# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from the State of 
Alaska, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess subject 
to the call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 10:45 p.m., recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 11:39 p.m. when called to 
order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Begich).
```
