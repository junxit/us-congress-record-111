# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on September 29, 2010, she 
had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 2868. An act to provide increased access to the Federal 
     supply schedules of the General Services Administration to 
     the American Red Cross, other qualified organizations, and 
     State and local governments.
       S. 3839. An act to provide for an additional temporary 
     extension of programs under the Small Business Act and Small 
     Business Investment Act of 1958, and for other purposes.
       S. 3847. An act to implement certain defense trade 
     cooperation treaties, and for other purposes.
```
