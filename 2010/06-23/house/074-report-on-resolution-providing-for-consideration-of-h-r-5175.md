# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5175, DEMOCRACY IS STRENGTHENED BY CASTING LIGHT ON SPENDING IN ELECTIONS ACT

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5175, 
DEMOCRACY IS STRENGTHENED BY CASTING LIGHT ON SPENDING IN ELECTIONS ACT

  Mr. ARCURI, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 111-511) on the resolution (H. Res. 1468) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5175) to amend the Federal Election 
Campaign Act of 1971 to prohibit foreign influence in Federal 
elections, to prohibit government contractors from making expenditures 
with respect to such elections, and to establish additional disclosure 
requirements with respect to spending in such elections, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
