# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COMMISSION ON INTERNATIONAL RELIGIOUS FREEDOM

```
APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COMMISSION ON INTERNATIONAL RELIGIOUS FREEDOM

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 201(b) of the 
International Religious Freedom Act of 1998 (22 U.S.C. 6431), and the 
order of the House of January 6, 2009, the Chair announces the 
Speaker's appointment of the following members on the part of the House 
to the Commission on International Religious Freedom:
  Ms. Elizabeth W. Prodromou, Boston, Massachusetts, for a 2-year term 
ending May 14, 2012, to succeed herself And upon the recommendation of 
the Minority Leader:
  Mr. Ted Ven Der Meid, Rochester, New York, for a 2-year term ending 
May 14, 2012, to succeed Ms. Nina Shea

                          ____________________
```
