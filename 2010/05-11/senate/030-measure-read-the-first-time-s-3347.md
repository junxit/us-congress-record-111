# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3347

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3347

  Mr. DODD. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title for 
the first time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3347) to extend the National Flood Insurance 
     Program through December 31, 2010.

  Mr. DODD. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading, and in 
order to place the bill on the calendar under provisions of rule XIV, I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be read for the second time on the next legislative 
day.

                          ____________________
```
