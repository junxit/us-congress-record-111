# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 12, 2010

```
                   ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 12, 2010

  Mr. DODD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday, 
May 12; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 3217, Wall Street 
reform, as provided for under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
