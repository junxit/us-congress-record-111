# PETITIONS AND MEMORIALS

```
                        PETITIONS AND MEMORIALS

  The following petitions and memorials were laid before the Senate and 
were referred or ordered to lie on the table as indicated:

       POM-138. A resolution adopted by the St. Charles County 
     Council of the State of Missouri relative to the 
     Comprehensive Plan for Flood Control on the Mississippi and 
     Illinois Rivers; to the Committee on Environment and Public 
     Works.
       POM-139. A resolution adopted by the City of Wentzville, 
     Missouri relative to the Comprehensive Plan for Flood Control 
     on the Mississippi and Illinois Rivers; to the Committee on 
     Environment and Public Works.
       POM-140. A message from the Canadian Parliament extending 
     best wishes to the United States Congress and the people of 
     the United States of America as they celebrate Independence 
     Day on July 4, 2010; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
       POM-141. A message from the National Assembly of Kuwait to 
     the President of the Senate expressing congratulations on the 
     occasion of the National Day of the United States of America; 
     to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
```
