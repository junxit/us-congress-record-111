# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  At 4:11 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker has 
signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 3562. An act to designate the federally occupied 
     building located at 1220 Echelon Parkway in Jackson, 
     Mississippi, as the `James Chaney, Andrew Goodman, Michael 
     Schwerner, and Roy K. Moore Federal Building'.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Inouye).

                          ____________________
```
