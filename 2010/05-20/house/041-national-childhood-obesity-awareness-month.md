# NATIONAL CHILDHOOD OBESITY AWARENESS MONTH

```
               NATIONAL CHILDHOOD OBESITY AWARENESS MONTH

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Kissell). The unfinished business is the 
question on suspending the rules and agreeing to the resolution, H. 
Res. 996, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentlewoman from California (Mrs. Capps) that the House suspend the 
rules and agree to the resolution, H. Res. 996, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the resolution, as amended, was agreed to.
  The title was amended so as to read: ``A resolution expressing 
support for the designation of September as National Childhood Obesity 
Awareness Month.''.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
