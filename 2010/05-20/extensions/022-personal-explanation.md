                             HON. TOM COLE

                         Thursday, May 20, 2010

  Mr. COLE. Madam Speaker, during the week of May 10, 2010, I was not 
in Washington, DC because I was surveying extensive damage that 
occurred in my Congressional District due to several tornadoes on May 
10, 2010.
  My vote explanations for the week are as follows:

258: ``aye'' (On agreeing to H. Res. 1299).
