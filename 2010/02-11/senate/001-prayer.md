# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, You are present with us in light and shadows. Keep our 
Senators responsive to Your light as they seek to please You in all 
they say and do. Lord, remind them that You are available to support 
and sustain them in all situations. Give them the ability to grasp 
sufficient truth to fulfill Your purposes on Earth and to glorify Your 
name. Fill them with Your power, transforming them into instruments of 
Your love and grace.
  We pray in Your holy Name. Amen.
```
