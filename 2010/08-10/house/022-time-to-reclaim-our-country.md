# TIME TO RECLAIM OUR COUNTRY

**Speaking:** Smith, Lamar (S000583) [R-TX]

```
                      TIME TO RECLAIM OUR COUNTRY

  (Mr. SMITH of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SMITH of Texas. Madam Speaker, how bad does it have to get?
  Over 20 million Americans are out of work or have given up looking 
for work. Federal spending is out of control, and congressional 
Democrats won't even propose a budget.
  The administration intentionally takes actions to weaken immigration 
laws; Federal judges assault our time-tested values; and the 
administration wants to hike taxes on individuals, small businesses, 
and investments, which will kill jobs.
  How bad does it have to get before Americans reclaim our country?

                          ____________________
```
