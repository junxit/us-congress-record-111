# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for 1 
hour, with the time equally divided between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the Senator from Vermont, Mr. Sanders, controlling 15 
minutes of the majority time.
  The Senator from Vermont.
  Mr. LEAHY. Mr. President, I thank my good friend from Nevada, the 
majority leader. Before I speak, I see the distinguished Republican 
leader. I will reserve my time and allow him to speak, of course.

                          ____________________
```
