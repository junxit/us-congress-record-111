# PROVIDING FOR A JOINT SESSION OF CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Baucus, Max (B000243) [D-MT]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 228

```
               PROVIDING FOR A JOINT SESSION OF CONGRESS

  Mr. BAUCUS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 228 at the desk and just 
received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 228) providing for a 
     joint session of Congress to receive a message from the 
     President of the United States.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consideration of 
the concurrent resolution.
  Mr. BAUCUS. I ask unanimous consent the concurrent resolution be 
agreed to and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 228) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
