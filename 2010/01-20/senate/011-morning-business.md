# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period for the transaction of morning business for 1 hour, 
with the time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees and with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 
minutes each.
  The Senator from Tennessee is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
