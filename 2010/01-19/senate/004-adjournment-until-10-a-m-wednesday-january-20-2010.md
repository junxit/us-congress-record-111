# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M., WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 20, 2010

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M., WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 20, 2010

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 10 a.m., Wednesday, January 20, 2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 o'clock and 30 seconds a.m., adjourned 
until Wednesday, January 20, 2010, at 10 a.m.
```
