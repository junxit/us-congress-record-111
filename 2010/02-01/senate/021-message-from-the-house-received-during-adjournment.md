# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  Under the order of the Senate of January 6, 2009, the Secretary of 
the Senate, on January 29, 2010, during the adjournment of the Senate, 
received a message from the House of Representatives announcing that 
the Speaker pro tempore (Ms. Edwards of Maryland) has signed the 
following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 4508. An act to provide for an additional temporary 
     extension of programs under the Small Business Act and the 
     Small Business Investment Act of 1958, and for other 
     purposes.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 6, 2009, 
the enrolled bill was signed on January 29, 2010, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, by the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).

                          ____________________
```
