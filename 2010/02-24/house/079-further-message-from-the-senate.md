# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 3961

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed with amendments in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 3961. An act to amend title XVIII of the Social 
     Security Act to reform the Medicare SGR payment system for 
     physicians and to reinstitute and update the Pay-As-You-Go 
     requirement of budget neutrality on new tax and mandatory 
     spending legislation, enforced by the threat of annual, 
     automatic sequestration.

                          ____________________
```
