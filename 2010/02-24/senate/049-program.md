# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 1299

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, tomorrow several Senators will be 
participating in the bipartisan, bicameral health care summit with 
President Barack Obama. I am honored to be one of those Senators. As a 
result, though, there will be no rollcall votes prior to 4 p.m. 
tomorrow. We will continue to work on an agreement to consider the 30-
day tax extenders legislation, which I just referred to in an earlier 
statement.
  As a reminder, Senator Reid also filed cloture on the motion to 
concur with respect to H.R. 1299, which is the legislative vehicle for 
the Travel Promotion Act. We hope to reach an agreement to have that 
vote tomorrow.
```
