# HONORING ALFRED MULLER, AROSA ARSHAD, DIMITRI JONES, EBONEE PADILLA, AND ESTEFONIA YACTAYO FOR WINNING THE HONORING OUR FUTURE LEADERS COMPETITION

```
 HONORING ALFRED MULLER, AROSA ARSHAD, DIMITRI JONES, EBONEE PADILLA, 
   AND ESTEFONIA YACTAYO FOR WINNING THE HONORING OUR FUTURE LEADERS 
                              COMPETITION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEVE ISRAEL

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 24, 2010

  Mr. ISRAEL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge five students 
in my district, Alfred Muller, Arosa Arshad, Dimitri Jones, Ebonee 
Padilla, and Estefonia Yactayo, from Brentwood High School.
  These students will receive the Honoring Our Future Leaders Award on 
February 27, 2010. To win this award, they wrote their own rendition of 
the ``I Have a Dream'' speech delivered by Martin Luther King, Jr.
  I am proud to honor Alfred, Arosa, Dimitri, Ebonee and Estefonia for 
their academic and personal achievements and congratulate them upon the 
receipt of this prestigious award.

                          ____________________
```
