# HONORING CHRISTINA CAPUTO FOR WINNING THE HONORING OUR FUTURE LEADERS COMPETITION

```
 HONORING CHRISTINA CAPUTO FOR WINNING THE HONORING OUR FUTURE LEADERS 
                              COMPETITION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEVE ISRAEL

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 24, 2010

  Mr. ISRAEL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge a student in 
my district, Christina Caputo, from Half Hollow Hills West High School.
  Christina will receive the Honoring Our Future Leaders Award on 
February 27, 2010. To win this award, she wrote her own rendition of 
the ``I Have a Dream'' speech delivered by Martin Luther King, Jr.
  I am proud to honor Christina for her academic and personal 
achievements and congratulate her upon the receipt of this prestigious 
award.

                          ____________________
```
