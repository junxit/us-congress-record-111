# HONORING ASHLEY MORENO AND JAMILAH LINDO FOR WINNING THE HONORING OUR FUTURE LEADERS COMPETITION

**Speaking:** Israel, Steve (I000057) [D-NY]

```
 HONORING ASHLEY MORENO AND JAMILAH LINDO FOR WINNING THE HONORING OUR 
                       FUTURE LEADERS COMPETITION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEVE ISRAEL

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 24, 2010

  Mr. ISRAEL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge two students 
in my district, Ashley Moreno and Jamilah Lindo, from Amityville High 
School.
  These students will receive the Honoring Our Future Leaders Award on 
February 27, 2010. To win this award, they wrote their own rendition of 
the ``I Have a Dream'' speech delivered by Martin Luther King, Jr.
  I am proud to honor Ashley and Jamilah for their academic and 
personal achievements and congratulate them upon the receipt of this 
prestigious award.

                          ____________________
```
