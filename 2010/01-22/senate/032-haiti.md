# HAITI

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                 HAITI

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, we have all been working to help the people 
of Haiti and the American citizens who have been caught up in this 
tragedy. That is why we are committed to work with Senator Baucus, 
Senator Nelson, Senator Menendez, and Republican colleagues to make 
sure we pass legislation next week so the Department of Health and 
Human Services will continue to have the funding they need to help 
American citizens who are repatriated from Haiti.
```
