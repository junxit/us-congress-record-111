# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JANUARY 25, 2010

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 45

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JANUARY 25, 2010

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m. on Monday, 
January 25; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate proceed to a period of morning business until 3 
p.m. with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each; and 
that following morning business, the Senate resume consideration of 
H.J. Res. 45, a joint resolution increasing the statutory limit on the 
public debt.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
