# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 25, 2010

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 25, 2010

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business, it adjourn until 9:45 a.m. today, 
Thursday, March 25; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 4872, as provided for 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
