# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MARCH 19, 2010

```
                   ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MARCH 19, 2010

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I now ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Friday, March 19; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 1586.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
