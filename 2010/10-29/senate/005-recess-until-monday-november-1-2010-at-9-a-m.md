# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, NOVEMBER 1, 2010, AT 9 A.M.

```
            RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, NOVEMBER 1, 2010, AT 9 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will stand in recess until 9 a.m. on Monday, November 1, 2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11:30 and 30 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Monday, November 1, 2010, at 9 a.m.
```
