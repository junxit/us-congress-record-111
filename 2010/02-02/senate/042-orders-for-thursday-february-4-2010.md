# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 4, 2010

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 4, 2010

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 12 noon on 
Thursday, February 4; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate proceed to executive session to resume 
consideration of the Smith nomination, as provided for under the 
previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
