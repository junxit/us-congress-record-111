# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 24, 2010

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 24, 2010

  Mr. BROWN of Ohio. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9 a.m. on 
Wednesday, March 24; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 4872.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
