crafts through the improvement of applicable criminal proceedings, and 
for other purposes ``aye.''

Res. 1537 Waiving a requirement of clause 6(a) of rule XIII with 
respect to consideration of certain resolutions reported from the 
Committee on Rules and providing for consideration of motions to 
suspend the rules ``aye.''
  Had I been present for rollcall 459, On Motion to Suspend the Rules 
and Pass, as Amended to H.R. 5566 Prevention of Interstate Commerce in 
Animal Crush Videos Act of 2010 ``aye.''
