# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 26, 2010

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 26, 2010

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m. on Monday, 
July 26; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and following any leader remarks the Senate resume consideration 
of the motion to proceed to S. 3628, the DISCLOSE Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
