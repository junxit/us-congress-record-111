# RECOGNIZING MARCIA HILSABECK OF ROUND ROCK HIGH SCHOOL IN ROUND ROCK, TEXAS

```
 RECOGNIZING MARCIA HILSABECK OF ROUND ROCK HIGH SCHOOL IN ROUND ROCK, 
                                 TEXAS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 12, 2010

  Mr. CARTER. Madam Speaker, I would like to recognize Mrs. Marcia 
Hilsabeck upon her retirement from Round Rock High School after 43 
years of service.
  Marcia served all but the first two years of her career in the 
English Department and has led the English Department since 1994. 
Throughout the years of her teaching career, she has been honored as 
Teacher of the Year and Round Rock Local Legend. When Marcia first 
began teaching at Round Rock High School in 1967, there were 368 
students in grades nine through twelve, today there are ten times that 
many students. Times have changed since Marcia first began her teaching 
career, but her dedication and love for education has not. She has been 
called the ultimate teacher by colleagues and generations of students 
fondly remember and recognize the impact she made on their lives. Her 
commitment to education is extraordinary and it is with great pride I 
honor Marcia's career today as I wish her all the best for her 
retirement years.

                          ____________________
```
