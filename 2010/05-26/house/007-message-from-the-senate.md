# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 211, H.R. 5128

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 5128. An act to designate the United States Department 
     of the Interior Building in Washington, District of Columbia, 
     as the ``Stewart Lee Udall Department of the Interior 
     Building''.

  The message also announced that the Senate has agreed to without 
amendment a concurrent resolution of the following title:

       H. Con. Res. 211. Concurrent resolution recognizing the 
     75th anniversary of the establishment of the East Bay 
     Regional Park District in California, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
