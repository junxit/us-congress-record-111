# IRAN'S CONCESSIONS UPDATE

**Speaking:** Quigley, Mike (Q000023) [D-IL]

```
                       IRAN'S CONCESSIONS UPDATE

  (Mr. QUIGLEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. QUIGLEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to urge the U.N. Security 
Council to reject Iran's attempt to continue down the path of nuclear 
proliferation.
  Iran would have us believe that the nuclear deal it reached with 
Brazil and Turkey was of the same caliber as the offer Tehran rejected 
in October. The truth is, it's not even close. This agreement would 
allow Iran to pursue richer-grade uranium, keep more of its nuclear 
materials, and maintain access to the dangerous supplies it would send 
to Turkey.
  The Security Council must recognize the severity of this threat posed 
by a nuclear Iran. It must choose a deal based on substance over 
convenience. But most of all, it must remember the safety and security 
of the State of Israel and the Israeli people.
  In the face of Iran's latest diplomatic diversion, it is more 
important than ever that we levy real sanctions against a nation bent 
on destroying a friend. The future of Israel--our most important ally--
depends on it.

                          ____________________
```
