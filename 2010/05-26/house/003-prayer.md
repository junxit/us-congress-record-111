# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  ``Rejoice in the Lord always, again I say rejoice.''
  Here is another day of creation; another opportunity to serve God's 
people in this land of freedom.
  By the Spirit, may the Lord lift us in prayer renewing our faith. 
Knowing that lasting goodness is discovered in the Lord alone; and 
human freedom is a gift given to all the children of God; may Congress 
give the Lord glory by accomplishing great deeds in His Holy Name.
  In the process, may we encourage one another and live in harmony and 
peace both now and forever. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
