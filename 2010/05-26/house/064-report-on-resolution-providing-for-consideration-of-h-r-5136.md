# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5136, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2011.

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5136, NATIONAL 
            DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2011.

  Mr. PERLMUTTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 111-498) on the resolution (H. Res. 1404) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5136) to authorize appropriations 
for fiscal year 2011 for military activities of the Department of 
Defense, to prescribe military personnel strengths for such fiscal 
year, and for other purposes; waiving a requirement of clause 6(a) of 
rule XIII with respect to consideration of certain resolutions reported 
from the Committee on Rules; and for other purposes, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
