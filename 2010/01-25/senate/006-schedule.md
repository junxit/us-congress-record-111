# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, the Senate will 
proceed to a period for the transaction of morning business until 3 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  I ask unanimous consent that the first speaker during morning 
business be the Senator from California, Mrs. Feinstein, and that she 
be recognized for up to 15 minutes.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. REID. Following morning business, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the resolution increasing the statutory limit on the 
public debt. At 5:30 p.m., the Senate will turn to executive session to 
consider the nomination of Rosanna Peterson to be a U.S. district judge 
for the Eastern District of Washington. At 6 p.m., the Senate will vote 
on her confirmation.

                          ____________________
```
