# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:50 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the following concurrent resolution, in which it requests the 
concurrence of the Senate:

       H. Con. Res. 285. Concurrent resolution recognizing the 
     important role that fathers play in the lives of their 
     children and families and supporting the goals and ideals of 
     designating 2010 as the Year of the Father.
```
