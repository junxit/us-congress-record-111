# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, under a previous order, at 5 p.m. on Monday, 
the Senate will proceed to executive session to debate the nomination 
of Gary Feinerman to be United States District Judge for the Northern 
District of Illinois, with the time until 5:30 p.m. equally divided and 
controlled between Senators Leahy and Sessions or their designees.
  At 5:30 p.m., there will be two rollcall votes. The first vote will 
be on the motion to invoke cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 
5297; the second vote will be on confirmation of the Feinerman 
nomination.
```
