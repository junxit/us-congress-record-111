# ORDER FOR RECORD TO REMAIN OPEN

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Record

legislation, the insertion of statements, and any cosponsors,
```
