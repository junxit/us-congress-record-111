# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Eternal God, strong to save.
  Make us Your instrument to strengthen the union and assure the peace.
  Let us speak only the truth and work for justice.
  May the aspirations we hold out to Your people be rooted in the 
promises You have made and the Word You have spoken.
  Rule over us, Lord. Rule over all our actions both now and forever.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
