# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Kerry, John F. (K000148) [D-MA], Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. KERRY. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that Senator Webb 
be authorized to sign any duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions 
beginning December 27 through 11:59 a.m., Monday, January 3, 2011.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. KERRY. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LEVIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
