**Measures:** H.Res. 1782, H.R. 3082

REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENT TO 
     HOUSE AMENDMENT TO SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 3082, CONTINUING 
     APPROPRIATIONS AND SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSIONS ACT, 2011
