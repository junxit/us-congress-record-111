# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Measures:** H.Res. 1771, H.R. 6540

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mrs. McMORRIS RODGERS. Mr. Speaker, on rollcall No. 657 on H. Res. 
1771, On Agreeing to the Resolution, Waiving a requirement of clause 
6(a) of Rule XIII with respect to consideration of certain resolutions 
reported from the Committee on Rules, and providing for consideration 
of motions to suspend the rules, I am not recorded because I was absent 
because I gave birth to my baby daughter. Had I been present, I would 
have voted ``nay.''
  Mr. Speaker, on rollcall No. 658 on H.R. 6540, On Motion to Suspend 
the Rules and Pass, Defense Level Playing Field Act, I am not recorded 
because I was absent because I gave birth to my baby daughter. Had I 
been present, I would have voted ``yea.''

                          ____________________
```
