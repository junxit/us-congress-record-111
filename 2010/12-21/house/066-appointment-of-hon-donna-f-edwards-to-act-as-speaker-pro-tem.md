# APPOINTMENT OF HON. DONNA F. EDWARDS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH REMAINDER OF SECOND SESSION OF 111TH CONGRESS

```
 APPOINTMENT OF HON. DONNA F. EDWARDS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO 
 SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH REMAINDER OF SECOND 
                       SESSION OF 111TH CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:


[[Page 23372]]


                                                December 21, 2010.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Donna F. Edwards or, if she 
     is not available to perform this duty, the Honorable Gerald 
     E. Connolly to act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled 
     bills and joint resolutions through the remainder of the 
     second session of the One Hundred Eleventh Congress.

                                                 Nancy Pelosi,

                                                    Speaker of the
                                         House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
