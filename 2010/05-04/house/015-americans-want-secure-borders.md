# AMERICANS WANT SECURE BORDERS

```
                     AMERICANS WANT SECURE BORDERS

  (Mr. SMITH of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SMITH of Texas. Mr. Speaker, Arizona's immigration enforcement 
law mirrors what is already in Federal law. So why are some special 
interest groups in an uproar? It shouldn't be surprising. The very same 
people who want to throw out Arizona's new immigration law also want 
Congress to throw out America's immigration laws. Open borders 
advocates want amnesty for millions of illegal immigrants, so they find 
fault with any law that tries to reduce illegal immigration.
  Arizona has every right to protect its residents and secure the 
border. The message from Arizona is not to pass an amnesty bill in 
Washington, but to enforce immigration laws and strengthen border 
security.

                          ____________________
```
