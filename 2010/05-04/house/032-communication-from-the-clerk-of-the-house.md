# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

     May 3, 2010.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, The Capitol,
     House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, I have the honor to transmit a sealed 
     envelope received from the White House on Monday, May 3, 2010 
     at 3:23 p.m., and said to contain a message from the 
     President whereby he submits to the Congress a copy of a 
     notice continuing the national emergency with respect to the 
     Syrian Government.
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
                                               Lorraine C. Miller,
     Clerk of the House.

                          ____________________
```
