# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House, reports that on April 29, 
2010 she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bill:

       H.R. 5147. To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to 
     extend the funding and expenditure authority of the Airport 
     and Airway Trust Fund, to amend title 49, United States Code, 
     to extend authorizations for the airport improvement program, 
     and for other purposes.

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House, reports that on May 3, 2010 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bill:

       H.R. 5146. An act to provide that Members of Congress shall 
     not receive a cost of living adjustment in pay during fiscal 
     year 2011.

                          ____________________
```
