# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Bingaman, Jeff (B000468) [D-NM]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND NATURAL RESOURCES

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that the hearing scheduled 
before the Senate Committee on Energy and Natural Resources previously 
announced for May 6, 2010, at 9:30 a.m., has been rescheduled and will 
now be held on Tuesday, May 11, 2010, at 10 a.m., in room SR-325 of the 
Russell Senate Office Building.
  The purpose of the hearing is to review issues related to deepwater 
offshore exploration for petroleum and the accident in the Gulf of 
Mexico involving the offshore oil rig Deepwater Horizon.
  For further information, please contact Linda Lance at (202) 224-7556 
or Allyson Anderson at (202) 224-7143 or Abigail Campbell at (202) 224-
1219.

                          ____________________
```
