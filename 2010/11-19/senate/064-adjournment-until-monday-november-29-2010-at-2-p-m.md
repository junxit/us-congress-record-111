# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, NOVEMBER 29, 2010, AT 2 P.M.

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, NOVEMBER 29, 2010, AT 2 P.M.

Monday, November 29, at 2 p.m.
```
