# APPOINTMENT OF HON. DONNA F. EDWARDS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS ON TODAY

```
 APPOINTMENT OF HON. DONNA F. EDWARDS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO 
           SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS ON TODAY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                 January 29, 2010.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Donna F. Edwards to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions on this day.
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
