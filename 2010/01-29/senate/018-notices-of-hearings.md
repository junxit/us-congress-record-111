# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


               COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND NATURAL RESOURCES

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a hearing has been 
scheduled before the Senate Committee on Energy and Natural Resources. 
The hearing will be held on Tuesday, February 9, 2010, at 10 a.m., in 
room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building.
  The purpose of the hearing is to examine financial transmission 
rights and other electricity market mechanisms.
  Because of the limited time available for the hearing, witnesses may 
testify by invitation only. However, those wishing to submit written 
testimony for the hearing record may do so by sending it to the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, United States Senate, 
Washington, D.C. 20510-6150, or by e-mail to Gina_Weinstock@energy
.senate.gov.
  For further information, please contact Leon Lowery or Kevin Huyler 
or Gina Weinstock.


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that the hearing scheduled 
before Committee on the Energy and Natural Resources, previously 
announced for February 9th, has been rescheduled and will now be held 
on Thursday, February 11, 2010, at 9:30 a.m., in room SD-366 of the 
Dirksen Senate Office Building.
  The purpose of the hearing is to receive testimony on the U.S. 
Department of Energy's Loan Guarantee Program.
  Because of the limited time available for the hearing, witnesses may 
testify by invitation only. However, those wishing to submit written 
testimony for the hearing record may do so by sending it to the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, United States Senate, 
Washington, D.C. 20510-6150, or by e-mail to Abigail_Campbell@
energy.senate.gov.
  For further information, please contact Mike Carr or Abigail 
Campbell.

                          ____________________
```
