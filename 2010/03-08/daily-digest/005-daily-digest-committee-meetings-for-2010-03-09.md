```
                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR TUESDAY,

                             MARCH 9, 2010

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Interior, 
Environment, and Related Agencies, to hold hearings to examine 
proposed budget estimates for fiscal year 2011 for the Department of 
the Interior, 9:30 a.m., SD-124.

mechanisms, 10 a.m., SD-366.

laws, 10 a.m., SD-406.

215.

  Select Committee on Intelligence: to hold a closed meeting to

  Committee on Appropriations, Subcommittee on Interior,

  Select Defense Oversight Panel, executive, on National

  Committee on Armed Services, Subcommittee on Military Personnel,

military children, 5:30 p.m., 2118 Rayburn.
  Committee on Financial Services, hearing entitled ``Community 
Development Financial Institutions (CDFIs): Their Unique Role and 
Challenges Serving Lower-Income, Underserved and Minority 
Communities,'' 2 p.m., 2128 Rayburn.
  Committee on Rules, to consider H. Con. Res. 248, Directing the 
President, pursuant to section 5(c) of the War Powers Resolution, to 
remove the United States Armed Forces from Afghanistan, 5 p.m., H-
313 Capitol.
```
