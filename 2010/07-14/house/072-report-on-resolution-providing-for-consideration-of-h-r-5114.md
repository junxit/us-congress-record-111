# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5114, FLOOD INSURANCE REFORM PRIORITIES ACT OF 2010

**Measures:** H.Res. 1517, H.R. 5114

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5114, FLOOD 
                INSURANCE REFORM PRIORITIES ACT OF 2010

  Mr. ARCURI, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 111-537) on the resolution (H. Res. 1517) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5114) to extend the authorization 
for the national flood insurance program, to identify priorities 
essential to reform and ongoing stable functioning of the program, and 
for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
