# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3588

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3588

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at the 
desk. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
first time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3588) to limit the moratorium on certain 
     permitting and drilling activities issued by the Secretary of 
     the Interior, and for other purposes.

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask for a second reading, and under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
