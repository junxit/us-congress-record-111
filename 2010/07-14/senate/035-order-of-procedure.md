# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 4173

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that on Thursday, 
July 15, following any leader time, the Senate then resume 
consideration of the conference report to accompany H.R. 4173, with the 
time until 11 a.m. equally divided and controlled between Senators Dodd 
and Shelby or their designees; with the 20 minutes prior to 11 a.m. 
divided as follows: 5 minutes each in the following order: Senators 
Shelby, Dodd, McConnell, and Reid; that at 11 a.m. the Senate proceed 
to vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the conference report.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
