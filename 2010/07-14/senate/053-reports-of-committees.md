                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

       Report to accompany S. 1376, a bill to restore immunization 
     and sibling age exemptions for children adopted by United

     Adoption to allow their admission to the United States (Rept.

     judgments and certain foreign judgments against the providers 
     of interactive computer services.
       By Mr. BAUCUS, from the Committee on Finance, without 
     amendment:
       S.J. Res. 29. A joint resolution approving the renewal of 
     import restrictions contained in the Burmese Freedom and 
     Democracy Act of 2003.
