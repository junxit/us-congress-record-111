# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Hare, Phil (H001040) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4438, H.R. 4514, H.R. 4773

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. PHIL HARE

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 14, 2010

  Mr. HARE. Madam Speaker, on July 13, 2010, I was unavoidably detained 
in Illinois due to a family medical emergency. I would like the Record 
to reflect that had I been present, I would have voted as follows: on 
rollcall No. 434, On Motion To Suspend the Rules and Pass H.R. 4514, 
the Colonel Charles Young Home Study Act, I would have voted ``aye''; 
on rollcall No. 435, On Motion To Suspend the Rules and Pass H.R. 4438, 
the San Antonio Missions National Historical Park Leasing and Boundary 
Expansion Act of 2010, I would have voted ``aye''; and on rollcall No. 
436, On Motion To Suspend the Rules and Pass H.R. 4773, the Fort 
Pulaski National Monument Lease Authorization Act, I would have voted 
``aye.''

                          ____________________
```
