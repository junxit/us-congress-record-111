# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 26, 2010, AT 12 NOON

```
           RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 26, 2010, AT 12 NOON

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 12 noon, Tuesday, October 26, 2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 1:00 and 35 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, October 26, 2010, at 12 noon.
```
