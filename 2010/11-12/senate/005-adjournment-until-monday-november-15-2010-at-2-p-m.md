# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 2010, AT 2 P.M.

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 321

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 2010, AT 2 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senate stands adjourned, 
pursuant to the provisions of H. Con. Res. 321, until Monday, November 
15, 2010.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:30 and 39 seconds a.m., adjourned until 
Monday, November 15, 2010, at 2 p.m.
```
