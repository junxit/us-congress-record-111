# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** S. 1053, H.R. 1586, S. 1749, H.R. 2765, H.R. 4684, H.R. 5874

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D908)
  H.R. 4684, to require the Secretary of the Treasury to strike medals 
in commemoration of the 10th anniversary of the September 11, 2001, 
terrorist attacks on the United States and the establishment of the 
National September 11 Memorial & Museum at the World Trade Center. 
Signed on August 6, 2010. (Public Law 111-221)
  S. 1053, to amend the National Law Enforcement Museum Act to extend 
the termination date. Signed on August 6, 2010. (Public Law 111-222)
  H.R. 2765, to amend title 28, United States Code, to prohibit 
recognition and enforcement of foreign defamation judgments and certain 
foreign judgments against the providers of interactive computer 
services. Signed on August 10, 2010. (Public Law 111-223)
  H.R. 5874, making supplemental appropriations for the United States 
Patent and Trademark Office for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
2010, and for other purposes. Signed on August 10, 2010. (Public Law 
111-224)
  S. 1749, to amend title 18, United States Code, to prohibit the 
possession or use of cell phones and similar wireless devices by 
Federal prisoners. Signed on August 10, 2010. (Public Law 111-225)
  H.R. 1586, to modernize the air traffic control system, improve the 
safety, reliability, and availability of transportation by air in the 
United States, provide for modernization of the air traffic control 
system, reauthorize the Federal Aviation Administration, and for other 
purposes. Signed on August 10, 2010. (Public Law 111-226)
```
