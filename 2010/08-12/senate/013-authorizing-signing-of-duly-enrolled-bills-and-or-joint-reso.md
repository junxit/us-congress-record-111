# AUTHORIZING SIGNING OF DULY ENROLLED BILLS AND/OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
  AUTHORIZING SIGNING OF DULY ENROLLED BILLS AND/OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that after today, 
Thursday, August 12, Senator Landrieu be authorized to sign any duly 
enrolled bills and/or joint resolutions on any day until Friday, August 
20, 2010.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
