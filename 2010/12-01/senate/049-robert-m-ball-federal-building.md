# ROBERT M. BALL FEDERAL BUILDING

```
                    ROBERT M. BALL FEDERAL BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5773) to designate the Federal building located at 
6401 Security Boulevard in Baltimore, Maryland, commonly known as the 
Social Security Administration Operations Building, as the ``Robert M. 
Ball Federal Building,'' was ordered to a third reading, read the third 
time, and passed.
```
