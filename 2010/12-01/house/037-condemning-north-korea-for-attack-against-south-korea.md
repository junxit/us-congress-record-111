**Speaking:** DeGette, Diana (D000197) [D-CO]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1735

         CONDEMNING NORTH KOREA FOR ATTACK AGAINST SOUTH KOREA

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and agreeing to the resolution (H. Res. 1735) 
condemning North Korea in the strongest terms for its unprovoked 
military attack against South Korea on November 23, 2010.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from California (Mr. Berman) that the House suspend the rules 
and agree to the resolution.
  The question was taken.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. In the opinion of the Chair, two-thirds 
being in the affirmative, the ayes have it.
