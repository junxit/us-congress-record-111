# TRIBUTE TO BROOKE JEAN ANDERSON

```
                    TRIBUTE TO BROOKE JEAN ANDERSON

● Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize Brooke Jean 
Anderson, an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard 
work she has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over

the years to come.●
```
