# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 20, 2010

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 20, 2010

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on 
Tuesday, April 20; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate proceed to a period for the transaction of morning 
business for 1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 
10 minutes each, with the time equally divided and controlled between 
the two leaders or their designees, with the Republicans controlling 
the first half and the majority controlling the final half; that 
following morning business, the Senate proceed to executive session, as 
provided for under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
