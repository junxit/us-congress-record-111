# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 4173

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, following leader remarks, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the conference report to accompany H.R. 4173, 
which is the Wall Street reform legislation. At about 11 a.m. this 
morning, the Senate will proceed to a rollcall vote on the motion to 
invoke cloture on that conference report. If cloture is invoked, we 
would like to yield back some of the

postcloture debate time so we may complete action on the Wall Street 
reform legislation today. There could be additional rollcall votes this 
afternoon.

leaders. We still have some hope of being able to set up votes on the 
small business jobs bill. I hope we can do that; otherwise, we will 
have to proceed to a cloture vote on that sometime next week.
```
