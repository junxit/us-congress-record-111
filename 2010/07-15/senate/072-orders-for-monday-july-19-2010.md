# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 19, 2010

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 5297

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 19, 2010

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m. on Monday, 
July 19; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and, following any leader remarks, the Senate then proceed to a 
period for the transaction of morning business until 3 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; that 
following morning business, the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 
5297, the small business jobs bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
