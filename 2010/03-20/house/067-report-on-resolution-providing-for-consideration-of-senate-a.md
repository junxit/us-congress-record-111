 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENTS

  PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4872, HEALTH CARE AND EDUCATION

(Rept. No. 111-448) on the resolution (H. Res. 1203) providing for

the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to modify the first-time homebuyers 
credit in the case of members of the Armed Forces and certain other 
Federal employees, and for other purposes, and providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 4872) to provide for reconciliation 
pursuant to section 202 of the concurrent resolution on the budget for 
fiscal year 2010, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered 
to be printed.
